## Objective

Resolve the table/descriptor mechanism suggested by ROM pointer 0x13B28 → 0x16CD0 and the structured records beginning at 0x13B50. Determine whether either mechanism maps ADC/peripheral channels to RAM sensor records, specifically FFFF84B4 and field FFFF84BA.

## Supplied observations (discovery evidence; not yet independently verified)

- 0x13B28 contains BE32 0x00016CD0.
- 0x16DA4 within the pointed-to block contains BE32 FFFF84BB; surrounding entries include FFFF84B7, FFFF98F0, FFFF98F4, FFFF84F4, and FFFF28B6.
- 0x13B50 begins repeated four-word patterns such as FFFFF819, FFFFF818, 07000000, FFFFF80E, then similar records with channel-like values 08, 0A, 0B.
- These patterns may represent hardware-register descriptors, but their meaning and layout are unproven. The consumer must establish interpretation.
