## Findings

### Producer re-derivation

- In `sub_969C2`, `0x969EA` loads `R11=FFFF2150`. After the B956 call at `0x96A08`, `0x96A0C` copies return `R0` to `R7`; `0x96A0E` loads offset `0x68E`; `0x96A10` performs `mov.w R7,@(R0,R11)`. The effective address is `FFFF2150+0x68E = FFFF27DE`. This is a UWORD store of the interpolated result with no intervening arithmetic or sign conversion. FC90 remains separately stored at `FFFF27DC` by `0x969F8`.
- The ROM instruction bytes at `0x96A08-0x96A11` match the listing and the pinned ROM hash.

### Direct consumer and immediate stage

- `0x972B4` loads `R13=FFFF2150`. `0x972DC` loads `0x68E` into `R0`; `0x972DE` executes `mov.w @(R0,R13),R0`, a UWORD read from `FFFF27DE`.
- Immediately after the read, `0x972E0` stores `R0` unchanged with `mov.w R0,@(0x38,GBR)`. `GBR` is established as `FFFF9C10` at `0x969D8`; the destination is therefore UWORD `FFFF9C48`. The UWORD is copied without a comparison, arithmetic, extension, or status test at this consumer.
- The GBR value remains established through this access: `sub_96918` saves/restores GBR (`0x9691A/0x969BC`); the B956 helper path `sub_22D30 -> sub_2340C -> sub_23700` and its tail interpolation path `loc_232D0 -> sub_231C8` contain no GBR writes; `sub_22F54`'s helper path also contains no GBR writes; `sub_22448`/`sub_22434` contain no GBR writes. `sub_969C2` restores its saved GBR at `0x97332` and returns at `0x97344`.
- `0x96D96` also reads UWORD `GBR+0x38` (`FFFF9C48`), but it precedes the new `0x972E0` store and is a prior-state read, not a read of the value just copied from `FFFF27DE`. No later direct read of this established `GBR+0x38` slot was found before `sub_969C2` returns. Indirect aliases were not enumerated.
- The direct read is from the same EA written at `0x96A10`. No intervening direct overwrite was found in the exact-target/known-offset checks; intervening indirect/global writers were not enumerated.
