## Disposition and confidence

- Two of the four producer gaps are closed at the transport level (`FFFF85B2`, `FFFF8614.bit0`). Both are ordinary in-routine block copies inside `sub_20DCE`, not DMA.
- `FFFF9858` and `FFFF99AE` are excluded from the enumerated literal copy constructs. They remain producer gaps except for the runtime-conditional receive parser.
- No DMA, DTC, `movca.l` or post-increment-store transport exists, so the earlier "no direct writer" negatives for these cells cannot be explained by those mechanisms.
- Physical meaning, units, consumers of newly found sources, and target-specific runtime requests are **out of scope and not claimed**.
