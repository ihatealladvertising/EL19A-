Pattern class: **inline literal-pool byte read → zero-extend → signed/unsigned compare → conditional branch** (5 instructions, no stack frame, no subroutine call, no helper dispatch).

## Finding

**No SH-2 instruction alignment exists** between the CF48D ignition reader at ROM:0008ED10 and any EL19A candidate reader for 0x9B2E/0x9C2E/0x9DAE. The two patterns belong to different code classes:

- CF48D: inline literal-pool byte-read-compare-branch (5 instructions)
- EL19A: subroutine-based two-axis 16×16 interpolation with helper dispatch and tail-call (~20+ instructions)

The surface similarity (`extu.b` + `cmp/ge`/`bt` vs. `extu.b` inside helpers) is incidental: CF48D performs a single-byte threshold compare inline; EL19A performs a full 16×16 bilinear-style lookup through separate axis helpers and an interpolation tail target. No normalized instruction sequence aligns across more than one or two instructions, and the surrounding context (stack frame, helper `jsr` calls, tail `jmp`, axis count) differs fundamentally.
