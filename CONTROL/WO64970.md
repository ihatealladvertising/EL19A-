## Correction
WO64969 correctly records the 0x473C0 argument registers, but its conclusion that the axes are dynamic and that no ROM addresses can be assigned is incorrect.

In loc_472DC:
- 0x472EC: save incoming GBR to stack.
- 0x472EE: load literal E4AC.
- 0x472F0: ldc r0,gbr, establishing GBR=E4AC.
- 0x4762E: restore the saved GBR on exit.
- No intervening ldc ... ,gbr occurs between setup and restore in this function.

Therefore, at the 9B2E call 0x473C0:
- R5=GBR+0x10 = E4BC; first axis, sub_2344C comparison ×4.
- R6=GBR = E4AC; second axis, sub_233CC raw comparison.

## Exact ROM axis bytes
- E4AC–E4BB (16 UBYTE): 08 10 18 20 28 30 38 40 48 50 58 60 68 70 78 80.
- E4BC–E4CB (16 UBYTE): 08 0C 10 18 20 28 34 38 40 48 50 60 68 70 78 80.

Thus 9B2E uses the same fixed ROM axis pair and comparison transformations as the static 9C2E/9DAE calls reconciled in WO64968. The axes are not runtime-generated or unknown. Preserve XML orientation: X=E4AC raw; Y=E4BC ×4.
