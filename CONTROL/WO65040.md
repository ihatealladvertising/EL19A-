## Findings — 2026-11-17
- R4 = `EB30` at `0x4811C`; R2 points to `sub_22E10` at `0x4811E`.
- Query source: UWORD `[FFFF859C+0x16]` = `FFFF85B2`, loaded at `0x480FE` and zero-extended.
- Caller transforms it at `0x48102–0x48118`: subtract `0x60`; add one only when the signed difference is negative; arithmetic shift right one; floor at zero; cap at `0xFF`. Equivalent integer expression: `q = clamp_u8(ceil((FFFF85B2 − 96)/2))`. No caller-side clamp to helper's maximum valid query `0xEF`; values `0xF0..0xFF` reach the helper rejection path.
- Result: UBYTE from R0; zero-extended and biased by `−0x80` at `0x48124–0x4812A`. It is added to existing UWORD `FFFF1054+0x32E = FFFF1382` (`0x4812C–0x48132`), with lower bound zero and upper bound `0xFFFF`, then written back to `FFFF1382` (`0x4813A–0x48150`). This is an additive signed-offset update: curve result is interpreted relative to `0x80` by this caller.
- The successful update also sets byte `FFFF1F9A+0x41` to `0x64` (`0x48152–0x48154`). The routine then continues with separate state/range gates and updates the GBR-relative status byte `FFFF1D99+0x27`; EB30 result itself is not carried forward as a named scalar.
- **Confidence:** high for input, exact arithmetic, result staging/use, bounds and status update. No semantic meaning assigned to `FFFF85B2`, EB30 or `FFFF1382`.
