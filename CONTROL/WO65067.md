## Findings

### Query construction and enclosing gates

- The verified calling context is `sub_6BB9C` at `0x6BB9C`. It reads ROM byte `0x8CD3` at `0x6BBA2`; if bit 7 is set, `bf` at `0x6BBA6` skips the update to `0x6BBFE`. It next reads ROM byte `0x8B51` at `0x6BBAA`; if bit 5 is clear, `bt` at `0x6BBAE` skips. Thus this update path runs only when `0x8CD3.bit7` is clear and `0x8B51.bit5` is set. No symbolic meaning is assigned to these gate bytes.
- `sub_6BAD8` establishes `GBR=FFFF84D4` at `0x6BAEA–0x6BAEC`. Its first lookup query is the raw UWORD at `FFFF1000`, loaded at `0x6BB14` and zero-extended into R7 at `0x6BB1C` for the first call; it is reloaded at `0x6BB38` and zero-extended in the second call's delay slot at `0x6BB42`.
- The second lookup query is R13, stored as a UWORD stack argument at `0x6BB22` and again at `0x6BB3C`. The helper reads that argument at `0x22D46` and zero-extends it at `0x22D48`.
- Query R13 is selected locally from `FFFF3628+0x0E = FFFF3636`. `tst #2` at `0x6BAF4` and `bt/s` at `0x6BAF6` take the `bit1 clear` path; the delay slot loads UWORD `FFFF84FE` at `0x6BAF8`, and `0x6BB0A` copies it to R13. When bit 1 is set, the other path reads UWORDs at `FFFF84FE` and `FFFF850C` (`0x6BAFA–0x6BB08`) and selects their unsigned maximum into R13. Both query paths are locally defined; no incoming R13 provenance is assumed.
- The identity and units of all three query values, and the meanings of the branch-gate bits, remain unknown.

### Direct downstream use

- After `sub_6BAD8`, `sub_6BB9C` calls `sub_6BB64` at `0x6BBB4`, then reads UWORD `FFFF1D62` at `0x6BBBC`. `sub_6BB64` updates that word before this read (`0x6BB74–0x6BB96`).
- If the updated `FFFF1D62` query is zero, `0x6BBCA` selects `FFFF1570`; if it is `0xFFFF`, `0x6BBDA` selects `FFFF1572`. For interior values, `0x6BBE0–0x6BBF8` loads both staged words as signed words, multiplies one by `0x10000-q` and the other by `q`, adds the products and takes the high word.
- The result is stored back to `FFFF1D62` at `0x6BBFC`. Thus these two independently addressed tables are evaluated with the same two map queries, their results are shifted and staged in adjacent words, then used as endpoints in a 16-bit weighted blend. The bounded trace stops at this store; no global consumer search or physical endpoint trace was performed.
- The lookup outputs and downstream words are outside WO65062's active ranges `FFFF85C4–FFFF85FF` and `FFFF860B–FFFF860F`; the active-WO address stop condition was not reached.
- The later semantic boundary is explicit: the blended word is written at `FFFF1D62`, but this WO did not continue into its other consumers or hardware effects.
