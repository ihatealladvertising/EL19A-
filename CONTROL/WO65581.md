## Objective

Test one cheap, bounded discriminator for whether the opaque EM43A Osiris payload is protected by a short repeating XOR/period transformation. This is a file-format function-identity question only; no EL19A calibration semantics are promoted.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

- **Hypothesis:** the 1 MiB payload's high entropy is produced partly by a short repeating XOR or fixed-period byte transformation (period ≤64).
- **Evidence:** WO65569: payload entropy 7.999866/7.999812, 99.597073% differing bytes, direct payload-XOR entropy 7.992326, header `ROMData` length `0x00100000`, trailer key names only.
- **Prediction:** at the true period, byte columns will be measurably non-uniform (higher index of coincidence / lower column entropy) and a per-column most-frequent-byte key will yield visibly non-random bytes or recognizable structure when applied to both payloads.
- **Cheap discriminator:** compute period 1–64 index-of-coincidence and per-column byte-frequency statistics on each payload, then test the single most-frequent-byte key candidate per period against both payloads.
- **Result:** UNRUN — do not claim support or rejection before execution.
- **Information gain:** separates a simple repeating-key obfuscation from stronger encryption/compression without broad cryptanalysis.
