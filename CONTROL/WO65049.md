## Results — 2026-11-17

### Pointer-relative axis correction

- `sub_969C2` at `0x969DC–0x969EE` loads RAM base `FFFF84B4`, reads UBYTE +6 at `0x969E0`, zero-extends, shifts left 8 at `0x969E8`, and calls `sub_22F54` with:
  - data pointer R4 = `0xFC90`
  - axis pointer R5 = `0x111EA`
  - query R6 = `UBYTE[FFFF84BA] × 0x100`
- The LST data row begins at `0x111E8` with bytes `00 00 32 00 46 00 50 00 6E 00 73 00 78 00 7D 00 82 00`.
- The pointer is `0x111EA`, i.e. row start +2. Therefore the eight axis words begin with bytes `32 00`, not `00 32`.
- Correct pointer-relative BE UWORD axis:
  `3200, 4600, 5000, 6E00, 7300, 7800, 7D00, 8200`.
- These are Q8.8 representations of integer breakpoints 50, 70, 80, 110, 115, 120, 125 and 130. The query transformation UBYTE×256 exactly aligns with these values.
- **Historical correction:** WO64815's recorded `0032,0046,...,0082` sequence was two-byte misaligned. The actual axis starts at 111EA and is `3200,4600,...,8200`. Do not carry forward the former values.

### Helper contract and output

- `sub_22F54` passes the UWORD query to `sub_2340C`; the latter reads BE UWORD axis entries and uses unsigned comparisons while walking the 8-point axis (last entry at axis+0x0E).
- `sub_22F54` then stages adjacent UWORD curve values from `FC90` and calls `sub_231C8` for interpolation.
- FC90 data is eight UWORDs:
  `8460, 8460, 8640, 8780, 85A0, 8000, 8000, 8000`.
- The interpolated UWORD result is stored at `FFFF2150+0x68C = FFFF27DC` at `0x969F8`. The following B956 lookup has a separate query/data/axis path; it is not the same lookup.

### Identity discrimination

- The 50–130 Q8.8 axis and UBYTE×256 query establish a **temperature-like input with high structural confidence**.
- This alone does not distinguish coolant, oil, fuel, transmission, or another temperature input. No sensor-channel identifier, conversion producer, or coolant-specific label was found in this bounded task.
- Historical V56 evidence separately associates `FFFF84B8 = FFFF84B4+4` with Juke donor temperature-axis references (mTSTNGTM/mTTXNEL) and a user-reported coolant display observation. That evidence concerns **+4 / FFFF84B8**, not +6 / FFFF84BA; do not transfer its semantic label to this candidate.
