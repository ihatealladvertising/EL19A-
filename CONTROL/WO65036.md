## Differential arithmetic and result-consumer trace — 2026-11-17
- **Gate polarity correction from the actual TST/BT/BF instructions:** after both lookups, the differential calculation at 0x42BE4 is entered only when GBR+6.bit5 is CLEAR (0x42BD0–0x42BD6) and GBR+3.bit7 is SET (0x42BDA–0x42BE0). The earlier provisional gate polarity in the starting notes was reversed; this instruction-level correction supersedes it.
- Let A = first lookup result (0x9C2E), B = second lookup result (0x9DAE), both masked to 0..127 at 0x42BB6 and 0x42BCC. Let C be the signed UWORD loaded from FFFF1054+0xA4 = FFFF10F8 at 0x42BF2.
  - Difference D = A−B (A sign-extended from the masked 0..127 value, B zero-extended).
  - Fixed-point accumulator W = C + (D << 8), at 0x42BE4–0x42BF6.
  - W is stored as a word at FFFF100A at 0x42C04. The field is not further directly read in the audited listing.
  - If W is negative, the code adds 0xFF before the subsequent logical right shift by 8 (0x42C00–0x42C0C), then uses SWAP.W / EXTS.B / XTRCT to pack and stores one byte at FFFF1C79 (base FFFF1C78+1) at 0x42C14. This is consistent with signed Q8.8-to-byte conversion with negative truncation correction; do not assign physical units.
- Direct consumers of FFFF1C79 found by tracing FFFF1C78 base plus offset 1:
  - 0x46358 reads it as a signed byte, adds it to byte [FFFF1D12+0x1C], passes a negative sum through sub_22494, then writes a bounded byte to GBR+0xBE (effective address depends on the routine's GBR frame).
  - 0x46BF6 reads it as a signed byte, compares it against a negative value derived from byte [FFFF85C4+0x1B], selects the smaller value, and stages the result at FFFF1994 (0x46C04–0x46C06).
- No other direct FFFF1C78+1 consumer was found in the bounded base-pointer-plus-offset audit. No direct consumer of FFFF100A was found; it is currently a staged intermediate.
- **Result:** the paired lookup difference is added to a signed 16-bit baseline at FFFF10F8 in Q8.8 form; the raw word is staged at FFFF100A and a signed-byte conversion at FFFF1C79 is consumed by at least two downstream control calculations. Baseline producer/units, final physical meaning, and the GBR+0xBE consumer role remain unresolved.
