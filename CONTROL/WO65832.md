## Question

Do the big-endian U16 sequences at ROM offsets `0x0F8F2` and `0x0F942` have genuine target-code references as lookup axes?

## Falsifiable hypotheses

1. At least one of the two longword occurrences is a genuine SH7058 PC-relative literal loaded by executable code and passed, directly or after bounded register movement, as an axis argument to a lookup helper. **Result: SUPPORTED** — both are genuine.
2. The occurrences may instead be mixed code/data bytes, unused constants, non-axis pointers, or incidental values. **Result: REJECTED** — both are loaded by decoded `mov.l` instructions and passed as axis arguments.
3. Any true axis reference may belong to an already-catalogued data object. **Result: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** — 0xF8F2 and 0xF942 are unregistered sequences; their consuming data objects (0xBB56 and 0xF952) may or may not be catalogued.

**Prediction:** a valid axis relationship requires a decoded instruction that loads the exact ROM address, a trace of the receiving register to the lookup call, and helper argument evidence identifying the address as the query axis. **Result: CONFIRMED** — both candidates satisfy all three criteria.
