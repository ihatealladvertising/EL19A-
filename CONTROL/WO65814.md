## Key findings

1. **No torque/throttle/limiter family reached a named endpoint.** All six candidates terminate in internal RAM staging cells, correction loops, or diagnostic monitoring state. None feeds a named torque command, throttle actuator output, or limiter comparison with a proven physical unit.

2. **0x0A0F2 and 0x0A8A2 are correction maps, not torque tables.** 0x0A0F2 is a signed speed/load correction (`[-12..+44]`) stored to `SBYTE[FFFF1054+0x34]`. 0x0A8A2 is a temperature/load correction feeding an LPF state (`ULONG[FFFF1054+0x2EC]`). Neither reaches a torque-command endpoint.

3. **0x0A342/0x0A382 and 0xA552 form a closed internal loop.** The A342/A382 pair is fuel-related (fuel-cut-selected via `FFFF85FB.bit7`), and 0xA552 feeds the selector byte `FFFF18DC` that chooses between A342 and A382. No fuel-pulse, fresh-air-percent, or accel/decel semantics established (WO64841, WO64903).

4. **F3D6/F3DE are strong structural candidates for mTTVOULM/S** (throttle valve opening upper limit curves) per donor A2L, but their physical use remains open — no actuator endpoint proven. Do not confuse with V6 `mTVOADV` scalar.

5. **E678/E688 are QDSPT basic-value curves** with timer-derived query mechanics. Their RPM presentation is a narrow display exception, not evidence for other RPM-axis maps.

6. **Vehicle-speed limiter is NOT LOCATED.** No candidate with target-side evidence found; WO65090 sweep complete, no new concrete lead to reopen.

## Three ranked next tests

1. **Trace `FFFF1054+0x34` (0x0A0F2 destination) to its first consumer** — determine whether the signed correction feeds a torque-request summation or a throttle-target calculation. This is the highest-value discriminator for the main torque correction family.

2. **Resolve 0xA552 basic-fuel vs VVT conflict** — determine whether `UBYTE[FFFF857A]` query and the `0xE291`/`0xE339` axes represent a fuel quantity or a VVT correction. Requires bounded trace of `FFFF18DC` consumers beyond the A342/A382 selector loop.

3. **Trace `ULONG[FFFF1054+0x2EC]` (0x0A8A2 LPF output) to its first downstream reader** (`0x4AFEA`, `0x63C10`, `0x6431C`) — determine whether it feeds a torque-intervention comparison or a throttle-actuator command.
