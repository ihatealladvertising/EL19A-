### 1. `0x04E71` — embedded byte-map entry, not an independent calibration curve

- Exact bytes: `05 06 07 08 09 0A 0B 0C` (8 bytes); SHA-256 `a6f12f405cc3dab754664147cb8562b1ca2c6e3ede35c3f0ca81d7a583287725`.
- It is at offset `+0x45` within the listing's `0x04E2C` data block, not at a separately defined object boundary. The candidate's eight bytes are part of the sixteen-byte identity sequence beginning at `0x04E6C` (values 0 through 15), which precedes a longer collection of 16-entry byte permutations. The combined listing data region ends at `0x04FC3`; loader/database identification strings follow at `0x04FAC` onward, including `LOADER80`, `DATABASE` and `SH705822N`.
- A real indexed byte-read path to the parent data exists. In `sub_3458`, instruction `0x03496` loads parent address `0x04E2C` from `word_354E`; `0x03498` zero-extends the byte read from `@r9`; `0x0349A` executes `mov.b @(r0,r5),r2`. Thus the effective address is `0x04E2C + UBYTE[@r9]`. The data subspan `0x04E71–0x04E78` is selected when that byte is `0x45–0x4C`.
- Caller setup confirms `r9` points to a RAM descriptor selected through the pointer table at `0x04D70`: call `0x037E8` has delay-slot `mov #1,r4`, selecting pointer `0x04D74 → FFFF8234`; call `0x0381A` has delay-slot `mov #IVECT1,r4`, with `IVECT1=0`, selecting `0x04D70 → FFFF801C`. The caller-visible descriptor byte controls whether the `0x04E2C` indexed byte lookup is taken (the routine tests its top two bits before the lookup).
- **Classification:** a subrange of an early byte-translation/control table, reached by a byte-indexed lookup. It is not an eight-point interpolated calibration object: no calibration axis, interpolation helper, physical query or calibration consumer is established. The static trace establishes the possible index range; it does not claim every address in this eight-byte subspan is exercised in every runtime state.

### 2. `0x106EC` — table-shaped UWORD data, no reader established

- Exact bytes: 64 bytes; SHA-256 `1d234d4a0a359ae8e55e49b730c264f22f62f94a326ae461c9fe0bec90672ccc`.
- Interpreted only as a structural check, the payload is 32 big-endian UWORD values:
  `0, 503, 1007, 1510, 2014, 2517, 3020, 3524, 4027, 4531, 5035, 5556, 6123, 6759, 7490, 8352, 9404, 9862, 10321, 10779, 11369, 11991, 12666, 13508, 14457, 15632, 17003, 18759, 21185, 24945, 30460, 45187`.
  The values are strictly increasing. This supports a table-shaped 32×1 UWORD BE hypothesis only; it does not establish an axis role or reader.
- The LST places this subspan at `+0x20` within the contiguous `dword_106CC` data block, whose next defined label is `dword_1078C`. The preceding 32 bytes (`0x106CC–0x106EB`) are all `0xFF`.
- Two adjacent subranges of the same data block have explicit pointer references, but neither points to the candidate: `off_66EEC` at `0x066EEC` resolves to `0x1072C` (`dword_106CC+0x60`), passed as R5 to `sub_22F80` at call `0x066E60`; `off_5857C` at `0x05857C` resolves to `0x1074C` (`dword_106CC+0x80`), passed as R4 to `sub_22F54` at calls `0x058470` and `0x0584B8`. These are distinct adjacent vectors/readers; their reader mechanics do not reach `0x106EC`.
- **Classification:** raw, monotonic UWORD vector candidate embedded within a larger declared data block; target-side lookup use is **not established**. The identical donor payload at EE310 `0x0A892` and its helper category do not assign the EL19A segment an axis, curve, calibration name or physical meaning.
