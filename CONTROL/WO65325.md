6. **`0x0E904` must use catalogue axis `0x0FA62`.** Not applicable in v1: `0x0E904` is not included (its axis width is not in WO65323's verified list). Recorded as a gap.
7. **`0x0D8D6` axis-confirmation blocked.** Applied by exclusion.
