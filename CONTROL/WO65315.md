## Objective

Extract donor A2L fuel injection, mixture and airflow MAP and CURVE characteristics to complement the WO65306 extraction, preserving the distinction between scalar limits and dimensional tables.

## Scope

1. Read only 64811000p2_m.a2l if present in the authorized workspace; if absent, report unavailable and stop.
2.
