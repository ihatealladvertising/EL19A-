# WO65007 — FFFF9860+0x18 Shared-Field Reader Census

**Status:** COMPLETE — second bounded consumer found; producer not resolved.
**Scope:** Check whether the query field used by WO65006 has another independent reader or a directly identifiable ROM-derived producer. Stop at unresolved pointer/dataflow.

## Evidence
- WO65006 callsite 0x6F6F6 reads UWORD [FFFF9860+0x18] and zero-extends it as the common query for curves 0x10CC2/0x10CE2.
- A separate routine, sub_986F0, reads the same UWORD at 0x986F4 (mov.w @(0x18,r5),r0, with R5=FFFF9860) and zero-extends it into R4 at 0x986F6.
- That routine reads another UWORD from [FFFF9860+0xB4] at 0x986FFC, zero-extends it, subtracts it from the +0x18 value at 0x98700, then calls sub_22494 at 0x98704.
- The result is staged at FFFF2150+0x2C2 (0x98712).
- The routine is invoked from sub_98714 at 0x98726 after sub_986B4; the preceding conditional call to sub_986A0 is controlled by FFFF3628+5 bit 1 (0x98718–0x98720).

## Findings
- FFFF9860+0x18 is not unique to the WO65006 pair; it is also consumed by sub_986F0 in a separate arithmetic path.
- The second reader applies a difference against FFFF9860+0xB4 before sub_22494. This establishes shared-field reuse, not a physical unit or semantic identity.
- The available bounded evidence does not identify a writer for FFFF9860+0x18, nor a ROM calibration directly feeding it. Do not infer that 0x10CC2/0x10CE2 are the source of the field.

## Unresolved
- Producer/writer and initialization/copy path for FFFF9860+0x18.
- Meaning and units of +0x18 and +0xB4.
- sub_22494 conversion contract and physical meaning of FFFF2150+0x2C2.
