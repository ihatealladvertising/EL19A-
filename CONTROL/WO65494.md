## Objective

Resolve the genuine 0-row GBR export cell exposed by WO65485. With `GBR=FFFF871C` loaded at `0x3AB9A`–`0x3AB9E`, the `0x3B0xx` routine writes the clamped `U16[FFFF2D8E]+r1` value to `word_87DC` and later reads it back as a comparison input; a separate `0x4FExx` routine loads the same address as a pointer from `.data.l word_87DC` and reads a byte:

- `0x3B0F8` writes `r0=U16[FFFF2D8E]+r1` to `U16[FFFF87DC]`; `0x3B108` writes `#FFFF` as a clamp.
- `0x3B136` reads `U16[FFFF87DC]` and compares it against a `var_78` table word at `0x3B140` (`cmp/gt r14,r13`, `movt r7`).
- `0x4FEC2` loads `r2=0x000087DC` from `off_4FF34`, and the `bra loc_4FEDE` delay slot at `0x4FEC6` reads `U8[0x87DC]` into `r2`.

Test whether this cell reaches a consequential compare, returned flag, intervention, previously dispositioned state cell or one of the six discriminator cells.
