## Objective
Inspect the existing NissanDefinitions archive(s), specifically `A2L.xml` and `ScalingData.xml`, to extract a compact, cited taxonomy and exact scaling-formula reference that can be used to test (not assume) EL19A candidate names, dimensions, units and conversions.

## Falsifiable hypothesis
- **Hypothesis:** the archive contains reusable standardized Nissan category/table/axis vocabulary and scaling formulas that match a subset of EL19A storage/scaling patterns.
- **Evidence:** archive paths already recorded in WO65211; XML nodes and formula definitions inside the archive; independently verified EL19A reader mechanics and calibration objects.
- **Prediction:** selected formula definitions can be mapped exactly to EL19A raw storage/reader arithmetic, while unrelated formula/name matches can be rejected.
- **Cheap discriminator:** inventory archive versions/hashes, locate A2L.xml and ScalingData.xml, then test no more than five exact formulas against already reader-verified EL19A objects.
- **Result:** unresolved until extracted and compared.
- **Information gain:** distinguishes genuine reusable scaling conventions from superficial name/shape matches and identifies possible current XML scaling errors.
- **Identity layer:** function identity; input/unit identity only where the EL19A target reader independently agrees.
