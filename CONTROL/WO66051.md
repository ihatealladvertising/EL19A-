## Question

`sub_8AD7C` (gbr = `FFFF34C1`, installed from `off_8B014` at `0x08AD8E`) copies `FFFF34C1`-based status bytes into consecutive stack slots around `0x08AF60`-`0x08AF88` — `@(h'AB,gbr)` → slot `0x58`, `@(h'B9,gbr)` → `0x5C`, `@(h'BF,gbr)` → `0x60`, `@(h'CF,gbr)` → `0x64`, `@(h'D4,gbr)` → `0x68`, and onward. Does that stack frame leave the routine as a published diagnostic frame (serial queue, message buffer, MMIO), or is it a routine-private scratch copy?

This is the hop WO65922 deliberately stopped at: the `FFFF284E` mask memo's only outward product is `FFFF357A.bit2`, and `FFFF357A` is snapshotted into this frame.

## Why it has value

Every status-bit trace in this lane so far — `FFFF3579.bit4` (WO65910), `FFFF34F5.bit5` (WO65877), `FFFF284E` (WO65922) — has terminated in "internal state, empty consumer set". This frame is the first located place where a *block* of those status bytes is gathered together, so it is the cheapest discriminator for whether the whole `FFFF34xx`/`FFFF35xx` status network has any reader outside the firmware's own logic.

## Exact scope

1. Enumerate the full copy run in `sub_8AD7C`: every source byte, its stack slot, and the slot-to-slot layout (`0x08AF20`-`0x08AFC0`).
2. Determine what happens to the frame: is it passed to a callee (register or stack argument), copied to RAM, published via `sub_33E18`/`sub_32790` or another queue append, or discarded at `rts`?
3. Follow at most **two** consumer hops from the frame.
4. Classify: `NAMED OUTPUT` (diagnostic/serial frame, lamp, MMIO), `INTERNAL STATE` (terminal named), or `UNRESOLVED`.

## Stop boundary

One copy run, its destination and at most two consumer hops. No re-trace of `sub_8AD7C`'s six conjuncts or the `FFFFA074` sweep (WO65916), no new bit-level analysis of the snapshotted bytes (already closed by WO65877/WO65910/WO65922), no `FFFF2150` limit-band work, no vehicle testing, no XML or definition generation.
