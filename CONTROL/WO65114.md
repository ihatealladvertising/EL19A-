### Reconciliation with prior records

- **WO64835 wording corrected.** WO64835 states that `sub_387B4` "reads the same mailbox word and can place its high byte into `FFFF8578`". The inspected window shows no mailbox payload read: `0x387C8` loads the constant `0xDA80`, and `0x387CE` stores that constant as a word. `FFFF8578` therefore receives an address-constant/low-word value, not a mailbox data byte. The WO64835 mailbox analysis for `sub_288B4` is unaffected.
- **WO64836 limitation preserved.** MB12 is initialized inactive (`MBC=111`) and its control/identifier word is initialized to zero. The `FFFF8578` store is not live CAN payload and does not establish a physical signal.
- **No conflation with RAM `FFFF83B8`.** The ROM byte at `0x83B8` is distinct from the RAM longword symbol `dword_83B8` at `FFFF83B8`, which is read at `0x2BA2` (GBR `0x8278`) and written at `0x2C0E` and `0x146A` (GBR `0x83AC`). The latter is a separate accumulator and is outside this WO's registered objects.
