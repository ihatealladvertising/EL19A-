## Objective

WO65502 proved `0x108B4` is a 16-point interpolated characteristic indexed by `U8[FFFF85A9]` in **4.4 fixed point**, carrying one step at index byte `0x60` (6400 below, 7750 at and above). Its open item is the producer of that byte. Find it, and state its numeric domain and whether it is a state flag, a computed ratio or a temperature/voltage-like input.

## Mandatory falsifiable hypothesis

- **Identity layer advanced:** input identity (what drives the `0x108B4` step).
- **Hypothesis:** `U8[FFFF85A9]` is a state/mode variant selector rather than a continuously varying measurement, because a table storing only two levels would otherwise be a very expensive way to encode a step.
- **Evidence:** WO65502 §1 (`0x5B5C0 mov.b @(h'D,r6), r0` with `r6 = FFFF859C`), §4 (single step at `0x60`).
- **Prediction:** a writer will set the byte from a small set of constants or from a bit test (mode), not from an arithmetic scaling chain; a temperature/voltage-like reading would instead show a scaled conversion with a wide numeric range.
- **Cheap discriminator:** find every write to `FFFF859C+0x0D` (displacement `0xD` against that base, the literal form, and any GBR-relative form), decode at most three, and inspect the assigned values.
- **Information gain:** either turns the `0x108B4` step into a mode-selected pair (nameable as a two-entry table) or establishes it as a continuous threshold on a physical input, which changes its RR description.

## Scope

1. Enumerate writes to the byte `FFFF859C+0x0D` = `FFFF85A9` in all addressing forms, including the GBR-relative notation used in this ROM (`@((byte_FFFF85A9 - h'FFFF85xx),gbr)`), and report the count even if zero.
2. Decode at most three writers: PC, routine, gate condition, source value(s) and the resulting numeric range.
3. If the writers only ever assign a small constant set, list those constants and the branch conditions.
4. State the byte's domain (constant set / bounded range / unbounded arithmetic) with separate confidence.
5. Stop at an indirect dispatcher, a CAN/ADC/peripheral read, or after three decoded writers.

## Required deliverable

- Writer count and up to three decoded sites (PC, routine, gate, source, range).
- The observed constant set, or the arithmetic lineage if continuous.
- Verdict on mode-selector vs continuous input, with confidence, and the RR consequence for `0x108B4` (two-entry table vs threshold on an unnamed input).
- Exact unresolved boundary, reused evidence, no-repeat list.
