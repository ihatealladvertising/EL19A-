## Findings

### C0D6 result at FFFF2936

- `sub_A90BA` loads `R14=FFFF2150` at `0xA91E6`, calls `sub_A95BA` at `0xA91E8`, and returns to the caller. `sub_A95BA` saves caller R14 at `0xA95BA` and restores it in the RTS delay slot at `0xA95EA`.
- In `sub_A95BA`, `0xA95D6-0xA95DC` gate the value: gate byte zero copies lookup `R0` into `R2`; nonzero selects zero. `0xA95DE` loads base `FFFF2150`, `0xA95E0` loads offset `0x7E6`, and `0xA95E2` stores UWORD `R2` to `FFFF2936`. The ROM bytes at `0xA95E2` are `05 25`, matching the listing.
- The caller's delay-slot copy at `0xA91EA` is not the consumer: `0xA9200` overwrites R6 before its later use. The stored word is read directly at `0xA9266`: `0xA924A` loads offset `0x7E6`, `0xA924C` branches to `loc_A9266`, then `mov.w @(R0,R14),R4` reads UWORD `FFFF2150+0x7E6 = FFFF2936`. R14 still holds `FFFF2150`: `sub_A95BA` saves/restores it, and the intervening helpers `sub_A96BA`, `sub_A9644`, `sub_A9620`, `sub_A96F2`, and `sub_A9544` have no R14 writes. `sub_A9644` calls `sub_21BE4`, which also has no R14 references or nested calls.
- The `0xA926E` call passes this read in R4 to `sub_A9580` (R5 and the delay-slot R6 are separate neighboring fields). `0xA9590` zero-extends R4 into R14; after `sub_21BE4` returns, `0xA95A2` multiplies R14 by R2. The helper contains no R14 references or nested calls, so the C0D6 value is retained for this multiplication.
- `sub_A9580` stages its first helper result as UWORD at `FFFF2150+0x7E8 = FFFF2938` (`0xA959E`), then uses the C0D6 value in the multiplication and calls `sub_21BE4` again. `0xA95B0` stores the resulting U32 at `FFFF2150+0x7E0 = FFFF2930`. This is the shortest directly proven output path; no unit, semantic name, status use, or downstream consumer of `FFFF2930` is assigned.
- The exact `FFFF2936` spelling in the listing is only the RAM allocation row. In the known `0x7E6` offset family, the code uses are the producer offset load at `0xA95E0` and consumer offset load at `0xA924A`. This does not exclude other arbitrary-base/offset or indirect paths.

### FFFF1D62 attribution and existing consumer coverage

- The direct consumer is already present in WO65067 and confirmed in the listing. `0x6BBB8` loads `R2=FFFF1D12`; `0x6BBBA` sets `R0=0x50`; `0x6BBBC` reads UWORD `R5` from `FFFF1D12+0x50 = FFFF1D62`. `0x6BBBE` zero-extends it, `0x6BBC0` tests zero, and `0x6BBD0` compares against `0xFFFF`.
- The existing caller uses `FFFF1D62` as a selector/weight for the C756/C956 values: zero selects UWORD `FFFF1570`, `0xFFFF` selects UWORD `FFFF1572`, and an interior value weights both values in the multiply/add sequence at `0x6BBE0-0x6BBF8`.
- Source correction: `0x6BBFC` does **not** store the blend result to `FFFF1D62`. R4 remains `FFFF1054`, R0 is loaded with `0x516` at `0x6BBFA`, and the `mov.w` at `0x6BBFC` stores the blended result to **`FFFF1054+0x516 = FFFF156A`**. The ROM bytes at `0x6BBFC` are `04 25`, matching the listing. The `FFFF1D62` word is therefore a blend input/control value in this path, not the C756/C956 weighted result.
