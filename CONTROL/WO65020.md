### Conclusion
- **No complete runtime vector longword at `FFFF85FC–FFFF85FF` is established.** Observed byte operations are fragmented: `85FB→85FC`, `85FE→85FD`, and `8602→85FF`; `85FE` itself has no direct writer in the inspected literal-reference set. The byte at `85FE` therefore remains unresolved.
- The fragments occur in different routines/contexts; their execution order relative to TRAPA site `0x157E` is not established. No handler destination or link to `INT_TRAPA62` can be claimed.
- The reads of bit7 from `85FB` and `85FD` are consistent with nearby status-byte use, but do not prove the slot is not used as a vector under a special runtime state.
- **Stop condition met:** bounded direct-literal and identified copy-path audit found no complete vector. Indirect aliases, computed writes, unrepresented bulk/DMA/peripheral initialization, and live runtime values remain outside this pass. Do not infer fault/recovery behavior.
