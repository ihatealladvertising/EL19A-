# WO65044 — F33E shared-query result/dataflow audit

**Status:** BOUNDED COMPLETE  
**Candidate:** F33E, 16-point UBYTE, target ROM offset `0x0F33E`  
**Target:** `VERSA f501.bin`, 1 MiB, MD5 `46991697c502052752a2cabcb84c9e27`

## Scope
Trace F33E through its two known callers and record result handling without repeating the FFFF84B8 producer investigation from WO64819.

## Verified mechanics

- Both callers read the same raw UBYTE query `[FFFF84B4+4] = FFFF84B8`; no caller-side conversion or clamp is applied at either F33E call.
- First caller: `loc_4F8AC`, call PC `0x4F8C8`. R13 is set to `FFFF84B4`; query byte is loaded at `0x4F8C4`; R4 receives F33E at `0x4F8C2`; R14 holds `sub_22E10`. Result is zero-extended and retained in R12 (`0x4F8CA–0x4F8CC`).
- In the same caller, R12/F33E is used as a multiplicative operand at `0x4FA32` with the preceding `sub_22180` result in R0. The product is captured in R2 at `0x4FA36` and feeds the subsequent fixed-point arithmetic and bounded output calculations. The path is coupled to F34E/F35E/F36E and other intermediate values; F33E is not directly written to a standalone RAM result field in this caller.
- Second caller: `loc_4FBC2`, call PC `0x4FBE0`. It reads the same raw query at `0x4FBD8`, passes R4=F33E (`0x4FBD4`), and receives the UBYTE result in R6 (`0x4FBE4`).
- The second caller zero-extends UWORD `FFFF1054+0x2FA = FFFF134E`, multiplies it by the F33E result, multiplies by 100, then shifts right 14 bits (8+2+2+2). The result is capped at `0xFFFF` and stored as UWORD at `FFFF1D12+0x46 = FFFF1D58` (`0x4FBE6–0x4FC08`).
- The routine then copies that UWORD from `FFFF1D12+0x46` to `FFFF1D12+0x48 = FFFF1D5A` (`0x4FC88–0x4FC8E`). This is a confirmed RAM staging/copy path, not a physical actuator endpoint.
- Neighboring curves remain separate: F34E produces the `FFFF1054+0x54E` derived value and FFFF1D5C copy; F35E produces `FFFF1054+0x550` and FFFF1D5E; F36E produces `FFFF1054+0x552` and FFFF1D60.

## Target payload

`0F33E:` `3F 3F 35 29 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00`

Payload read directly from the hash-identified target ROM. Reader is the existing fixed-grid `sub_22E10` UBYTE curve with 16 points at breakpoints 0,16,…,240; helper accepts queries `0x00..0xEF`. Since callers do not clamp FFFF84B8, query `>=0xF0` may follow the helper rejection path.

## Disposition

- **Structure / reader:** confirmed 16-point UBYTE curve; fixed-grid `sub_22E10`.
- **Query:** shared raw UBYTE FFFF84B8; source producer and operational range remain unresolved under WO64819's bounded audit.
- **Result:** first caller retains F33E in R12 for coupled fixed-point arithmetic; second caller scales it with UWORD FFFF134E and stages UWORD FFFF1D58, then copies it to FFFF1D5A.
- **Semantics / units:** unassigned. The largely zero payload does not justify a function label or unit.
- **No artifact changes:** no workbook, XML, ROM, LST, IDB, scripts or Library files modified.

## Evidence boundary
Annotated V32 listing around `0x4F8AC–0x4FB4A` and `0x4FBC2–0x4FCA2`; target ROM bytes at `0x0F33E–0x0F34D`; WO64819 PAIR-01 Trace sheet for shared-query history. No broad RAM/XREF scan was performed.
