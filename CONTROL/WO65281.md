### Helper contract used above

`sub_2245C` at `0x2245C` takes `r0` = byte count, `r1` = destination, `r2` = source. The unrolled loop stores `@r1`, `@(1,r1)`, `@(2,r1)`, `@(3,r1)` in four-byte chunks. For `r0=3`, the three-byte stores are exact. The count is supplied in the `jsr` delay slot at `0x20FB8` and `0x20FC0`.

## Producer chain A — `FFFF85F8.bit4`

1. `INT_TRAPA62` polls `FFFF8641.bit0` at `0x25098` and calls the contiguous `sub_20DCE`/`sub_20FEA` body at `0x250A8`.
2. That body sets `GBR = FFFF85EC` at `0x20DE2` and keeps it unchanged through the callback loop at `0x21210`.
3. At `0x20FA2`, `r0` is loaded from `FFFF85F9`. At `0x20FB0`, `r0` is stored to `GBR+0xC = FFFF85F8`. This is the definite whole-byte producer; `FFFF85F8.bit4` is a copy of `FFFF85F9.bit4`.
4. The only bounded direct setter of `FFFF85F9.bit4` is `0x290D2`: `or #h'30,r0` sets bits 4 and 5, and the `bsr sub_28B0C` delay slot stores that value before the helper executes. It lies in `loc_290A4` of `sub_28C36`, called from `loc_3F304` in `sub_3E67C` when `r10 != 0`.
5. The other direct `FFFF85F9` writes found in scope do not set bit4: `0x250B6` clears bit6 after the producer call, `0x35E88` sets bit6, `0x77944` clears bit3, and `0x7A944` sets bit3.

**Latency note:** `sub_3E67C` is called by the `sub_403D8` callback `loc_3C478` at `0x3C49A`, i.e. after `0x20FB0` has already copied the byte in the same interrupt invocation. A bit4 set at `0x290D2` therefore appears at `FFFF85F8` on the next `INT_TRAPA62` copy, not retroactively in the current lookup.

## Producer chain B — `FFFF8600.bit6`

1. In the same contiguous interrupt body, `0x20FB6` copies `FFFF85FF–FFFF8601` to `FFFF8605–FFFF8607`; this does not touch `FFFF8600`.
2. At `0x20FBE`, `sub_2245C` copies `FFFF8602–FFFF8604` to `FFFF85FF–FFFF8601`. Therefore `UBYTE[FFFF8600] = UBYTE[FFFF8602]`, and the selector bit is sourced from `FFFF8602.bit6`.

## Chronology relative to `sub_403D8`

- `INT_TRAPA62` (`0x25004`) sets its own GBR, polls `FFFF8641.bit0`, and enters the producer body at `0x250A8`.
- `0x20FB0` and `0x20FBE` occur before the body's callback loop at `0x21210`.
- The loop at `0x21210–0x21218` walks 122 pointers from `off_2187C`. Entry index `(0x21908-0x2187C)/4 = 35` is `loc_3C478`.
- `loc_3C478` calls `sub_3E67C` at `0x3C49A`, then calls `sub_403D8` at `0x3C4BE`. Thus, within one `INT_TRAPA62` invocation, both selector bytes are written before the QDSPT lookup runs.
- `sub_3E67C → sub_28C36 → 0x290D2` can update the `FFFF85F9` source later in the same callback; that affects `FFFF85F8` only on the next interrupt copy (chain A latency note).
- The `FFFF8602` producers (`sub_B0C1A`, `sub_B1A24`) belong to separate configuration/mode dispatch paths, not the contiguous `INT_TRAPA62` producer body. No static ordering between those paths and a given `INT_TRAPA62` copy is provable from this listing; the exact boundary is: producer dispatch updates `FFFF8602`, then the next `INT_TRAPA62` copy at `0x20FBE` transfers it to `FFFF8600`.
