## Question
Which fuel/air calibration candidates have confirmed EL19A physical identities (table address, scalar address, reader consumer, functional endpoint), and which remain generic candidates without established target-side identity?

## Falsifiable hypotheses
1. Fuel/air candidates identified by WO65293/WO65305 as unresolved physical identities do not map to verified EL19A table or scalar locations.
2. Candidates that share structure with known fuel-cut or mixture-control paths may be misidentified as fuel/air tables when they serve actuator-endpoint or protection functions.

**Prediction:** a confirmed physical identity requires a trace from candidate storage through a reader/consumer PC to a fuel quantity, injector pulse-width, MAF scaling, or AFR target endpoint. A storage location without a consumer trace remains a generic candidate.

## Exact scope
- Reconcile fuel/air candidate physical identities flagged as unresolved in WO65293 (3D ignition/fuel semantic triage) and WO65305 (bounded ignition/map/fuel investigation).
- For each unresolved candidate: record address, storage/geometry, query/axes, reader/consumer PCs, endpoint (if any), source WO/findings, structural/axis/functional confidence separately, and unresolved boundary.
- Do not assume CF48D or MR16DDT donor names/scaling are EL19A identities.
- Do not perform broad ROM scans, donor-label promotion, vehicle testing, live logging, XML edits, or definition generation.
- MAF scaling (`0x0F712` axis, `0x10F8A` curve) is already located; do not repeat its discovery.

## Required classification
For each candidate: `FOUND`, `GENERIC CANDIDATE`, `NOT LOCATED`, or `NOT APPLICABLE`. Include candidate addresses, storage/geometry, query/axes, reader/consumer PCs, endpoint, source WOs/findings, structural/axis/functional confidence separately, and unresolved boundary.

## Smallest discriminators
Trace each unresolved candidate as far as the first fuel-quantity, injector scheduling, MAF, or AFR target endpoint. If none reaches such an endpoint, preserve the negative with exact terminal cell/path.

## Stop boundary
Produce a candidate identity matrix and at most three ranked next tests. A bounded negative is complete.
