## Pairing / selection determination

**B421 vs B42A:** Alternate surfaces, not paired banks.

Evidence:
1. Single branch at `0x2D96C` (`tst #h'40` on byte at `FFFF23AC`; `bt loc_2D972` / `bra loc_2DD14`) selects exactly one of the two reads per invocation.
2. Both reads use the **same index register `r13`** and stage to the **same RAM location `FFFF228C + 0x16`** — they are mutually exclusive views of one logical slot, not two simultaneously-consumed banks.
3. The flag bit at `FFFF23AC.bit6` is the selector chronology; no evidence that B10C influences this gate.

**B10C:** Separate knock-slice calculation.

Evidence:
1. Independent reader at `0x2E3CC` in `sub_2E390`, called from a different context.
2. Different index (`r14` vs `r13`), different element width (UWORD vs UBYTE), different stage base (`FFFF206E` vs `FFFF228C`).
3. No data or control edge from the B10C reader to the B421/B42A selector at `0x2D96C`, and vice versa.
