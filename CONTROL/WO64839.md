## Correction note — reader arguments and fixed-point/status handling (2026-11-16)

Instruction-by-instruction review of `sub_449C2` corrects three details in the Stage 3 starting summary above:

1. **Third lookup pointer:** the `FFFF84B8` query is passed to `sub_22E10` with **E43C**, not E434. The first `sub_22DF8` call uses E434 with `min(UWORD[FFFF8494] >> 7, 255)`; its JSR delay slot increments R14 by 8. The E424 call then uses UBYTE `FFFF85A9`; the third call uses the incremented pointer E43C and UBYTE `FFFF84B8`. The results are stored respectively at `FFFF1054+0x0A`, `+0x09`, and `+0x0B`.
2. **Product factor vs. status byte:** `FFFF1D99+6` is **not** a fourth numeric multiplier. The fourth product operand is branch-derived UBYTE `FFFF1054+8`. The 32-bit product is the successive `MUL.L` of UBYTE values at `+0x0A`, `+0x09`, `+0x0B), and `+0x08`; its upper byte (`product >> 24`) is stored at `FFFF1C78+0`. The old `FFFF1D99+6` byte is read only to preserve its other bits while bit 6 is set when the resulting byte is nonzero and cleared when it is zero.
3. **Query provenance wording:** WO64832 establishes `FFFF85A9 = min(UWORD[FFFF85CE] >> 4, 255)` via `sub_346E0). It does **not** establish that `FFFF85CE` is timer-derived or that it represents RPM. Its upstream source and physical units remain unresolved. The prior phrase "timer-capture-derived" in this WO is withdrawn for this field.

### Direct consumers of the generated byte

- `loc_401B0` reads `FFFF1C78+0` and `+6`, adds them, multiplies by ROM UBYTE `0x880C`, shifts right one and caps at 255; it writes `FFFF1054+0x25C = FFFF12B0` (PC `0x401B2–0x401DC`).
- `sub_44BB2` also reads `FFFF1C78+0` and `+6`; it subtracts `UBYTE(FFFF1054+0x2F5) >> 4`, clamps to 0–255, and writes `FFFF1D1A` (PC `0x44BB2–0x44BE8`).
- `sub_44BEE` reads `FFFF1C78+0x1E`, not `+0`. `sub_44C60` uses the two query fields for separate lookups and writes an internal structure field; it is not a consumer of the generated `FFFF1C78+0` byte. Do not group these by shared GBR base alone.

**Disposition unchanged:** exact structural fingerprint; query-to-RPM compatibility and donor raw/256 transfer remain unproven; direct downstream paths terminate at internal calculated/state bytes in the inspected scope. Keep E424 generic pending any new physical input/endpoint evidence.
