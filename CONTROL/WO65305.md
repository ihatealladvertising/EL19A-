Advance EL19A fuel/AF candidates from generic table/scalar morphology to bounded function identities, prioritizing base fuel, warm-up/enrichment, transient fuel, mixture targets and fuel correction factors.

## Scope

1. Use only existing target candidate inventory and completed reader/query/consumer reports; do not repeat the global scalar sweep or the 179-row catalogue audit.
