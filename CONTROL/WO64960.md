### Important pointer clarifications
- E8A8 at 0x4A5AA is a separate 8-point object, all bytes 0x14; the earlier call at 0x4A5A0 has R4 incremented from E8A8 to E8B0 in the delay slot. Thus E8B0 and E8A8 are distinct physical 8-byte lookup operands.
- EA98 at the first call 0x4AF64 has R4 incremented by 8 in the delay slot, so the helper actually receives EAA0 (all 0x80). The second call at 0x4AF72 reloads EA98 (all 0x40). Both are constant structural objects, not varying curves.
- The two calls at 0x402D8 and 0x402F2 both reload R4=E9DC; they are two queries of the same one 8-byte object, not an E9DC/E9E4 pair.
