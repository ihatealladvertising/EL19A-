## Objective

Test one cheap, bounded discriminator for whether the opaque EM43A Osiris payload uses a short fixed-period **additive** byte transformation (mod 256) rather than the already-tested XOR rule. This is file-format function identity only; no EL19A calibration semantics are promoted.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

- **Hypothesis:** the payload's high entropy is partly produced by a per-column additive constant with period <=64.
- **Prediction:** at a true period, byte columns will be measurably non-uniform (elevated index of coincidence / lower column entropy / raised max-frequency share), and a per-column most-frequent-byte additive candidate will yield visibly lower-entropy or structured bytes when applied to both payloads.
- **Result:** UNRUN — do not claim support or rejection before execution.
- **Information gain:** separates the already-falsified XOR/MFB model from the simplest sibling transformation without broad cryptanalysis.
