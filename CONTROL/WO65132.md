## Decision question — ANSWERED

The 32×1 UWORD BE curve at `0x0FEE0` is read once by `sub_22E98` at `0x369EA` inside `loc_369D0` (resolver/call-graph labels this region under `sub_366A4`). The query is `UWORD[FFFF182C+0x20 = FFFF184C] << 4`, zero-extended before the shift. The helper returns the selected UWORD entry (`r0` as word at `r4+0x7E` on the saturated path), and the caller stores that result as a UWORD to `FFFF84A4+0x02 = FFFF84A6`.

The first direct post-store consequence is in `sub_36A08` (`0x36A0A`): it reads `FFFF84A6`, clamps it to the signed-word range `[0, 0xFFFF]` by the explicit `cmp/gt 0xFFFF` / `cmp/pz` sequence, and writes the clamped UWORD to `FFFF182C+0x14 = FFFF1840`. `FFFF1840` is then read by `sub_36A26 @ 0x36A3A/0x36A72` and `sub_37074 @ 0x370B8` as a bounded downstream consumer chain. Two additional direct reads of `FFFF84A6` exist at `0x38B3A` and `0x38B58` in `sub_388B4`; they consume the same cell but are not in the immediate `loc_369D0` post-store chain.

No physical-unit, airflow/load/fuel/ignition, or operating-mode semantic is established. The fixed 2048-step query-grid label is consistent with the `<< 4` query scaling and helper threshold `0x3F00`, but the runtime rule is defined by code, not the catalogue label.
