### Critical boundaries

1. `0x11744` ends at `0x11943`; `0x11944` begins at the next byte and is a different lookup object with different geometry/helper. They must not be merged.
2. The matcher/workbench donor suggestions for `0x0BB56` and `0x11744` are not accepted as EL19A symbol assignments. Target-side geometry and runtime mechanics are stronger than donor-label evidence; the `0x11744` payload is constant in any case.
3. `0x0A092` is not an unverified 8×8 discovery. It was directly traced in WO64963 and explicitly classified as zero-filled. The fact that an older definition contained a mapping does not mean it belongs in the current conservative Release 1.
4. `0x0ADF2` axes are descriptor/GBR-rooted at runtime, with resolved backing ROM arrays `0xF4AE` and `0xF4CA`. Do not invent fixed-axis bytes or merge its column axis with `0x0AF72`.
5. This task does not revisit `0x0A412). WO65123's 16-byte all-`0x26` argument observation still does not prove or disprove the scanner's claimed complete 8×8 extent.
