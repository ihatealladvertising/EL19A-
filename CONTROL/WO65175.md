## Required work

2. Extract and normalize COMPU_METHOD scaling, units, format, coefficients, limits and referenced tables; identify shared Nissan naming/scaling conventions.
3. Compare characteristic/axis/function naming across X12C and V6; classify apparent Bank1/Bank2, Normal/Trust, Drive/Neutral and other pairs only where explicit metadata supports the distinction.
4. Crosswalk candidate scaling and pair structures to the 179 EL19A catalogue entries, carrying forward existing EL19A reader/axis evidence from WO65133–WO65141. Donor metadata is a search hint, never proof of EL19A meaning.
5. Produce a compact reusable mapping and contradiction/ambiguity register, including cases where identical names have different conversion methods.

## Acceptance criteria
- Reproducible inventory of A2L conversion methods and references, with exact source locations.

- Bank symmetry requires paired characteristic declarations and compatible axes/record layouts; do not infer bank identity from suffixes alone.
- Every proposed EL19A match links to its EL19A catalogue row and existing reader evidence; no ungrounded semantic promotion.
- Report top 20 high-value candidate matches and unresolved questions.
