## 2. Exact defect mechanism and algebraic proof

Inspection of `EVIDENCE/WO65118_ram_byte_bit_xref_generator.py` confirms the exact root cause:

1. The pinned listing already annotates SH7058 displacement operands in **bytes**:
   - Word instructions (`mov.w @(disp,GBR)` `C5 dd`/`C1 dd` and `mov.w @(disp,Rn)` `85 nd`/`81 nd`) encode a 4-bit or 8-bit `raw_op_disp`, which the listing displays as `h'<raw_op_disp * 2>`.
   - Longword instructions (`mov.l @(disp,GBR)` `C6 dd`/`C2 dd` and `mov.l @(disp,Rn)` `5n md`/`1n md`) encode `raw_op_disp`, which the listing displays as `h'<raw_op_disp * 4>`.
   - Symbolic GBR annotations such as `@((dword_83B4 - h'8278),gbr)` also evaluate to an **already byte-scaled** displacement (`0x13C`), matching `raw_op_disp * 4` (`0x4F * 4`).
2. `WO65118_ram_byte_bit_xref_generator.py` parsed these already byte-scaled listing displacements into integer `disp` and then multiplied by `width_scale(width)` (`1` for `b`, `2` for `w`, `4` for `l`) a **second time** in both affected generator paths (`gbr_disp` and `base_disp`), computing `base + (already_byte_scaled_disp * scale)` instead of `base + already_byte_scaled_disp`.
3. Non-displacement forms (`base_r0`, `base_indirect`, `gbr_r0`) and byte-width displacement forms (`scale = 1`) did not undergo a non-unit second multiplication and are numerically unaffected (`ea_changed = 0`).
