## Objective

Determine the exact scope and impact of the WO65118 effective-address calculation defect, produce a corrected, independently validated successor index without overwriting historical evidence, and identify prior reports whose conclusions relied on affected address columns.

The outside audit reports 4,822 corrupted non-byte rows among 22,308 records. Treat those counts as a hypothesis until reproduced from pinned inputs.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

The WO65118 generator applies access-width scaling a second time to displacements that its LST parser has already represented as byte offsets. For GBR-relative and register-base displacement rows this yields `base + width × already_scaled_offset` instead of `base + already_scaled_offset`, corrupting non-byte effective addresses. Byte rows may remain numerically unaffected. The hypothesis is supported only if the defect is reproduced from the exact generator logic, independently checked against encoded SH7058 instructions and LST operands, and its complete row population is enumerated.

## Exact scope

1. Pin and hash the original `EVIDENCE/WO65118_ram_byte_bit_xref_generator.py`, `EVIDENCE/WO65118_ram_byte_bit_xref.tsv`, source ROM and LST identity recorded by WO65118/its successors. Do not modify or replace any of them.
2. Reproduce the defect algebraically and with representative rows covering byte, word and long accesses, GBR-relative and ordinary base-displacement forms, reads and writes, and any other width-bearing addressing forms emitted by the generator.
3. Audit every generator path that computes an effective address. Distinguish displacement scaling required by the SH7058 opcode encoding from an LST annotation that is already a byte displacement. Do not assume all non-byte records share one defect mechanism.
4. Regenerate a corrected successor TSV at a new path, preserving original row identity and provenance fields and adding corrected EA/span plus explicit correction status/reason. Keep unresolved or unsupported forms unresolved; never force an address.
5. Validate row counts, byte-row invariance, corrected address spans, duplicate/overlap behavior, ROM/RAM boundaries and sampled exact opcode/LST concordance. Independently recompute the reported 4,822 / 22,308 counts.
6. Produce an impact matrix of prior WO reports and findings that cite WO65118 EA/address columns. Classify each as: unaffected; affected but locally corrected by independent opcode proof; affected and conclusion needs re-evaluation; or unknown pending evidence. Prior reports are historical and must not be edited in this WO.
7. Record the exact corrected-generation method, hashes, test results, affected-row counts and impact disposition in this report. Recommend a safe migration plan for downstream consumers.
