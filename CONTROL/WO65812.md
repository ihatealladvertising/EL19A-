## Question
Which expected naturally aspirated MR18DE ignition calibration families are actually represented in the EL19A candidate inventory / provisional definition, and which remain generic or not located?

## Falsifiable hypotheses
1. At least one existing generic candidate can be tied to a spark-advance, dwell/coil-charge, cranking-advance or idle-advance output by a target-side consumer chain.
2. The existing knock-related candidates may be internal correction/count inputs without a proven final spark endpoint; do not infer a physical ignition table from their labels or morphology.

**Prediction:** a valid identity requires a ROM-side chain from table reader through arithmetic/branching to a spark advance or dwell scheduling output. A candidate that ends at a RAM staging cell without that endpoint remains generic.
