# WO65323 — Whole-inventory consistency test for major maps and axes

**Status:** ASSIGNED — WORKER 1 — READY  
**Priority:** P1  
**Owner:** Worker 1  
**Dependencies:** Reuse existing catalogue, reader/query/axis reports and closed semantic WOs; no prerequisite re-tracing.

## Objective
Rank the major EL19A map and axis candidates by how well they fit together as one calibration system, rather than judging each table in isolation.

## Scope
- Reconcile all 2D candidates and their axes against verified ROM extents, storage, reader contracts, query producers, orientation and value ranges.
- Identify plausible pairings, alternate arrangements, duplicate/trust placements and contradictions.
- Include high-value 1D support curves only where they materially constrain a map pairing.
- Apply three tiers: **Established**, **High-confidence provisional**, **Exploratory**.
- Prioritize ignition, fuel/air, load/torque, throttle, temperature and speed-related families.

## Evidence rules
Target-side EL19A evidence controls. Donor A2L/XML metadata may suggest names or analogues, never prove identity. Preserve structural, axis and functional confidence separately. Do not repeat completed reader/payload/consumer work; cite and reuse closed WOs. No broad ROM/RAM scans.

## Deliverable
A compact whole-inventory matrix: candidate address, geometry/storage, axis pair and orientation, query contract, value/range fit, related tables, confidence tier, conflicts, and RR inclusion recommendation. Identify the smallest unresolved questions that prevent a coherent arrangement.

## Stop conditions
Stop when every major 2D candidate is classified into a tier and every proposed map/axis pairing has explicit evidence or an explicit unresolved conflict. Do not edit XML or the workbook.
