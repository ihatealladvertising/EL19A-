## Question

`loc_47B54` stores the 16-bit result of `sub_48C1E` (`jsr` at `0x047BF0`) twice: `mov.w r0,@r6` with `r6 = 0xFFFFB644` at `0x047BF8`, and a byte-split copy to `UBYTE[FFFF1054+0x19C]`/`0x19D` (= `U16[FFFF11F0]`) at `0x047BFA`–`0x047C08`. Who reads those two cells, and do they reach a named output (MMIO, command payload, lamp/diagnostic publication) or terminate in further internal state?

This is the hop WO66048 stopped at. The verdict chain is otherwise complete: coolant-temperature curves → thresholds → `FFFF34D2.bit6` + a 0/1 return → enum state `FFFF11F3` and this publication. The publication is the only place on the chain where a *value* (not just a bit) leaves the routine.

## Why it has value

Every status-bit trace in this lane — `FFFF3579.bit4` (WO65910), `FFFF34F5.bit5` (WO65877), `FFFF284E` (WO65922), `FFFF34D2.bit6` (WO66048) — has so far terminated in "internal state, empty or status-only consumer set". This is the first located 16-bit *value* publication produced by that status network, so it is the cheapest discriminator for whether the whole `FFFF34xx`/`FFFF35xx` block has any reader outside the firmware's own logic.

## Exact scope

1. Enumerate every reader and writer of `FFFFB644` (UWORD) and `FFFF11F0`/`FFFF11F1` (`FFFF1054+0x19C`/`0x19D`) — absolute pool literals, `mov.w word_NN` base loads with the `0x19C`/`0x19D` displacement, and any `@(h'NN,gbr)` form whose own base resolves to either cell.
2. For the two to three readers, trace at most two consumer hops: the test/arithmetic applied and where its result is stored.
3. Classify: `NAMED OUTPUT`, `INTERNAL STATE`, `CALIBRATION QUERY INPUT` or `UNRESOLVED`, and state which identity layer (input/function/output) the result advances.

## Stop boundary

Two 16-bit cells, their access census, and at most two consumer hops. No re-trace of `sub_488BA` or the `0xED64`/`0xEDA0` verdict tree (closed by WO66048); no characterisation of `sub_48C1E`'s internal inputs; no `FFFF2150` band work; no `FFFF34D2` bit-level work beyond noting a reader; no vehicle testing; no XML or definition generation.
