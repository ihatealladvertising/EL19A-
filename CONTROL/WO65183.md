## Objective

Resolve the target-side producers, polarity and chronology of the control flags that govern the QDSPT E678/E688 branch and its associated threshold behavior in `sub_403D8`. In particular, determine what the firmware establishes about:

- `FFFF1DB4.bit5` and the ROM threshold candidate at `0x8550`;
- `FFFF85F8.bit4` and `FFFF8600.bit6`, which jointly gate the E688 path;
- `FFFF860B.bit3`, which can bypass the pair of curve calculations;
- the relation between the E678/E688 selection and the corresponding `0x848A/0x848B` scalar selection.

The investigation must distinguish a proven software temperature-threshold condition from a physical coolant-temperature identity, and a proven branch selector from a Drive/Neutral label.
