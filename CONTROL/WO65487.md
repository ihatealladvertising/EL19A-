## Objective

Identify where the `0x0CD56` reader's query actually comes from. WO65322 proved the row index is an axis interpolation and that the query is offset-binary, but the query argument to `sub_690C6` is inherited from `sub_68C28`'s caller, and the always-executed lookup uses `U16[FFFF1640]`. Determine the producer and physical class of `FFFF1640` and `FFFF164C` and the identity of `sub_68C28`'s caller chain.

## Mandatory falsifiable hypothesis

- **Identity layer advanced:** input identity.
- **Hypothesis:** `FFFF1640` and `FFFF164C` are internal filtered state cells of one correction loop, so their producers are the same small function family rather than an acquisition or CAN source.
- **Evidence:** WO65322 (`0x6913C`/`0x6915A`, `0x6B24C`/`0x6B278`, `0x87C8=0x00`); WO65305 step 7 lists `FFFF1640` writers `sub_6DF26` (`0x6E1E6`/`0x6E20C`) and readers in `sub_690C6`, `sub_6B484`, `sub_6CB06`, `sub_6CC22`, `sub_8272C`, `sub_B203E`, `sub_B2300`, `sub_BF814`; WO65120 call graph for `sub_68C28`.
- **Prediction:** the callers of `sub_68C28` will be a small bounded set inside the same correction/limiting region, and `FFFF1640`/`FFFF164C` writers will be arithmetic (filter/clamp) rather than ADC/CAN reads.
- **Cheap discriminator:** resolve the caller set from `EVIDENCE/WO65120_call_graph.tsv` and decode only those call sites plus the two producers' arithmetic.
