## Objective

Resolve the genuine index anomaly exposed by WO65536's within-routine scan. In `sub_16FC`, three WO65118 rows (`0x174A` write, `0x178A`/`0x17AE` reads, all `mov.l @(h'15C,gbr)` / `mov.l r0,@(h'15C,gbr)`, `base=ffff8260`) carry the nominal endpoint `ffff87d0`. Base arithmetic gives encoded disp `0x57`×4 = `h'15C` (raw bytes `C2 57`/`C6 57`) and EA `FFFF8260+0x15C = FFFF83BC`, not `FFFF87D0`. Two further WO65118 rows are correctly labelled `ffff83bc` but belong to unrelated byte-sized routines: `0x2C05A` (`mov.b r0,@((dword_83BC - h'82D4),gbr)`, `base=ffff82d4`) and `0x2CFF4` (`mov.b @((dword_83BC - h'82E0),gbr),r0`, `base=ffff82e0`), both resolving to `FFFF83BC` under their own bases.

`FFFF83BC` has **2 exact WO65118 rows** (the two byte rows) and **0 WO65117 rows**; the three longword rows are absent from its index entry because of the `ffff87d0` label. `FFFF87D0` itself has 6 WO65118 rows, 3 of which are these mislabelled rows.
