## Objective

WO65125 lists candidate reads at PCs `0x68E62`, `0x69028`, `0x69258` and `0x88682`. WO65231 establishes that the E914 status bit `FFFF34C6.bit4` has no confirmed post-write bit-specific reader. The remaining question is whether the stored curve result itself is consumed even though its threshold flag is not.

## Result — REJECTED: no same-slot reader found
