## 1. Decision answer

**`FFFFB6F0` is consumed as a pointer, by indexed table load, in `sub_74212`.** It is slot 4 of record 0 in a RAM array of 10-pointer records based at `FFFFB6E0` (`FFFFB61C+0xC4`), stride `0x28` bytes. `sub_74212` selects a record, loads each of its 10 pointers with `mov.l @(r0,r14)`, reads **one byte** through each pointer, and stores the 10 bytes into a 10-byte RAM destination chosen by a mode argument. When record 0 is selected, the byte at `FFFF84BB` (the referent, not the pointer) is copied to `FFFFAF66` (mode 0) or `FFFF2B14` (mode 1).

The pointer value itself is never overwritten after the `sub_24EA8` bulk copy, within the access forms tested in §5. No direct reader of the destination bytes was found; that is the open boundary (§6).
