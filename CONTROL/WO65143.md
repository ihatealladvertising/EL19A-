## 1. Scope

Record the supplied statistical and first-difference comparison of three 256-byte blocks in `CF48D.bin`, each interpreted as a row-major 16×16 UBYTE matrix:

| Start | User-supplied label | Extent (inclusive) |
|---|---|---|
| `0x8DD1` | Cold / Knock Window | `0x8DD1–0x8ED0` |
| `0x8ED1` | High Octane / Knock Window | `0x8ED1–0x8FD0` |
| `0x8FD1` | High Detonation / Knock Window | `0x8FD1–0x90D0` |

For each matrix the supplied script computes raw min/max/span/mean/std, min-max normalized statistics and quartiles, row/column means, and mean signed/absolute first differences along each dimension. It then compares the first two matrices byte-for-byte and computes the signed difference of the third against the first.

No EL19A address search, donor-to-target address transfer, ROM/LST/XML modification, or calibration semantic promotion is in scope.

## 2. Supplied method

The supplied script uses NumPy to read the ROM as bytes, slices `rom[sa:sa+256]`, converts each slice to `float64`, and reshapes it to `(16,16)`. It computes:
- `dx = np.diff(b, axis=1)` (15 horizontal differences per row);
- `dy = np.diff(b, axis=0)` (15 vertical differences per column);
- per-row and per-column means;
- signed and absolute mean gradient profiles;
- min-max normalized values and z-scores;
- exact equality and elementwise difference for the pairwise comparisons.

The supplied code computes `zscore` but does not print or otherwise report its statistics. The reported normalized quartiles are min-max normalized values, not z-score quartiles.

## 4. Bounded observations

1. The three blocks have a common broad two-region shape: low values in the first five columns and boundary/outer rows, with a higher-valued interior region. In the first two blocks, the supplied gradient profile shows the strongest vertical transition around C04→C05 and strong horizontal transitions around R00→R01 and R11→R12.
2. `0x8DD1` and `0x8ED1` are exact 256-byte duplicates in the supplied dump. This is a payload-level fact, not proof that their runtime roles, selectors, axes, or labels are interchangeable.
3. `0x8FD1` is cellwise no greater than `0x8DD1` and differs by up to 13 raw counts. Its average reduction is strongest in later columns, reaching approximately -7.8 counts in C12–C15.
4. These statistics are useful as a donor-side shape fingerprint for candidate comparison. They do not establish axis orientation, signedness beyond the supplied UBYTE interpretation, physical units, interpolation method, table extents in the ECU's actual reader, or ignition/knock semantics.
