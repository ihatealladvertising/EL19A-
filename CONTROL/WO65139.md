## Coordinator erratum — axis-domain comparison (2026-11-22)

The earlier comparisons in §§3 and 4 incorrectly compared EL19A raw UWORD/UBYTE axis coordinates directly with V6 A2L converted engineering values (for example, COMP_0434_S and COMP_0054). That is not a valid range test unless the target-side conversion is independently established. It is not established in this work order.

Accordingly, withdraw the claims that E904/E914 have a proven non-overlapping axis range against mTHOSNTP/mTHOSRCV, and withdraw the associated CONTRADICTED dispositions based on those range comparisons. Likewise, raw-vs-converted range differences do not establish a contradiction for E934 against the pressure candidates. Axis storage-width differences for E904/E914 versus the pressure candidates remain observed structural differences, but do not by themselves prove different physical semantics.

Corrected disposition: no credible V6 counterpart is ESTABLISHED for E904, E914 or E934 in this bounded comparison. The outcome is NOT ESTABLISHED, not a proven semantic contradiction. Structural declaration facts and the V6 function-group descriptions remain valid donor-side observations; target physical domains, conversion functions and comparable engineering-unit ranges remain unresolved. No target-side semantics or scale is promoted, and the V6 A2L remains metadata-only evidence.

No ROM/LST/XML/A2L/catalogue/workbook changes were made. This erratum supersedes only the raw-vs-converted range reasoning and the associated contradiction labels; it does not change the underlying target reader traces in WO65124–WO65127.
