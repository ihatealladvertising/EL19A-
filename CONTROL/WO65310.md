## Objective

Resolve the upstream producer contract of `FFFF184C`, the query source for the 32×1 UWORD BE curve at ROM `0x0FEE0`. Determine whether the source is a sensor, operating-state variable, derived control quantity or unresolved internal field, and establish the numeric domain actually presented to the lookup.

## Mandatory falsifiable hypothesis

- **Identity layer advanced:** input identity and function identity.
- **Hypothesis:** `FFFF184C` is populated from `FFFF848E` on one branch and updated by a separate bounded calculation in `sub_36D80`; its source/units may distinguish the curve's functional domain.
- **Evidence:** WO65132 records query read at `0x369E0`, `query << 4` at `0x369E6/0x369E8`, lookup call `0x369EA`, and producer routine `sub_36D80` around `0x36D80–0x36DF8`. These are target-side anchors; no physical units are established.
- **Prediction:** the bounded producer/caller trace identifies a source field, arithmetic and state guards, or ends at a specific indirect/peripheral/interrupt boundary.
- **Cheap discriminator:** trace only the existing `sub_36D80` branches and nearest producer of `FFFF848E`, reusing the existing indexes and pinned LST.
- **Information gain:** distinguishes a sensor-derived query from a software state/normalized control variable without repeating the curve reader or helper mechanics.

## Scope

1. Reuse WO65132's exact reader/query contract: `UWORD[FFFF184C] << 4` is passed to `sub_22E98`; the helper saturates at `0x3F00`. Do not re-decode the table or helper.
2. In `sub_36D80`, resolve the branch on `FFFF35C6.bit7`, the copy/read of `FFFF848E`, and the alternate update path at `0x36DA2–0x36DF8`.
3. Record exact PCs, effective addresses, widths, signedness, arithmetic, clamp/wrap behavior and branch conditions for each accepted producer.

5. State whether a physical input or engineering unit is proven. If the path reaches an unresolved computed pointer, interrupt/acquisition boundary or dispatcher, stop there and retain the field as generic.
