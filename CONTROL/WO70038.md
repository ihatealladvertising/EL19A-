## Important scope boundary

The workbook is intentionally a **focused review workbook**, not a replacement for the complete raw registries. It does not embed the 2,161-row vehicle catalogue, the 3,691-row expanded ROM-ID crosswalk, the 43-row raw ECU/FID inventory, or all 431 per-file detailed DAT rows. Their complete source TSVs are linked on the **Sources** sheet. The workbook includes all 16 target-engine catalogue rows and a verified aggregate review of the full scan. No per-file scan rows were fabricated or inferred.

This is the bounded adjustment from the initial all-source-sheet concept: the repository connector exposed the complete scan index for review, but a reliable local transfer of all 431 raw rows into the spreadsheet builder was not available in this session. The raw source index remains unchanged and authoritative.
