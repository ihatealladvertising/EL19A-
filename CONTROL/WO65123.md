## 5. Two-input correction surface — determination

The two tables form a **coherent two-input correction surface**, not independent factors combined by the caller:

- `sub_22AB8` (8×8 UBYTE surface indexed by row+col from `FFFF84B8`/`FFFF851A`) and `sub_22E60` (8-point UWORD BE curve indexed by clamped `UWORD[FFFF1010]<<5`) are both called from the same caller `sub_4AD54`
- Their results are multiplied (`mul.l r14,r2` where `r14`=surface, `r2`=curve) within the same straight-line code block
- The product is shifted >>7 and clamped as a single atomic computation
- The result is stored once to `FFFF1346`, then flows `FFFF1346 → sub_4AE58 → FFFF1344 → sub_4AEAA → FFFF1340`

This is a multiplicative combination of two independent lookup results, computed entirely within `sub_4AD54`. No evidence of independent factors being combined by the caller outside this path.
