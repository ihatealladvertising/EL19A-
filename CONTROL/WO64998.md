## Evidence

- The preserved annotated LST defines `sub_449C2` at 0x449C2. At 0x449F6 it loads pointer `FFFF859C` into R5; at 0x449FA, `mov.b @(h'D,r5),r0` reads byte `FFFF85A9` as the E424 query.
- At 0x449FC it loads table pointer E424; 0x44A00 calls `sub_22E10`. The call delay-slot at 0x44A02 zero-extends the returned byte to R5. At 0x44A08 the result is stored at GBR+9, i.e. FFFF105D (GBR was set to FFFF1054 at 0x449CC–0x449CE).
- The same routine separately reads E434 through `sub_22DF8` at 0x449F2, using UBYTE query `FFFF859C+0x0A`; its result is stored at GBR+8 at 0x449F8. E434 is a separate lookup input, not an axis for E424.
- The E424 result is subsequently reloaded from GBR+9 at 0x44A1A and participates in the local multiply sequence at 0x44A24–0x44A30. The routine later writes a byte through the pointer loaded from ROM 0xFFFF1C78 at 0x44A32–0x44A3A. This is an immediate composite calculation boundary; no physical meaning is established.
- The RAM map lists FFFF85A9 as an undefined byte at LST line 475340. The preserved 419,661-row XREF CSV contains no row naming FFFF85A9 as source or target. This CSV is a ROM-oriented xref export and its lack of a RAM-address row is not evidence of global absence.
- The bounded LST search found no explicit direct store to FFFF85A9. Other uses of the FFFF859C base are numerous; no broad writer/copy reconstruction was performed.

- **Confirmed:** E424 reader query field is UBYTE FFFF859C+0x0D; lookup call is 0x44A00; result is staged at FFFF105D and enters a local composite multiply path.
- **Unresolved:** producer/containing-object/copy source for FFFF85A9; raw range and conversion; whether it matches any donor RPM-axis prediction; downstream physical endpoint.
