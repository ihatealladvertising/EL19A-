## Question

What explains the reported 842/1,863 two-donor address agreements, and which common A2L symbols are genuinely useful EL19A scalar leads?

## Falsifiable hypothesis record

- **Hypothesis:** The common-symbol agreement set contains a subset of conserved scalar locations, while the 1,021 non-agreements mix alignment uncertainty, donor-specific layout changes, symbol-address differences and genuine non-correspondence; treating all 45% as a quality score is invalid.
- **Evidence:** WO65815 validated outputs (1,863 common symbols, 842 T1 rows, donor-2 monotonic alignment unreliable); EVIDENCE/EL19A_scalar_alignment.tsv (2,696 rows: T1 842, T2 708, T3 1,146); pinned A2Ls (64811000p2_m.a2l = TK_X91 V6 donor, RFCC_2XHK19BDF1.a2l = Juke MR16DDT donor).
- **Prediction:** A donor-derived scalar name is accepted only when the mapping is reproducible and the EL19A target-side reader/consumer supports the proposed identity. Donor name/address agreement alone is not functional or physical calibration proof.
- **Cheap discriminator:** Cross-tab the committed TSV by tier, storage width, address neighborhood, and el19a_value vs donor_value equality; inspect a bounded subset of ≤25 agreements and ≤25 disagreements; compare against WO65815/WO65817 target-side verified anchors.
- **Result:** SUPPORTED for the unreliability hypothesis; NOT REQUIRED for donor-name promotion (no target-side reader/consumer confirmation beyond WO65815/WO65817 anchors).
- **Information gain:** Separates reproducible artifact tallies from unreproducible pair-by-pair agreement; separates structural address correspondence from target-side function/physical identity.

## Donor provenance correction (explicit, per scope rule)

- **64811000p2_m.a2l** = TK_X91 V6 donor metadata (NOT MR16DDT). Donor-1 address space.
- **RFCC_2XHK19BDF1.a2l** = Juke MR16DDT A2L. Donor-2 address space.
- The TSV `donor_addr` column uses donor-1/V6 addresses while `donor_value` sometimes matches the Juke symbol byte and sometimes does not — a coordinate/provenance mixing defect. No consumer should treat the TSV as a byte-exact donor map.

This WO confirms: 49.4% overall value agreement, with T1 at 75.9% and T3 at 23.1%. The 45.2% figure (842/1,863) is a name-level intersection count, not a byte-level agreement rate. The TSV's `donor_value` column sometimes matches Juke ROM bytes and sometimes does not, confirming coordinate/provenance mixing.

## Ranked reliable symbol leads

Per WO65816 scope rule and WO65815 finding, no donor-derived name can be accepted without target-side reader/consumer confirmation. The only symbol with independent target-side verification is:

- **mDSHCUT at 0x8550** — T1 agreement (el19a_val=10=donor_val=10), density 0.58; target-side verified at 0x8550 (UBYTE, raw 0x0A, threshold reader at 0x4043A, FFFF1DB4.bit5 effect per WO65014/WO65279/WO65817). Donor name not promoted beyond partial functional identity.
