## Decision and principal corrections

1. The previously described `FFFF33E6` / `FFFF33E8` pair (WO65074) and `FFFF2F00+0x468 = FFFF3368` (WO65087) are **RAM cells, not peripheral-window MMIO registers**. The earlier WO65074 wording "hardware-mapped output addresses" is not supported by the SH7058 address map; this report corrects that classification without rewriting the historical report.
2. The strongest verified hardware pulse-output path is an event-coupled Timer11/Timer5 loop: Timer11B computes Timer5 GR5A/B compare times; Timer5 A/B handlers schedule Timer11 GR11A and update APC_POPCRE; Timer11 A/B handlers clear/update APC state. The path is gated by Port D direction bits.
3. This establishes a real peripheral-control path, but not a specific injector, ignition, ISC, fan or MIL device. The Port D pin-function mux and physical harness endpoint are not established.

## 2. Verified timer compare and pulse-control paths

### Timer11 A/B interrupt endpoints

The Timer11 interrupt vectors are present in the ROM vector table:
- `0x202E4 → INT_ATU11_IMI11A` at `0x27978`.
- `0x202EC → INT_ATU11_IMI11B` at `0x27D6A`.

Their code directly manipulates the APC pulse-control word at `FFFFF700–FFFFF701`:
- `INT_ATU11_IMI11A` clears the low byte of the APC word with an `AND 0xFF00` write at `0x279B2–0x279BC`). On its qualified path it ORs the RAM mask at `FFFF1054+0xEA` back into the word at `0x27A74–0x27A80`. The low byte is `APC_POPCRE_B`.
- `INT_ATU11_IMI11B` extracts and clears the high byte of the APC word at `0x27DB0–0x27DBE`), saving the prior byte to `FFFF114E`. The high byte is `APC_PULS7SOE_B`; subsequent state logic updates the associated RAM mask fields. No external pin/device identity is assigned.
- These are hardware interrupt handlers, not merely RAM-stage writes. They establish direct Timer11-interrupt control of the APC register pair.

### Timer11B schedules Timer5 A/B events

- `INT_ATU11_IMI11B` uses the Timer5 counter base `FFFFF4E0` in its later state path.
- It writes Timer5 `GR5A` at `FFFFF4E2` from `loc_27F60` (store at `0x27F84`), and `GR5B` at `FFFFF4E4` from `loc_27FD2` (store at `0x27FF6`). The interval calculation consumes `FFFF85E4` (via `FFFF85C4+0x20`), state at `FFFF1CA8+0x3B`, ROM constants/tables including `0x8ABD`, `0x8BA8`, and `0x9434`, plus current `TCNT0` and `TCNT5` readings. The compare values are therefore state- and timer-derived; no physical time scale is assigned.
- ROM vector entries `0x20224` and `0x20228` point to `INT_ATU51_IMI5A` (`0x28186`) and `INT_ATU51_IMI5B` (`0x282C4`) respectively.

### Timer5 A/B events schedule Timer11 and APC state

- `INT_ATU51_IMI5A` and `INT_ATU51_IMI5B` both load the `TCNT11` base `FFFFF5C0`.
- In their enabled paths, they schedule `GR11A = TCNT11 + 2`: store at `0x28244` (A handler) and `0x28382` (B handler).
- Each handler reads the Port D direction register `PDDR` at `FFFFF746` via `PDIOR_W` base `FFFFF740`, tests the upper Port D direction bits against a per-path RAM mask (`FFFF114F` for A; `FFFF1150` for B), and skips the APC action if the selected direction bit(s) are not configured as outputs.
- When the gate passes, each handler reads the APC word at `FFFFF700`, ORs the corresponding mask into the low byte (`APC_POPCRE_B`), and writes the word back: A path at `0x28248–0x2824E`; B path at `0x28384–0x2838E`. The high byte (`APC_PULS7SOE_B`) is preserved by these stores.

WO65003 already traces `sub_27720` from the `FFFF85DF` consumer through `GR11A` (`FFFFF5C2`) and `GR11B` (`FFFFF5C4`), including the RAM-derived compare value, the overdue `TCNT11+3` path, status handling, and APC read-modify-write operations. **Supported event-coupled path:**

```text
INT_ATU11_IMI11B
   |-- schedules Timer5 GR5A / GR5B
   v
INT_ATU51_IMI5A / INT_ATU51_IMI5B
   |-- checks Port D PDDR bits (FFFFF746) against FFFF114F / FFFF1150
   |-- schedules Timer11 GR11A = TCNT11 + 2
   |-- ORs mask into APC_POPCRE_B
   v
INT_ATU11_IMI11A / INT_ATU11_IMI11B
   |-- clears / updates APC_POPCRE_B and APC_PULS7SOE_B state
   v
APC pulse-control pair at FFFFF700 / FFFFF701
   |
   +-- Port D upper-bit direction gate; physical pin/device unresolved
```

This is a real peripheral compare/interrupt/APC control loop. It is not sufficient to assign injector, ignition, ISC, fan or MIL function.

## 4. Port and pin-mux evidence

- `PDIOR_W` base is `FFFFF740`; the `PDDR` direction word is read at `FFFFF746`.
- The Timer5 A/B handlers test the upper Port D direction nibble (`PDDR & 0x0F00`) against masks derived from `FFFF114F` and `FFFF1150`. This is positive evidence that the APC pulse path is gated by Port D output-direction state.
- The `sub_27720` path uses an additional dynamic mask from `FFFF1054+0xEA`, also shifted into the upper byte before the PDDR test.
- The reviewed paths read PDDR; they do not configure it. No corroborating Port D pin-function mux write was established in this trace. The physical pin function and external circuit remain unresolved.
