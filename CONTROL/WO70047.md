### 4.2 Caller and helper anchors

At caller `0x85B2E` (LST lines 283511–283516) the listing shows the UBYTE query load at
`0x85B28`, `R4 = 0x0F10E`, `R2 = 0x22E10`, `jsr @r2`, and delay slot `extu.b r0,r5` at
`0x85B30`. The target and query path match the reported golden trace.

`sub_22E10` (9 instructions, LST 26379–26389) contains:

- `0x22E10 mov.w word_22E22,r0` with `word_22E22 = h'F0`;
- `0x22E12 extu.b r5,r5`;
- `0x22E14 cmp/hs r0,r5` → `0x22E16 bt locret_22E1E` for `q >= 0xF0`;
- otherwise `0x22E18 mov.l off_22E24,r6 | h'2350C` and `0x22E1A jmp @r6`;
- `0x22E1C mov #IVECT1,r3` is the jump delay slot.

### 4.3 Grid endpoint wording — resolved

Two mutually exclusive readings were carried forward by WO70040. The exact listing shows
they are complementary:

1. **Normal indexed path (`q < 0xF0`).** `loc_2350C` (LST 27498–27529) performs four
   `shlr r5` / `rotcr r3` pairs, making `R5 = q >> 4` (index 0–14) and placing the
   fractional bits in `R3`. Because `word_22E22 = h'F0`, `shlr8 r0` yields zero and
   `tst r0,r0` / `bt loc_23532` selects the byte path. `add r5,r4` points at
   `table[index]`; `mov.b @r4,r1` and `mov.b @(1,r4),r0` pass the indexed byte and the
   next byte to `sub_231C8`. Thus the reachable lower knot coordinates are exactly
   `0,16,…,224`, and the last indexed interval uses `table[14]` at 224 and `table[15]`
   at 240 as its endpoints.
2. **Clamp path (`q >= 0xF0`).** `sub_22E10` branches to `locret_22E1E` before any
   `add r5,r4`. The `rts` delay slot at `0x22E20` is `mov.b @(h'F,r4),r0`, loading the
   16th table element directly; the caller delay slot at `0x85B30` zero-extends it.
   Therefore 240 is the explicit final clamp point and upper endpoint, not a separately
   reachable lookup index.

`sub_231C8` entry lines 26984–26997 are consistent with the already-reported generic
interpolation contract (low endpoint, high endpoint, fraction). No full 243-instruction
opcode re-decode and no physical-unit claim is made.

**Resolution:** `0,16,…,224` is correct as the set of lower interpolation knots; `240`
is correct as the upper endpoint of the final interval and the clamp point. The apparent
conflict was a notation ambiguity, not a contradictory trace.
