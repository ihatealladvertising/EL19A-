4. Compare every regenerated TSV byte-for-byte with its committed blob.
5. Re-derive independently from regenerated rows: header fields, row counts, enumerations, distributions, anchors and negative controls specific to each package.
6. Re-derive listing-to-ROM PC concordance once with separate code (shared check from WO65121).
7. Record per-package and overall dispositions in this record; store the script and log under `EVIDENCE/WO65122_*`.
