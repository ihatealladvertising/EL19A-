# WO65080 — Role of the `0x11700–0x13000` Table Bank: Passive Mirror, Parallel Bank, or Flag-Selected Path

**Status:** BOUNDED COMPLETE (2026-11-21)
**Target:** `VERSA f501.bin` (1 MiB SH7058, MD5 `46991697c502052752a2cabcb84c9e27`) + `EL19A_Complete_Disassembly_ANNOTATED_V3_NEXT6_16_ALL_PENDING_ANNOTATIONS(1).lst` (MD5 `99635224d16ab7705445e27bf233e549`)
**Depends on:** WO65060 (18-for-18 byte-identical pairing, `0x0C756–0x1109A` ↔ `0x11700–0x13000`), WO65076/65070/65071 (helper and result-cell conventions).

## Objective

WO65060 established that eighteen `0x11700–0x13000` tables are byte-identical twins of eighteen `0x0C756–0x1109A` tables and labelled the region a "redundant / Trustful monitor bank". Byte identity is a property of the data; it says nothing about how the copies are consumed. This WO settles the consumption question only:

1. Are the trust-bank tables reached as **table pointers** (not merely mirrored bytes)?
2. When a routine reaches both banks, do the two results go to the **same** RAM cell (mutual exclusion / selector) or to **different** cells (parallel evaluation)?
3. Is any **ROM flag** found that gates which bank is evaluated?

## Scope and stop condition

- Method: literal-pool `mov.l off_X, rN ; h'<target>` loads whose annotated target lies in `0x0C756–0x13000`, followed within ten instructions by a `jsr`/`jmp` to one of the table helpers already catalogued in WO65060 (`sub_22*`, `sub_23*`); then the first same-register result store and its `r0` displacement.
- Stop at: the store displacement and the immediately surrounding gate instructions. No downstream consumer trace, no unit or physical identity, no whole-ROM cross-reference, no index/bounds proof.
- Concurrency: this WO does not reopen WO65060's byte-comparison work and does not touch WO65062/65064/65065/65077 scopes.

## 1. The trust bank is reached as table pointers, not only as stored bytes

| Quantity | Count |
|---|---:|
| literal-pool loads whose target is one of the 18 normal mirrors | 52 |
| literal-pool loads whose target lies in `0x11700–0x13000` | 131 |
| distinct `0x11700–0x13000` addresses used as helper arguments | 38 |
| of those, addresses that are one of the 18 verified mirrors | 15 (37 loads) |
| distinct normal-mirror addresses used | 17 (`0x10CE2` never loaded by this method) |

Twenty-three other `0x117xx`/`0x12xxx` addresses are consumed as helper arguments and are **not** among the eighteen mirrors — for example `0x12844` (35 loads, the most-referenced address in the whole scan), `0x128AE`/`0x129EE` (used together: a `16`-entry value table and its axis, `sub_230E8`), `0x128DE`/`0x12E18` (axes passed to `sub_230B0` alongside `0x121C4`/`0x123C4`), and `0x1293E`/`0x1295E`/`0x1297E`/`0x1298E`/`0x129AE` (axis arguments). The region is therefore a **complete table set with its own axes**, not a byte copy awaiting comparison.

Grouping by function: 23 functions reference a normal mirror, 61 reference the trust range, and two functions reference both.

## 2. Both-bank routines stage their results into different cells

`sub_69496` (18 loads) is the clearest case. All displacements are into the `FFFF2150` record:

| Load PC | Table | Bank | Helper | Store PC | `r0` displacement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0694DC`/`0694DE` | `0x128FE`/`0x12D58` | trust | `sub_2319C` | `0694EE` | `+0x6D0` |
| `069710`/`069712` | `0x128FE`/`0x12D58` | trust | `sub_2319C` | `069724` | `+0x6CE` |
| `06976C`/`069770` | `0x12C7E`/`0x12B9E` | trust | `sub_2319C` | `06977C` | `+0x726` |

`sub_68C28` (8 loads) shows the same separation: its trust pair `0x128FE`/`0x12D58` (`sub_2319C`) stores at `068D62`, `+0x730`, while its normal loads `0x10EEA`/`0x10F2A`/`0x1104A` (`sub_22FAC`) feed a different stage that stores at `0690B4`.

**Consequence:** across the two routines in which both banks appear, no routine selects between a normal and a trust copy for one output. Both are evaluated in the same pass and kept in distinct record cells. That is parallel computation, not a selector and not a comparison-in-place.

## 3. A ROM byte does gate a trust-bank path — `0x8405` bit 2

`loc_704D0` (routine `0x704D0–0x705C8`, entered as a `loc_`; the preceding `sub_703A8` ends at its `rts` `0x70486`) tests the ROM byte at `0x8405`:

- `0704D8 mov.l off_705D0,r6 ; h'8405` → `0704DC tst #2,r0` → `bf loc_704EE`.
- **bit 2 set** (`0x704EE` onward): the routine reads `FFFF34C1-0x16` byte state, then evaluates the trust bank — `070514 mov.l off_705DC… h'11744` with axes `0x1293E`/`0x1295E` into `sub_230B0` (`070538` store, `+0xB2C`), and `0x12B0E` with axis `0x1297E` into `sub_23144` (same store).
- **bit 2 clear**: `0704E0 mov.l … h'FFFF1054` → `0704E2 mov.w word_705BC,r0 ; h'73C` → `0704E6 mov.w @(r0,r6),r2` → `0704EA bra loc_705B2` with `0704EC mov.w r2,@(r0,r14)` copying `FFFF1054+0x73C` into `FFFF2150+0xB2E`. No table is read.

`sub_703A8` mirrors the same byte test on the normal side (`0703B6 … h'8405`, `0703BA tst #2`): bit 2 **set** evaluates the normal `0x104EC` 8×1 table (helper `sub_22F54`, axis `0xF932`) and stores `+0x310`; bit 2 **clear** copies `FFFF1D12+0x60` into `FFFF2150+0x30E` without reading any table.

**Consequence:** ROM byte `0x8405` bit 2 selects, for at least these output cells, "recompute from the calibration tables" versus "reuse a value computed elsewhere and staged in RAM". It is a compute/reuse switch, not a normal-vs-trust selector: the two branches write **different** displacements (`+0xB2C` vs `+0xB2E`, `+0x310` vs `+0x30E`). `0x8405` is referenced 21 times in the listing (load sites at `02BF24`, `036520`, `05B6A8`, `06518E`, `0686DC`, `06CF7C`, `06CFC2`, `06D41C`, `06E1DA`, `06E2FC`, `06EDC6`, `06F606`, `06F6B4`, `06F93A`, `06FB02`, `06FC04`, `06FD9A`, `06FFDC`, `07030A`, `0703B6`, `0704D8`); only the last two were read in this WO.

## Findings

1. The `0x11700–0x13000` region is **consumed as a full table set with its own axes** (38 distinct helper arguments, 131 loads); it is not a passive copy. "Byte-identical twin" is a data property and should not be read as "unused duplicate" in the workbook or catalogue.
2. Only 15 of the 18 verified twins appear as helper arguments by this method; three trust twins and one normal twin (`0x10CE2`) are not reached this way. Twelve of the region's most-referenced arguments are not mirrors at all (`0x12844`, `0x128AE`, `0x129EE`, `0x128DE`, `0x12E18`, `0x1293E`, `0x1295E`, `0x1297E`, `0x1298E`, `0x129AE`, `0x12A00`, `0x12B7E`).
3. Where both banks appear in one routine (`sub_69496`, `sub_68C28`), their results occupy **different** record cells — parallel evaluation, no per-output selector.
4. ROM `0x8405` bit 2 gates a **compute-vs-reuse** choice at `sub_703A8` and `loc_704D0`; the compute branch is the one that reads tables (normal in the first, trust in the second).

## Bounded negatives

- No routine in the scan compares a normal-derived and a trust-derived value in place; equality/inequality tests between the two banks were not found, so the "monitor comparison" mechanism WO65060's label implies is **not** established by this method.
- No ROM flag selecting normal-vs-trust for the same output cell was found. The only bank-adjacent gate found is `0x8405` bit 2, and it selects compute-vs-copy, not which bank.
- 501 other literal-pool table loads in `0x0C000–0x116FF` remain uncounted for role; they were only used here as the contrast class.
- `FFFF1054+0x73C`, `FFFF1D12+0x60` and the RAM cells `+0x5BC`, `+0x6CE`, `+0x6D0`, `+0x726`, `+0x730`, `+0x732`, `+0xB2C`, `+0xB2E` received no consumer trace.
- Nothing here assigns a unit, physical quantity, or calibration name; "Trustful"/"monitor" remain an inherited label, not a proven function.

## Unresolved / dependencies

1. Whether the trust-bank results are (a) monitored against the normal results by a downstream task, or (b) control values for a second operating mode, is open. The consumer cells above are the entry points.
2. `0x12844` (35 loads) is the region's dominant table and is not one of the eighteen twins; its geometry and role are unknown.
3. `0x8405` bit 2's other 19 load sites are unread; the byte's complete gate inventory is open.
4. The 501 non-mirror `0x0C000–0x116FF` table loads were not classified.
