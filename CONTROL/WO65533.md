## 2. Falsifiable hypothesis record

- **Hypothesis:** `FFFF26DA` is a genuine 0-row byte cell used as a saturating 0–4 counter at the head of the `r12=FFFF2150` routine; the increment is a read-modify-write through the same `r0` displacement and the post-reset value drives a dispatch.
- **Prediction:** exactly 2 `h'58A` rows, 5 byte accesses to one EA, 0 intervening base writes, a `cmp/ge` threshold of 5 with a reset write, no direct/`mov.l`/GBR form, and no discriminator overlap.
- **Cheap discriminator:** prove the base from ROM, account for both `h'58A` rows, enumerate the read-modify-write and reset with raw bytes, scan `mov.l @(r0,rN)` and direct literals, and test the six discriminator cells.
- **Result:** **POSITIVE for the saturating byte counter; NEGATIVE for direct absolute, GBR-scaled, longword/word forms, stale-base inheritance, alternative-base conflation, intervention, new physical identity and discriminator overlap.**
- **Information gain:** the counter is fully bounded and its neighbour offsets h'58B/C/D were re-proved under a *second* independent `FFFF2150` base (`r13` in the export routine at `0x8EF52`), exposing three further 0-row cells as WO65542.
