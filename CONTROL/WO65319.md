## Objective

Determine how much of the Juke MR16DDT calibration and control architecture is genuinely shared with EL19A's MR18DE, and identify which Juke-only features are consequences of forced induction. Produce a target-grounded crosswalk that helps locate and name MR18DE counterparts without transferring donor addresses or semantics blindly.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

- **Hypothesis:** The Juke MR16DDT and EL19A MR18DE share substantial Nissan ECU/control architecture and naturally aspirated engine-control families, while boost/charge-air/turbo control adds distinct MR16DDT-specific calibration families.
- **Identity layer advanced:** function identity; architecture correspondence where independently established.
- **Evidence:** exact EL19A ROM addresses, readers, query/axis construction, callers and consumers; exact donor A2L source/build identity, characteristic metadata and any existing cross-ROM code/structure evidence. Keep target evidence separate from donor metadata.
- **Prediction:** shared functions should show specific correspondence in naming/family, dimensions/storage, control-flow role or reusable reader/axis patterns; forced-induction functions should have explicit pressure/turbo/charge-air dependencies or dedicated control paths.
- **Cheap discriminator:** classify a small, high-value set of donor calibration families and test each against existing EL19A candidate inventory and target-side paths. Do not infer correspondence from name or geometry alone.
- **Result:** supported, weakened, rejected or unresolved per family.
- **Information gain:** separates reusable MR18DE calibration families from turbo-only families and prevents both missed counterparts and false donor transfers.

## Scope

1. Establish the exact identity/build/engine context of each Juke MR16DDT A2L/source used. If the source cannot be identified confidently, mark the provenance limitation and stop using it as a specific Juke donor.
2. Build a compact family-level comparison for:
   - likely shared engine-control functions: base fueling, ignition, knock, throttle, idle, temperature compensation, airflow/load estimation, torque intervention and RPM limiting;
   - forced-induction functions: boost/charge-pressure target and limits, wastegate/turbo actuator control, compressor/charge-air temperature and pressure compensation, turbo protection and boost-related torque/airflow limits.
3. For each proposed shared family, record exact donor symbol/description/type/dimensions/storage/axes/conversion where available, then identify the strongest existing EL19A candidate(s) and target evidence anchors. State explicitly when no EL19A counterpart is currently identified.
4. For each forced-induction family, identify the evidence that makes it turbo-specific and whether it appears absent, structurally analogous, or unresolved in EL19A. Do not assume absence solely because an A2L symbol is missing.
5. Rank no more than 10 high-value crosswalk leads by expected information gain. Recommend the next target-side discriminator for each; do not perform broad new ROM scans or duplicate active worker investigations.

## Required deliverable

A concise table with donor family/symbols, source provenance, donor-only metadata, EL19A candidate/address, target-side evidence, classification (**shared-likely**, **engine-specific analogue**, **forced-induction-specific**, or **unresolved**), confidence and next discriminator. Include explicit false-match/rejection examples and the exact unresolved boundary.

## Stop conditions

Stop after the bounded family comparison and ranked shortlist. No full A2L re-extraction, declaration-order/address alignment, broad ROM/LST scan, new scripts, generated datasets, or edits to A2L, ROM/LST, XML, workbook, catalogue, indexes or evidence files. Do not repeat WO65303/65306/65309 donor metadata extraction or WO65293's generic 3D-map triage.

## Guardrails

- Target remains EL19A `f501.bin`, MR18DE; Juke donor is MR16DDT turbocharged.
- Treat the Juke as a closely related architectural donor, not an unrelated engine and not a drop-in calibration source.
- Donor symbol names, addresses, values, dimensions, axes and conversions are hypotheses/evidence about the donor only; require EL19A reader/query/consumer evidence before assigning target identity or RR definitions.
- Distinguish shared ECU software architecture from shared calibration semantics. Similar helper routines or geometry do not prove the same physical function.
- Do not classify a function as turbo-only unless its pressure/turbo/charge-air dependency or dedicated control role is established.
- No live-vehicle testing; preserve unknowns and use provisional labels where appropriate.
