## Finding — No Four-Byte Dashpot-Decay Consumer

**`0x83F0–0x83F3` is not accessed as a four-byte dashpot-decay value.** The evidence:

1. **Not a 4-byte value**: No `mov.l` reads 4 bytes from ROM 0x83F0. The region contains two independent 16-bit words (0x0080 and 0x28C8).
2. **RAM mirrors are dead stores**: sub_50F4 reads-modifies-writes FFFF83F0 and FFFF83F2 via GBR=FFFF83E4, but neither cell is ever read by any other code. sub_50F4 is called exactly once (from sub_54EA at 0x54FC).
3. **Individual ROM byte reads are isolated gates**: Byte at ROM 0x83F0 (0x00) is copied to RAM FFFF1E80+r2 once. Byte at ROM 0x83F3 (0xC8) is compared once in a gate at 0x7D0F0. Neither establishes a dashpot-decay consumer role.
4. **No first consumer**: No downstream consumer reads FFFF83F0, FFFF83F2, FFFF83F3, or any staged result derived from them.
