## Objective

Resolve the genuine 0-row GBR sibling exposed by WO65506's `h'840` clamp gate. At `0x3B0CE` the same proved `GBR=FFFF871C` routine reads `r0=U16[word_871E]` and compares it (`extu.w`) against the `h'840` table value at `0x3B0D4` to choose between `r12` and `r14` before writing `FFFF87DE`. WO65506 did not retrace this cell. Targeted checks found **6 `word_871E` LST rows** (1 low label and 5 CODE accesses) under three distinct GBR bases:

- `0x3B0CE` `C5 01` and `0x3B290` `C1 01` under `word_871E - h'871C = 0x2` (encoded `h'01`×2).
- `0x3B6DC` `C1 09` under `word_871E - h'870C = 0x12` (encoded `h'09`×2), where `0x3B6B6` loads a new GBR.
- `0x457E4` `C4 08` (`word_871E+1 - h'8717 = 0x8`) and `0x4586A` `C4 07` (`word_871E - h'8717 = 0x7`), byte GBR offsets unscaled, under `0x457AA`'s GBR load.

All three bases must be proved independently; no base may be inherited from `GBR=FFFF871C`. Test whether this cell reaches a consequential compare, state export, previously dispositioned cell or one of the six discriminator cells.
