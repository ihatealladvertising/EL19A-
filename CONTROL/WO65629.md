## Objective and result

Determine whether the period-256 additive-delta column bias is distributed across the full EM43A payload or concentrated in one half, using two disjoint 512-KiB slices and no other periods.

**Result: GLOBAL-STABILITY HYPOTHESIS REJECTED / LOCALIZED SECOND HALF.** Both halves exceed the `+7.717e-5` IoC-excess threshold and both have positive entropy deficit and max-frequency excess, so the effect is not confined to a single half in an absolute sense. Because `0x80000` is divisible by 256, both halves keep the same column alignment; each column has `n=2048`. Per column: IoC `= Σ_x n_x(n_x−1)/(n(n−1))` against uniform `1/256`; entropy in bits/byte against uniform `8`; max-frequency share `max(n_x)/n` against uniform `1/256`. WO65617's whole-payload p256 row was carried as a reference only; the full-payload screen and other periods were not recomputed.

The p256 bias is therefore present in both halves but strongly asymmetric. The second half carries roughly 3.76× the first half's IoC excess and also leads on entropy deficit and max-frequency excess, so the `2×` rule classifies the effect as **localized** rather than globally stable. This is a bounded statistical localization only; it does not identify which bytes or sub-slices drive the excess and does not distinguish encryption, compression, transport padding or another file-format mechanism.
