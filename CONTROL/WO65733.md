## Objective

WO65499 proved the `FFFF1640` clamp edges are `FFFF2B6E`, `FFFF2B72`, `FFFF2B7C` — fields `A1E`/`A22`/`A2C` of `unk_FFFF2150` — and that no fixed-displacement store to any of `A1C`–`A54` exists anywhere in the ROM. Its remaining boundary is therefore precise: **how does that band acquire its values?** Resolve whether the band is copied from ROM at initialization (which would make it a calibration mirror after all) or computed in firmware (which would confirm pure RAM state).

## Mandatory falsifiable hypothesis

- **Identity layer advanced:** input identity (origin of the `FFFF1640` bounds).
- **Hypothesis:** the `A1C`–`A54` band is populated by a ROM→RAM copy at startup, so the values are calibration-mirrored even though they are unaddressable by displacement.
- **Evidence:** WO65499 §3/§4 (band addresses, zero fixed-displacement writers, in-place commit at `0x6E15E`); WO65496 §3 (the ROM does use whole-block copies, e.g. `copy_longwords_by_size` at `0x20EF0`).
- **Prediction:** a routine will compute the destination `FFFF2150` (or an address inside it) and pass it to a copy/fill helper with a ROM source pointer; the copied span will cover offset `0xA1C`–`0xA54` (absolute `FFFF2B6C`–`FFFF2BA4`).
- **Cheap discriminator:** destination-first search — find instructions that materialise `FFFF2150` or `FFFF2B6C`–`FFFF2BA4` as a pointer and are followed by a call to a copy/fill helper, then read that helper's arguments only.
- **Information gain:** converts WO65499's "unknown initializer" into either a ROM provenance (the bounds become nameable calibration, gaining a definition entry) or a firmware-computed origin (the bounds stay opaque, and the `[EXP]` row becomes permanent).

## Scope

1. Enumerate the reference sites that materialise `unk_FFFF2150` (the LST annotation `; h'FFFF2150` currently has ~1600 rows; report the count and filter to those that feed a copy/fill/memset helper, i.e. a `jsr`/`bsr` within a short window whose callee is a bulk-copy style routine).
2. Decode at most three of those call sites: destination register, source register/ROM pointer, length, and the resulting byte span.
3. State whether any decoded span covers `FFFF2B6C`–`FFFF2BA4`. If a ROM source is found, record the ROM address and read at most six ROM words there.
4. Report whether the band is (a) ROM-mirrored, (b) firmware-computed, or (c) still unresolved, with separate confidence for the span and the values.
5. Stop at an indirect dispatcher (a `jmp @rN` whose target is not statically resolved), an ADC/CAN/peripheral read, or after three decoded call sites.

## Required deliverable

- Reference-site count and the filtered call-site list.
- Up to three decoded copy/fill call sites (PC, destination, source, length, span).
- Verdict with confidence, and the resulting RR disposition for `FFFF1640` and its band.
- Exact unresolved boundary, reused evidence, no-repeat list.

## Stop conditions / exclusions

Stop after three decoded call sites. No whole-ROM RAM graph, no new index, no re-derivation of WO65499's clamp table or of WO65487's dual-writer/liveness proof, no `0x7800–0x9B00` sweep, no physical-unit claim for `FFFF1640`.
