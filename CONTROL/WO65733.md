## 1. Question and result

WO65499 proved the `FFFF1640` clamp edges are `FFFF2B6E`, `FFFF2B72`, `FFFF2B7C` (fields `A1E`, `A22`, `A2C` of `unk_FFFF2150`) and concluded no writer exists anywhere in the ROM. This WO asked whether the `A1C`–`A54` band is populated by a startup ROM→RAM copy, which would make the bounds calibration-mirrored.

**The copy hypothesis is rejected, and the band does have writers.** Three initializer clusters write it explicitly, and the writes are indexed (`mov.w rN, @(r0,rM)`) with the field offset held in a 16-bit pool word — which is exactly why a fixed-displacement sweep reports zero writers.

## 2. Why a block copy cannot be the mechanism (two independent negatives)

1. **There is no post-increment store in the entire ROM.** `grep` over all 509,428 listing lines for `mov.b/.w/.l rN, @rM+` returns **0** matches; all 51 `@rM+` operands in the ROM are loads (`mov.b @r4+, r2` …) or `mac` operands at `0xAB368`–`0xAB41A`. A classic copy loop therefore does not exist.
2. **The only whole-block copier is called once, and not with this destination.** `copy_longwords_by_size` (`0x21F3C`) is referenced exactly three times in the listing: its pool word at `0x20EA0`, its single call site `0x20EF0` (destination `FFFF84D4`, source `FFFF8534`, length `0x60` — WO65496), and its own label. Its body is a jump-table Duff's device (`0x21F44` `mov.l @(r0,r3),r3` / `jmp @r3`, table at `0x21F98`) whose stores are fixed displacements from the caller's destination base, maximum `@(h'3C,r1)` — so even a hypothetical call with base `FFFF2150` could reach only `+0x3C`.

No ROM literal anywhere encodes an address in `FFFF2B6C`–`FFFF2BA4`: the only occurrences of those addresses in the whole listing are the six `UNDEFINED .res.b` lines (2B6C, 2B6E, 2B72, 2B7C, 2B82, 2BA4).

## 3. The three writer clusters (decoded)

Every band offset is materialised the same way — a 16-bit pool word loaded into `r0` — so the set of sites is small and fully enumerable (`h'A1C` 6 lines, `h'A1E` 8, `h'A22` 8, `h'A54` 8, `h'A50` 7, `h'A2C` 13): each offset appears only in the routines below.

### Cluster 2 — `0x6D4A0`–`0x6D4B0` (base statically proven, publishes the bound)

```
0006D492  D6 8A  mov.l off_6D6BC, r6 ; h'FFFF2150
0006D494  90 89  mov.w word_6D5AA, r0 ; h'A52
0006D496  02 6D  mov.w @(r0,r6), r2        <-- read current FFFF2BA2

0006D4A0  D6 86  mov.l off_6D6BC, r6 ; h'FFFF2150   (pool word = FF FF 21 50, verified in ROM at 0x6D6BC)
0006D4A2  90 83  mov.w word_6D5AC, r0 ; h'A1E
0006D4A4  06 D5  mov.w r13, @(r0,r6)                 -> FFFF2B6E
0006D4A6  90 82  mov.w word_6D5AE, r0 ; h'A22
0006D4A8  06 45  mov.w r4, @(r0,r6)                  -> FFFF2B72
0006D4AA  90 81  mov.w word_6D5B0, r0 ; h'A1C
0006D4AC  06 E5  mov.w r14, @(r0,r6)                 -> FFFF2B6C
```

This is the routine that **sets the clamp bounds**: it reads `FFFF2BA2`, raises `r4` to at least that value (`cmp/gt`), then stores the four edge words `A1E`/`A22`/`A1C`/`A20` and returns (`rts` at `0x6D4B8`). It is a leaf (registers restored from the stack at `0x6D4B2`–`0x6D4BA`).

### Cluster 3 — `sub_67D0C` (base statically proven, saturation default)

```
sub_67D0C:
00067D0C  D6 1D  mov.l off_67D84, r6 ; h'90CC     <-- ROM word source
00067D0E  D4 1E  mov.l off_67D88, r4 ; h'FFFF2150 <-- base
00067D10  62 61  mov.w @r6, r2                    <-- r2 = U16[0x90CC]
```
then sixteen stores of `r2` at offsets `A3C`, `A3E`, `A3A`, `A5E`, `A48`, `A5A`, `A56`, `A4A`, `A4C`, `A4E`, `A50`, `A2E`, `A40`, `A42`, `A52`, `A58` (`0x67D12`–`0x67D60`), plus `mov.b r6, @(h'11,FFFF1EBD)`.

**The ROM word at `0x90CC` is `7F FF` = 32767 = `0x7FFF`** (verified byte-exact in `EVIDENCE/VERSA f501.bin`; the surrounding bytes read `… 00 00 7F FF 7F FF`). `sub_67D0C` is therefore a **reset-to-maximum / no-limit default**: it stamps positive full scale into the limit fields, including `A50`/`A52`/`A54`. It is called once (one `jsr` reference resolves to `sub_67D0C`).

## 5. RR consequence — this is a correction to a closed WO

- **`FFFF1640` and its band must be treated as initialised RAM state with a `0x7FFF` no-limit default**, overwritten by a bound publisher at `0x6D4A0`. It remains **opaque**: no calibration definition, no name, no unit — the values are code-defined, not calibration bytes.
- **Correction to WO65499 (additive, recorded here; that report is not edited):** its §4 statement of *"zero fixed-displacement stores for `A1C`–`A54` … → writable RAM state, not calibration"* is **correct only for fixed-displacement stores**. The band is in fact written by three indexed-store clusters. Its *conclusion* (writable RAM state, not calibration) is unchanged and now positively proven rather than inferred from absence; its *evidence* was incomplete and the earlier reading of "no initializer" must not be reused.
- Methodological consequence for the lane: any future "zero writers" claim must screen the indexed form `mov.* rN, @(r0,rM)` with 8/16-bit pool offsets, not only `@(h'XXX,rM)`. A displacement-only census under-reports writers throughout this ROM.
