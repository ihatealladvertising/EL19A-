## Confirmed / strong findings

### 1. Scheduler context
- Nine-entry task table at `0x21A64`: `34464, 4D02C, 3C45E, 59CAE, 47276, 5F81E, 500EC, 951E0, C29A2`.
- `sub_2088C` iterates all nine entries per invocation (`0x20984–0x20996`).
- It is called from ISR-labelled entries at `0x268E0` (`INT_ATU02_ICI0A`) and `0x276A8` (`INT_ATU12_IMI1AE`). These labels are listing labels; ISR entry conditions and fixed cadence were not audited.
- Within the task pass, `0x59CAE` precedes `0x47276`; the latter wrapper calls `0x46BC8`, `0x46E3E`, and `0x470CC` in sequence. Treat the first two wrapper links as STRONG per the report, not as a new independent re-verification.

### 2. FFFF85DF consumers — correction to prior negative
- `0x46BDE`: byte read via `mov.b @(r0,r12)`, with `r12=FFFF85C4`, `r0=0x1B`, resolving to `FFFF85DF`.
- `0x47014`: byte read via `mov.b @(r0,r4)`, with `r4=FFFF85C4`, `r0=0x1B`, also resolving to `FFFF85DF`.
- Both are instruction-level consumers. This **supersedes the earlier bounded-negative conclusion** associated with WO64828/WO64847: WO64847's literal/GBR-relative search did not cover these base-register-plus-index forms.
- Do not infer that the same result applies to `FFFF85E0`; it is the next byte and requires separate evidence.

### 3. Downstream dataflow and hardware boundary
- At `0x46BDE–0x46C06`, signed `FFFF85DF` contributes to a calculation stored at `FFFF1994`; no reader of `FFFF1994` was found in the reported scope.
- At `0x46CAE–0x46DE2`, the signed byte contributes to the bounded correction path stored at `FFFF1088` (`FFFF1054+0x34`). The path is confirmed; exact clamp algebra remains provisional because it was read but not fully tabulated.
- At `0x47010–0x470B6`, the second consumer combines signed `FFFF85DF`, `FFFF1CA8` data and ROM-selected offsets, with a cap based on ROM `0x841D`; result is stored at `FFFF1992`. Report confidence: STRONG.
- At `0x470F8–0x47180`, a derived argument is stored at `FFFF85CB` and passed to `sub_27720`; a `FFFF3632.bit3` override can bypass the `FFFF1992`-derived argument.
- `sub_27720` calls `sub_25D36`, which splits the argument into quotient/remainder by 10 and stores them at `FFFF126A` / `FFFF126C`. The subsequent `FFFF139C` multiply/divide calculation is reported, but helper `sub_21BE4` was not fully re-verified; retain that arithmetic implementation as qualified.
- The routine writes `FFFFF5C2` (`ATUII_GR11A_W`) with `FFFF1290+FFFF139C`, or `TCNT11+3` on the overdue path. It also writes `FFFFF5C4` with `TCNT11+2` and clears flags at `FFFFF5CA`.
- **Conclusion:** the `FFFF85DF`-derived path reaches an ATU-II channel 11 compare-register write, subject to the described gates. This is a timer-register boundary only. A physical pin, actuator, or external load is **not proven**.
- The reported `sub_2598E` Port-L function-select observation (WO64794) was not independently re-verified in this investigation; keep pin routing unresolved.
- `sub_27720` also performs word read-modify-write operations to `FFFFF700` (`APC_POPCRE_B`) at `0x27892–0x278A4` and `0x278FA–0x27906`. Their configuration and hardware meaning remain provisional/unresolved.
