## Findings — 2026-11-17
- **Caller-label correction:** call PC `0x4FA02` is inside entry `loc_4F8AC` (prologue `0x4F8AC`, epilogue `0x4F884`), not `sub_4F6DE`. XREF index shows callback/table pointer at ROM `0x21928` targets `0x4F8AC`. The V32 candidate register's caller label was stale/misassigned; use `loc_4F8AC` for this callsite.
- R4 is loaded with `E510` at `0x4F9F6`; R14 already holds `sub_22E10` from `0x4F8C6`.
- Query provenance: R13 is set to `FFFF84D4` at `0x4F8E6`; `mov.w @(0x44,r13),r2` at `0x4F9F8` reads UWORD `FFFF8518`. It is zero-extended at `0x4F9FE`, shifted right 8 at `0x4FA00`, and passed as UBYTE R5 in the `jsr` delay slot `0x4FA04). Thus query = high byte of UWORD `FFFF8518`; no caller-side clamp to `0xEF`.
- `sub_22E10` accepts query `0x00..0xEF`; query `>=0xF0` follows its helper rejection path. Do not assume caller guarantees in-range input.
- Result is UBYTE; staged at stack `[SP+4]` (`0x4FA08`) and RAM `FFFF1054+0x476 = FFFF14CA` (`0x4FA14`).
- Immediate use: result is multiplied by a scaled product formed from UWORDs `FFFF1054+0x2CC` and `+0x2FA`; product is passed through `sub_22180` (`0x4FA1A–0x4FA30`). The returned value is then combined with caller-held bytes/coefficients and UWORD `FFFF9796`; bounded outputs are written to `FFFF1D12+0x46`, `FFFF1054+0x54E`, and `FFFF1054+0x550), with subsequent helper processing at `sub_543CC` (`0x4FA80–0x4FB28`). This is a local multi-input arithmetic path; no physical meaning assigned.
- **Confidence:** high for pointer, query source/width/shift, result staging and immediate arithmetic chain; physical function/units unresolved.
