## Objective

Resolve the source and software representation of the two UWORD values `FFFF9866` and `FFFF9868` used as the dynamic bounds in the F3D6 lookup result clamp.

The known F3D6 routine sets GBR to `FFFF9862`; the two fields occupy offsets +4 and +6. The interpolated result is multiplied by 13 and bounded against four times these UWORD values before being staged at `FFFF2BD8` and later exposed as `FFFF33EA`.

The task is limited to the two bound fields. Establish whether they are initialized constants, periodically updated state, copied configuration, or values supplied through a computed/transport path. Do not infer physical units or a named calibration from the ×4 operation.
