## Confirmed

- `sub_2245C` contract at `0x2245C–0x22492`: R2 is source, R1 is destination, R0 is byte count. The helper copies forward from R2 to R1.
- At `0x20FBA–0x20FBE`: R0=3, R2=`FFFF8602`, R1=`FFFF85FF`; therefore the copy is **FFFF8602..FFFF8604 → FFFF85FF..FFFF8601**.
- In particular, source byte `FFFF8603) becomes destination byte `FFFF8600). The QDSPT gate at `0x40494–0x40498` tests bit `0x40` of that mirrored byte.
- The preceding call at `0x20FB4–0x20FB6` has R2=`FFFF85FF` and R1 retained as `FFFF8605`; its count is not established by this isolated slice and is not used to infer the three-byte copy direction.

## Correction / decision

WO64876's sentence stating that the three-byte copy runs from `FFFF85FF` to `FFFF8602` is reversed. Correct direction is `FFFF8602` → `FFFF85FF`. Treat `FFFF8603` as the upstream source byte for the copied `FFFF8600) bitfield.

This identifies a data-source relationship only; it does not establish Drive/Neutral semantics or the origin of bit `0x40`.
