## 3) Axis-scale discriminator (`20 mV/count` hypothesis)

### EL19A target evidence (reused, not re-censused)

- `0x0F406` is read by `sub_22E28` at `0x28B38`, `0x28B6E`, `0x354AE`, `0x3572C` on fixed grid `0,8,...,248` (WO65057/catalogue).
- WO65056: `sub_253C0(r4=0x1B/0x1F)` acquires 10-bit ADC via `ADCSR2_B`; raw 10-bit stored in `FFFF1798+...`; shifted `>>2` into `FFFF8588/FFFF857F`; evaluated through `0xF406` via `sub_22E28`; validity gate `3 <= shifted < 0xF2`; `FFFF84BF` is rescaled normalized temperature `[0..255]` from `[0x0A..0xAA]` with Nissan raw-minus-50°C interpretation noted by WO65056.

### Numeric comparison

| Axis | Points | Raw spacing | Physical interval declared/inferred | Range |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| V6 `mTTWN` | 32 | 1 axis point | **160 mV/point** (`COMP_0302_S`) | 0..4960 mV |
| EL19A `0x0F406` | 32 | 8 counts | **20 mV/count would imply 160 mV/step**; 8 counts/step matches exactly | 0..248 counts |

Arithmetic consistency: `8 counts * 20 mV/count = 160 mV`, and `31 steps * 160 mV = 4960 mV` matches V6 declared span exactly.

### 20 mV/count support/caveat

- **Supported by:** 10-bit ADC acquisition + `>>2` storage gives 0..255 count domain over full-scale ADC range; on a 5.0V 10-bit reference that is `5000/1024 = 4.883 mV/ADC count` and `19.531 mV` per `>>2` count (~20 mV). The observed 8-count grid step is exactly the V6 160 mV step under that common 5V 10-bit assumption.
- **Not independently proven in this bounded pass:** no SH7055/board ADC reference-voltage datasheet value or calibration constant was traced in-repo here; `20 mV/count` is therefore a **plausible/consistent inference**, not a proven hardware constant. The exact step-ratio match is strong structural/scale evidence, but the physical mV equivalence remains conditional on ADC reference.
