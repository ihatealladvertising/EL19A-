## Objective

Test whether EL19A's 32-point UBYTE NTC linearization curve at `0x0F406` is a functional counterpart of the V6 `mTTWN` coolant-temperature-normalization curve. The key proposed discriminator is the exact axis conversion: EL19A's raw grid is `0,8,...,248`; V6 `mTTWN` declares a 160 mV fixed-axis interval, which would correspond to 8 EL19A ADC counts only if the target ADC count is independently shown to represent 20 mV.
