Establish all proven initialization/update paths for this word, the provenance of direction input UBYTE `FFFF8613+1.bit0`, and the statically supportable invocation/scheduling context for `sub_69CE8` / `sub_69D1C`. ## Why high payoff

A single shared weight determines the interpolation between two byte-identical-bank lookup results (`0x11BC4` / `0x119C4`), and that result feeds a second blend. Understanding whether the weight is initialized, advanced, reversed, reset or held—and the update opportunity—clarifies the effective control behavior of this paired table set.
