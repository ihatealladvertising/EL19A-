## Finding

The claimed 41-pointer vector at `0x219C0` is **not a separate table**. It is the suffix of the already-confirmed 122-entry `sub_20DCE` table:

- Full table: `0x2187C–0x21A60`, 122 BE longwords.
- Suffix: `0x219C0–0x21A60`, 41 BE longwords.
- Offset: `0x219C0 - 0x2187C = 0x144 = 81 × 4`; suffix begins at zero-based slot 81.
- `0x21A0C = 0x219C0 + 19 × 4 = 0x2187C + 100 × 4`; this is zero-based slot 100 in the 122-entry table, not slot 19 of a distinct dispatcher.
- Slot 100 points to `0x70788`. At `0x70794–0x70796`, that routine loads literal `0xAAAE0` and calls `sub_AAAE0`.

## Conclusion

The earlier separate-41-entry-table interpretation is rejected. The target `sub_AAAE0` call is real, but it provides no evidence of a second table or separate owner. Retain only the 122-entry `sub_20DCE` structure in dispatcher counts.
