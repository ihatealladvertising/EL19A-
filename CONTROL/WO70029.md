### 3. `r4r42r24r2.bin` eight-byte discrepancy

Byte-diff against `unknown_EM62B_SH7055.bin` yields **exactly 8 differing bytes at `0x10404–0x1040B`** (`EM62B` `D2 BC 1C 62 C0 A8 0E E2` → `r4r42` `6B 3F F4 EA A7 D4 29 9A`), with 275/275 EM62B tables otherwise identical.

Those eight bytes are the stored `ck_alt2` fields (`sumt@0x10404`, `xort@0x10408`). `verify_checksums` reports `ck_alt2 = BAD` (`stored_sum 1799353578` vs `calc_sum 3535543394`; `stored_xor 2815699354` vs `calc_xor 3232239330`) and `main16 = BAD` (`stored 15499` vs `calc 44922`), while `cal16 = OK` (44478). Because the `main16` range begins at `0x10000` and therefore includes `0x10404–0x1040B`, the altered stored checksum words also change the computed `main16` sum while its stored word is unchanged.

**Conclusion:** the discrepancy is checksum metadata (two stored 32-bit `ck_alt2` words), not calibration data, and is unexplained in origin. **REPRODUCED**; no repair performed or authorized.
