### 3. Duplicate, paired-object and false-positive reconciliation

1. mMITANDA/mMITANDD remain a donor A/D pair, but EL19A's FFFF85FB.bit7 only proves set→0x0A382 and clear→0x0A342. Neither polarity is proven to mean acceleration or deceleration. Both target maps are different objects; donor payload bytes are unavailable.
2. mTDSHPTD/mTDSHPTN remain a donor Drive/Neutral pair. EL19A E678/E688 are distinct, same-payload curves selected by flags, but the timer-derived query and flag semantics do not prove D/N. Do not map donor polarity onto either EL19A branch.
3. mTTVOULM/mTTVOULMS remain separate donor main/sub characteristics. EL19A F3D6/F3DE have separate code paths, clamp regimes and staged outputs, but byte-identical curve and axis payloads. This supports a paired software path, not a proven physical main/sub distinction.
4. mMABFSFT/mMABFSFT2 are distinct donor maps. EL19A 0x0B756 varies, whereas 0x0B7D6 is a 0x8000 constant surface. Do not assume the donor bank-pair semantics are duplicated in the target.
5. mTATVO is not conflated with mTTVOULM/S merely because all have throttle-related names. The object widths, axes, query mechanics and runtime calculations differ.
6. mDDPAPO, mDDSHPTD/N and mDSHCUT remain separate hypotheses. mDDPAPO is not the target 0x848A/0x848B scalar without a target-side consumer/conversion proof. The mDSHCUT donor address 0x84C4 cannot resolve the prior EL19A XML label at 0x8550.
7. E904/E914/E934 remain distinct target lookup paths; shared interpolation helper sub_231C8 does not establish common physical meaning. Withdrawn raw-vs-converted comparisons are not reused as negative evidence.

## Coordinator scale-arithmetic erratum after WO65182 — 2026-11-22

WO65182 checked the SH7058 Rev.3 hardware specification (REJ09B0046-0300H, §17.6 p.648): when A/D is used, AVcc is specified as 5.0 V ±0.5 V, AVss=Vss, and the external AVref input must be 4.5 V to AVcc. The actual ECU AVref is not in the ROM and has not been established from board documentation or measurement.

Correct the 0x0F406 ↔ mTTWN scale comparison accordingly. For an ideal 10-bit ADC followed by the EL19A `>>2`, one staged 8-bit count is approximately AVref/256 and the 8-count table interval is AVref/32. At nominal 5.0 V this is 19.53125 mV/staged count and 156.25 mV/table step, not exactly 20 and 160. An exact 160 mV/table step corresponds to AVref=5.12 V, which is allowed by the MCU specification but not proven on this ECU.

The donor candidate remains strong structurally (32×1 UBYTE curve, two NTC input channels and compatible output encoding). The scale relation must be labelled **conditional numeric compatibility**, not an exact nominal-5-V match or verified physical calibration equivalence. Coolant-versus-IAT wiring remains unresolved. WO65182 contains the target-side AN27/AN31 path evidence and device manual pin/reference boundaries.
