## Coordinator synthesis — 2026-11-22

### Executive disposition

The V6 A2L is useful as a structured comparison source, but it does not establish EL19A physical calibration names by itself. WO65133 screened all 179 catalogue rows against the pinned V6 A2L declarations. The targeted follow-ups materially refine that screen:

- Strongest candidate: EL19A 0x0F406 ↔ V6 mTTWN. Geometry, arithmetic axis-step ratio, output encoding and dual NTC/diagnostic software context align. The 20 mV/count assumption and exact coolant-vs-IAT channel identity remain conditional.
- Strong structural/scale-compatible analogue: EL19A 0xF3D6/0xF3DE ↔ V6 mTTVOULM/mTTVOULMS. The raw values and axes are arithmetically compatible with donor conversions, but the EL19A runtime uses a different multiply/clamp path and does not execute the donor conversions.
- Plausible but weak family analogues: 0x0A342/0x0A382 ↔ mMITANDA/mMITANDD; 0x0B756/0x0B7D6 ↔ mMABFSFT/mMABFSFT2; and E678/E688 ↔ the V6 R301.26 dashpot D/N family. Each has substantial target-side disconfirmers or missing donor payload/query semantics.
- Structural-only comparison: 0x0FEE0 ↔ mTATVO. The 32-point UWORD geometry matches, but target query and output behavior do not establish a scale or physical-domain relationship.
- No V6 counterpart is established for E904/E914/E934 among the bounded donor candidates compared in WO65139. The earlier raw-vs-converted axis-range arguments were withdrawn by coordinator erratum. Do not call these semantic contradictions.

No byte-for-byte donor data or axis match is proven in this set: the pinned V6 A2L is available, but the V6 ROM payload bytes/axis bytes are unavailable. No V6 address, name, axis conversion, unit or physical interpretation is promoted into an EL19A definition.

### 3. Duplicate, paired-object and false-positive reconciliation

1. mMITANDA/mMITANDD remain a donor A/D pair, but EL19A's FFFF85FB.bit7 only proves set→0x0A382 and clear→0x0A342. Neither polarity is proven to mean acceleration or deceleration. Both target maps are different objects; donor payload bytes are unavailable.
2. mTDSHPTD/mTDSHPTN remain a donor Drive/Neutral pair. EL19A E678/E688 are distinct, same-payload curves selected by flags, but the timer-derived query and flag semantics do not prove D/N. Do not map donor polarity onto either EL19A branch.
3. mTTVOULM/mTTVOULMS remain separate donor main/sub characteristics. EL19A F3D6/F3DE have separate code paths, clamp regimes and staged outputs, but byte-identical curve and axis payloads. This supports a paired software path, not a proven physical main/sub distinction.
4. mMABFSFT/mMABFSFT2 are distinct donor maps. EL19A 0x0B756 varies, whereas 0x0B7D6 is a 0x8000 constant surface. Do not assume the donor bank-pair semantics are duplicated in the target.
5. mTATVO is not conflated with mTTVOULM/S merely because all have throttle-related names. The object widths, axes, query mechanics and runtime calculations differ.
6. mDDPAPO, mDDSHPTD/N and mDSHCUT remain separate hypotheses. mDDPAPO is not the target 0x848A/0x848B scalar without a target-side consumer/conversion proof. The mDSHCUT donor address 0x84C4 cannot resolve the prior EL19A XML label at 0x8550.
7. E904/E914/E934 remain distinct target lookup paths; shared interpolation helper sub_231C8 does not establish common physical meaning. Withdrawn raw-vs-converted comparisons are not reused as negative evidence.

### 5. Five highest-value next target-side discriminators

These are ranked by information gain, not by donor score. No new trace was performed by this synthesis.

1. **0x0B756/0x0B7D6 query-producer and consumer semantics.** Trace the two call arguments entering sub_22CF8 (call sites 0x39FD2/0x39FE2) through their first upstream producers, then resolve the FFFF86D4+0x0E result's first meaningful consumer. This is the clearest way to test whether the numerically RPM/ms-compatible axes actually represent those quantities. Keep the constant-neutral 0x0B7D6 caveat central.
2. **F3D6/F3DE source and endpoint closure.** Resolve the writer/derivation and numeric domain of FFFF85CE, then trace FFFF33EA and FFFF2C92 to the first actual consumer/actuator boundary. This can either validate a degree-like throttle-limiter family or decisively keep the donor conversion as metadata-only compatibility.
3. **0x0F406 ADC reference and channel identity discriminator.** Close the 10-bit ADC reference-voltage assumption behind the inferred 20 mV/count and identify AN27 versus AN31 physical sensor assignment from target-side evidence. Donor payload remains unavailable, so a perfect bytewise comparison cannot be claimed.
4. **E678/E688 plus 0x848A/0x848B branch and timer discriminator.** Resolve FFFF85AE producer/timebase and the meaning of FFFF85F8.bit4, FFFF8600.bit6 and FFFF860B.bit3, then follow the E678/E688 and scalar outputs to first consumers. This tests D/N and reduction-rate hypotheses without importing the donor labels.
5. **0x0A342/0x0A382 selector/query discriminator.** Trace upstream sources of FFFF860D, FFFF857A and FFFF85FB.bit7, and the first semantic consumer of FFFF189C/FFFF18C4. The key falsifier is whether query 1 remains only the observed 0/8 discrete state; if so, direct RPM-axis correspondence to mITANDNE remains weak.

Deprioritize a repeat of the E9xx donor comparison unless new target physical-domain/conversion evidence appears. Do not repeat the completed target-side payload/reader traces for WO65134–WO65140.
