## Objective

Resolve the producer and diagnostic-response lineage of UBYTE `FFFF84A4`, which WO65239 identifies as the source cell for OBD PID $43 (absolute load) and as one input to the `0x0A0F2` map query:

`q_2 = min(2 × UBYTE[FFFF84A4], 255)`.

The table/helper/output chain is already documented. This WO advances the investigation upstream: determine what writes or copies `FFFF84A4`, whether PID $43 actually serializes that same byte or a transformed value, and what the factor-of-two/clamp means strictly at the software boundary.

- **Prediction:** a bounded producer/copy path will establish the exact source, width, update conditions and any conversion before the value is consumed by the PID $43 response and map caller.
