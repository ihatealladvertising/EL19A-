## Objective

Determine whether the outputs of `0x0F712` and `0x128FE` converge on a throttle, air/load, torque, fuel, spark, diagnostic or other control calculation, or remain internal state. ## Scope

1. Root each second-stage curve result to its exact destination field(s) from WO65181; verify any disputed base/effective address against the pinned listing.
2. Identify direct readers of the result fields using the existing indexes first. Separate actual reads from writes, aliases, history copies and unresolved computed-base candidates.
3. Trace each output independently through its first meaningful calculation: arithmetic, signedness, scaling, clamp, comparison, selector, state transition or call argument.
4. Where the paths converge, identify the shared variable and show the exact contribution of each result. Where they do not converge, preserve the separation.
5. Test for a concrete endpoint only when the chain reaches a verified actuator/peripheral register, CAN transmit payload/request, diagnostic record, fuel/spark/throttle control state or similarly specific sink. Distinguish internal RAM staging from actual MMIO.
6. Stop at the first unresolved computed pointer, indirect dispatcher, bulk copy without field provenance, external transport or hardware boundary.
