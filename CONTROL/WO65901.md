## Question

`0x0296B4`-`0x0296BA` loads `off_29888` = `byte_83A8`, reads the ROM byte at `0x000083A8` into `r0`, and stores it to `FFFF36BE` (GBR `FFFF3614` + `0xAA`, established by WO65889). Is ROM `0x83A8` a catalogued calibration object with a known geometry/identity, or an internal firmware constant — and who reads the RAM copy `FFFF36BE`?

## Why it has value

`0x83A8` lies inside the `0x7800`-`0x9B00` ROM region that holds most of the identified 1D scalars, so this copy is either a calibration byte mirrored into RAM state (which the definition build must account for) or a plain constant that is already settled. Either answer removes a live ambiguity about whether a calibration byte has a runtime-active RAM mirror that a definition would have to follow.

## Exact scope

1. Read the ROM byte(s) at `0x000083A8` and say whether they sit inside a catalogued table/scalar already recorded in `CALIBRATIONS/discovered_tables_catalog.csv`, the workbook index, or `FINDINGS/INDEX.md`. Reuse those catalogues; do not re-run the `0x7800`-`0x9B00` interval census (WO65090 is complete).
2. Enumerate every reader and writer of `FFFF36BE`, resolving each site's own GBR base with the WO65865/WO65886/WO65889 method (function boundary = nearest preceding `rts`, then the routine's own `ldc rX,gbr` and its pool literal).
3. Classify: `CALIBRATION-MIRROR`, `INTERNAL CONSTANT IMAGE`, or `UNRESOLVED`, with PCs.
