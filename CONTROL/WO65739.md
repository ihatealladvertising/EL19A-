### FFFF84EA — UWORD structure member at FFFF84D4+0x16

- **Reader context:** WO65724 identifies consumers at 0x703EE (table 0x104EC), 0x70540 (0x12B0E), 0x701BA (0x12B3E) and 0x701AC (0x12B5E). In sub_703A8, r6 is loaded with FFFF84D4 at 0x703E8 and U16[FFFF84D4+0x16] is read at 0x703EA before the 0x104EC lookup. sub_70168 similarly loads r11=FFFF84D4 at 0x7019E and reads +0x16 at 0x701A6 and 0x701B2.
- **Confirmed writer:** PC 0x20EF0, routine sub_20DCE, calls copy_longwords_by_size (0x21F3C) with r1=FFFF84D4 (destination), r2=FFFF8534 (source), r0=0x60 (length). There is no local conditional branch between argument setup and the call; it executes on the path through sub_20DCE.
- **Copy mechanics:** because r0=0x60 is >=0x40, the helper takes its generic path at 0x21FDC. It sets end=r2+0x60 and copies longwords from @r2 to @r1 in 8-byte pairs, advancing both pointers by 8. The exact destination span is FFFF84D4–FFFF8533; source span is FFFF8534–FFFF8593. Thus destination U16[FFFF84EA] (offset +0x16) is copied from source U16[FFFF854A].
- **Direct displacement / GBR-relative / register-indexed writers:** 0 additional target-specific stores established. The 423 annotated FFFF84D4 base loads were checked for same-register stores; the five functions that set GBR=FFFF84D4 (sub_69C5C, sub_6BAD8, sub_839BC, sub_9652C, sub_AC6F8) read other fields but do not write +0x16. No explicit FFFF84EA literal or GBR symbol reference exists.
- **Verdict:** unresolved identity; one-hop copy from unnamed FFFF854A. FFFF854A is itself unmatched in the current crosswalk (two consumer rows), so no named/derived promotion. No source chasing beyond that hop.
