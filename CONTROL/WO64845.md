## Bounded closeout — 2026-11-16

**Technical status: SCOPE COMPLETE — SOURCE UNRESOLVED AT RAM BOUNDARY.**

### Findings
- The `0x20Fxx` routine is a multi-field RAM state-copy/snapshot sequence. It reads `FFFF860D/E` and copies those bytes into `FFFF860B/C`; it does not calculate or set bit 3.
- The generic helper `sub_2245C` copies bytes from R2 to R1 for the requested count. Its call at `0x20FE2` has R2=`FFFF861A`, R1=`FFFF8613`, count 7; that transfer does not write `FFFF860D`.
- The 44 exact-reference occurrences of `FFFF860D` were reviewed for direct pointer writes. Apparent store sequences at `0xB1EEC` and `0xB2362` are not stores to `FFFF860D`: the register previously holding that address is overwritten by `stc gbr` before the store, which targets a GBR-relative structure field.
- No direct literal-pointer writer to `FFFF860D` was established. The bounded listing pass does not exclude indirect bulk/DMA/peripheral updates or producers outside represented code.

### Disposition
`FFFF860B` is a copied/mirrored status byte in the observed path; its source `FFFF860D` producer remains unresolved. No coolant-temperature comparator, threshold scalar, X−50°C conversion, or `fDSHCUT` semantic identity was found. Keep both bytes generically named; do not promote XML semantics. No Master Workbook or XML edits.

### Stop boundary
The source is unresolved at the RAM boundary. Further progress requires a concrete initialization, interrupt, DMA, peripheral or external-data lead; no ROM-wide blind scan is justified.
