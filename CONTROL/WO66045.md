## Question

`sub_A6650` (`0x0A6650`) saturates a signed 16-bit difference of two ratios and stores it to `U16[FFFF2150+0x31C]` = **`FFFF246C`** at `0x0A671A`–`0x0A671C`. What reads `FFFF246C`, and does that reader reach a named output (MMIO, command payload, actuator/dashpot store) or a calibration object — or does the chain terminate in further internal state?

## Why it has value

WO65913 closed the `FFFF2478` question as INTERNAL STATE and stopped at this store. `FFFF246C` is the *only* sink of that whole computation: a signed, saturation-clamped quantity shaped like an error/acceleration term (two consecutive normalised deltas subtracted). If its readers feed a torque or throttle request, the lane has finally bridged from status/probability logic into the request layer; if they feed only more status bits, the entire `FFFF2150` measurement sub-band can be closed as internal. Either outcome is decisive, which is why this is the cheapest next discriminator.

## Exact scope

1. Enumerate every reader and writer of `FFFF246C` using `FFFF2150` as the base with own-base resolution for the `0x31C` displacement form (including the `mov.w word_XXXX,r0 ; h'31C` pool-literal form and any `@(h'31C,gbr)` fixed-displacement site), plus any absolute pointer literal to `FFFF246C`.
2. For the two to three readers, trace at most two consumer hops: the test/arithmetic applied and where its result is stored.
3. Classify: `NAMED OUTPUT`, `INTERNAL STATE`, `CALIBRATION QUERY INPUT`, or `UNRESOLVED`, and state which identity layer (input/function/output) the result advances.

## Stop boundary

One 16-bit cell, its access census, and at most two consumer hops. No re-trace of `U16[FFFF2478]`, `sub_9D26C` or the `FFFF34E7` bit0 handshake (all closed by WO65913/WO65892); no `FFFF2150` band-wide census; no XML or definition generation; no vehicle testing.
