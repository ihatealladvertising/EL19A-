# WO65023 — Residual ROM-Wide Indirect Call-Table Discovery

- **Status:** BOUNDED COMPLETE — CLOSED; additional candidates remain unresolved
- **Objective:** Test whether the currently known dispatcher inventory is complete by finding additional indirect function-pointer tables in EL19A.
- **Known structures excluded from duplicate counting:** six `sub_209A6` tables (8/14/7/62/6/3 entries), nine-entry `sub_2088C` table at `0x21A64`, and 122-entry `sub_20DCE` table at `0x2187C`.
- **Input used:** Annotated EL19A listing `EL19A_Complete_Disassembly_ANNOTATED_V3_NEXT6_15_V32_WO64794_TIMER11_2026-11-13(1).lst`, latest matching annotated LST available in Library during this pass; Xref Index `EL19A_Xref_Index.csv` (40,053,620 bytes; Library version last modified 2026-11-08).

## Closeout — 2026-11-17

### Bounded scan results

- Listing contains 4,620 register-indirect `JSR/JMP @Rn` instructions in total; 241 are unannotated with a resolved direct target in the instruction line.
- A bounded local-pattern pass identified 28 indirect transfers preceded within 18 listing lines by an indexed longword load `MOV.L @(R0,Rbase),Rcall`.
- Seven of these are the already-known six `sub_209A6` loops plus the nine-entry `sub_2088C` loop.
- The remaining 21 sites are additional dispatch/callback or switch-dispatch candidates:
  - Indexed function-call candidates: `0x35C4`, `0x35F8`, `0x3718`, `0x384C`, `0x3CCA`, `0x250C4`, `0x7207A`, `0xB4B3C`, `0xB4B70`, `0xB4CB6`, `0xB4DE2`, `0xB5280`.
  - Stack-sourced dynamic function-pointer calls: `0x574EA`, `0x574FE`, `0x6C6C4`, `0x6C702`, `0x6C9B4`, `0x87EB6`. These may be callback arguments or saved pointers; no table bounds were established.
  - Computed indirect jumps, not subroutine-call tables: `0x21F48`, `0x22004`, `0x22010` in copy-helper dispatch code.
- At `0x250C4`, the caller loads a pointer from ROM base `0x13AD8) indexed by a field-derived value ×4. The first three visible longwords at `0x13AD8` decode to `0x24BCC`, `0x24E50`, `0x24BD0`; the next word `0x3C8F3C87` is not a valid code pointer. Table extent/count is therefore unresolved, but this is a concrete ROM function-pointer-array lead.
- The `0x35xx/0x37xx/0x38xx/0x3Cxx` and `0xB4xx` sites fetch indexed longwords into call registers, but their table/record bases and bounds were not reconstructed in this bounded pass. Do not count them as fully confirmed tables yet.
- The remaining 213 unannotated indirect transfers were not shown by this local 18-line pattern to use indexed longword loads immediately before the call. This is not a global negative: pointers may be loaded earlier, passed through registers/stack, reached through computed aliases, or populated at runtime.
- The 122-entry `sub_20DCE` loop uses a distinct sequential pointer progression and was already confirmed in WO65018/WO65019; it is not lost by the indexed-load pattern.

### Result / scope boundary

**BOUNDED COMPLETE — CLOSED.** The audit disproves any claim that the three known dispatcher families alone constitute a proven complete call-dispatch inventory: additional indirect-call candidates exist, including a ROM pointer-array lead at `0x13AD8`. However, this scan does **not** prove how many additional complete tables exist or their exact bounds. Therefore the answer to “have we found all call tables?” remains **not yet established**.

The local-pattern screen is deliberately bounded and is not equivalent to a complete def-use trace of all 241 unannotated indirect transfers. Further work should prioritize resolving the `0x13AD8` array and the indexed callback/record families before attempting a global completeness claim.
