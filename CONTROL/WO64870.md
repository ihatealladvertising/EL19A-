## Objective
Trace the A0E2 lookup result through the complete local calculation and test whether the code implements the proposed `raw × 100/128 = raw × 0.78125` conversion. Correctly separate the intermediate fields at FFFF1206, FFFF1207 and FFFF120A.

## Confirmed
- A0E2 UBYTE result is stored at `FFFF1207` (PC `0x43F0A`).
- The routine reads UBYTE `FFFF1208` and UBYTE `FFFF11FE`; their product is multiplied by the A0E2 result, shifted right by 20 total bits, capped at `0xFF`, and stored as UBYTE `FFFF1206` at PC `0x43F38`. This is a dynamic composite product, not a direct scalar conversion.
- The same routine then uses the derived `FFFF1206` value in a further arithmetic chain with inputs including UBYTE `FFFF8507`, UWORD `FFFF11BE`, UBYTE `0x84A7`, UWORD `FFFF85B2 = FFFF859C+0x16), and ROM constants/lookup inputs. It uses `sub_21BE4` and `sub_21AC0), applies signed/unsigned bounds, and stores a UWORD at `FFFF120A` at PC `0x44026`.
- `FFFF120A) is therefore downstream of the A0E2-derived intermediate `FFFF1206`, but it is not the raw A0E2 lookup byte and must not be labelled as a direct table result.
- The code sequence contains no direct multiply-by-100 then divide-by-128 (or equivalent constant `100/128`) conversion of the A0E2 result. The visible operations are multi-input products, fixed shifts, helper divisions and saturation.
- The caller sequence at `0x467F2–0x467FA` invokes `sub_43E48` and then `sub_44054`, so the derived UWORD `FFFF120A` is consumed immediately by the next stage.

## Disposition
| Field | Supported role |

| `FFFF1207` | Raw UBYTE result from A0E2 4×4 lookup |

| `FFFF120A` | UWORD composite result derived partly from FFFF1206 and other inputs |
| `raw × 100/128` | **Not observed** in this path |
| 52.34% interpretation | **Not confirmed**; 0x43 cell alone is insufficient |
