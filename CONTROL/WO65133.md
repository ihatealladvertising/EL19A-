## Methodology

Parsed A2L `CHARACTERISTIC`, `AXIS_DESCR`, `COMPU_METHOD`, and `FUNCTION_LIST` declarations for all priority-family donor symbols and for every A2L characteristic whose name or dimensions suggested a possible match to an EL19A catalogue object. Screened all 179 EL19A discovered-tables rows by (1) record type, storage width, signedness, endian, dimensions; (2) axis count/type/points/conversion; (3) output COMPU_METHOD/units/range; (4) FUNCTION_LIST calculation group. No candidate was accepted as a verified physical calibration based on symbol name alone. At least two independent discriminators beyond table dimensions are required for a strong crosswalk. Structural, software-role, and physical-domain confidence are reported separately.
