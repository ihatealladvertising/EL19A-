## Confirmed

- The two independent UBYTE queries are `FFFF85A4 = FFFF859C+8` for the E006 axis and `FFFF849A` for E002. The second query is staged on the stack before the call; it is not derived from the first query result.

- Existing listing header notes describe `FFFF85A4` as copied from `FFFF85CC`, but the producer/source chain is not established by the bounded direct-reference evidence. The producer of `FFFF849A` also remains unresolved in prior audit notes.
- No observed runtime query values or interpolation result were available in the static listing to prove that the third cell (`0x43`) is selected. Axis values alone do not establish that selection.
