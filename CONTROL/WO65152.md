## 1. Scope

Resolve, on the **CF48D donor ROM only**, the identity, stored extent, axes, reader paths and any
bank-selector logic for the QH0 torque-conversion pair declared in the donor definition:

- `QH0/ Torque Conversion Map` — `storageaddress="0xA78C"` (X axis `0xC842`, Y axis `0xB4CA`)
- `QH0/ Torque Conversion Map Trustful` — `storageaddress="0xDBCA"` (X axis `0xDEA2`, Y axis `0xDE11`)

Decision question: **is there a demonstrable bank selector** that chooses between the normal and
trustful QH0 torque-conversion surface at run time, or are the two surfaces read independently?

## 2. Method

1. Enumerate every literal reference to `0xA78C` and `0xDBCA` in the pinned CF48D listing (pointer
   words and direct instruction operands); resolve each pointer word to its data declaration.
2. Identify the reader/interpolation helper used at each site and the axis pair supplied.
3. Determine task/scheduler membership of the enclosing functions from the donor task-pointer
   array at `0x33BC` and the dispatchers in `sub_299E` / `sub_2C38`.
4. Compare the two tables and both axis pairs byte-for-byte in `EVIDENCE/CF48D.bin`.
5. Search for any function that reads both members of the pair (candidate comparison/selector).
