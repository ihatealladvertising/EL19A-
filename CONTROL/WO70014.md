### Reciprocal direction and query provenance

The pair is bidirectional in both copies; each member is used as the value vector and as the axis depending on direction.

- Original pair: at `0x68A46`, `sub_22FAC` receives values `0x0F712`, axis `0x10F8A`, query UWORD[`FFFF33EA`]. Its result is further scaled through `sub_21AC0` and staged at UDWORD[`FFFF1580`]. This agrees with FND-000014.
- Duplicate pair: at `0x68D58`, `sub_2319C` receives values `0x128FE`, axis `0x12D58`, query UWORD[`FFFF2150+0xB42`]. Its result is stored as UWORD[`FFFF1784`] at `0x68D62`. This is the reciprocal direction of the `0x6810A` call.
- The `0x68D58` path is gated in the routine beginning at `0x68CD0`. On that branch, `FFFF2150+0xB42` is the query. An alternate branch constructs `FFFF1784` arithmetically from UWORD state at `FFFF14C8`, `FFFF1736` and `FFFF10BC`, with bounded arithmetic; therefore `FFFF1784` is an internal state/result cell, not a raw sensor identity established by this trace.
- The `0x6810A` query `FFFF1784` is also read at `0x68168` in `loc_68146`, where a correction UWORD at `FFFFA378+0xF46` is added and bounded before another `sub_2319C` call at `0x68188`. A separate call at `0x681D4` queries the same pair using UWORD[`FFFF1736`] and stores a quarter-scaled result at UWORD[`FFFF2150+0xB24`]. The state path later writes `FFFF2150+0xB42`, which is used by the reciprocal reader at `0x68D58`.
- `FFFF1784` is also consumed by `sub_68B78` (read at `0x68B8E`) in two `sub_21BE4` operations, with a resulting UWORD stored at `FFFF1054+0x720` (`FFFF1774`). Its physical interpretation remains open.

Thus the second pair performs a software-state round trip across the same two coordinate vectors: a bounded state query at `FFFF2150+0xB42` produces `FFFF1784` through the reverse lookup, and `FFFF1784` is then used by the forward lookup at `0x6810A` (and other state consumers). The round trip does not prove an external signal, unit or physical MAF input.

### Axis role, convergence and stop boundary

Both axis payloads are strictly increasing and byte-identical. In forward calls, `0x0F712` and `0x128FE` are passed as the axis arguments. In the reciprocal calls at `0x68A46` and `0x68D58`, those same payloads are passed as value vectors while `0x10F8A` / `0x12D58` are the axes. They are therefore active coordinates in the bidirectional transfer, not decorative or unreferenced arrays.

The two pairs converge only at the structural level: their raw axes and values are identical, their helpers perform the same family of 32-point UWORD search/interpolation, and both are used bidirectionally. Their runtime query and consumer paths differ. The `0x648D8` path stages an immediate same-routine result and a normalized byte result; the `0x6810A` path writes a UWORD staging result whose direct consumer was not identified, while its reciprocal output is used as a software-state query by other routines.

**Stop boundary:** the upstream physical identity of UWORD[`FFFF1064`]/UWORD[`FFFF1066`]/UWORD[`FFFF10A0`] remains as documented in FND-000014 / REC-000016; the physical source and units of UWORD[`FFFF2150+0xB42`], UWORD[`FFFF1784`] and the FFFF10BC/FFFF14C8 arithmetic inputs remain unresolved. WO65813's bounded negative for direct diagnostic/fuel-quantity identity is unchanged. No full MAF, fuel, injector, AFR or diagnostic hunt was opened.
