## Established target facts

FND-000021 / REC-000024 supersede the old 32-point interpretation:

- Base: `0x0FEE0`
- Element: unsigned UWORD, big-endian
- Minimum geometry: 64×1 (RomRaider curve presentation 1×64)
- Required extent: `0x0FEE0–0x0FF5F`, 128 bytes
- Terminal element: UWORD at `0x0FF5E–0x0FF5F`, index 63
- Reader: `sub_22E98`, called at `0x369EA`
- Caller query: UWORD[`FFFF184C`], zero-extended and multiplied by 16 before the helper
- Helper threshold: UWORD `0x3F00`
- Physical identity and exact displayed query breakpoints remain unresolved
