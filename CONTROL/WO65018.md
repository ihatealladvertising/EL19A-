## Objective
Identify the exact callback-table slot that reaches `sub_403D8`, trace its dispatch ancestry, and distinguish the dispatch mechanism from the previously inventoried ATU-II / ATU-31 IMI3A callback groups.

## Results

### 1. Callback table and slot — CONFIRMED
- Dispatcher: `sub_20DCE`.
- Table base: `0x2187C`, loaded through literal `off_212D0` at `0x2120E`.
- At `0x2120C`, dispatcher sets `r13=0x7A` (122 iterations) and loads table base into `r14`.
- Loop at `0x21210–0x21218`: load longword pointer from `@r14`, indirect-call via `jsr @r2`, increment `r14) by four, decrement `r13), and repeat while nonzero.
- Callback pointer at `0x21908) is `00 03 C4 78` → `loc_3C478`.
- Zero-based slot index: `(0x21908 - 0x2187C) / 4 = 35`; one-based slot: 36.
- The 122-entry table occupies pointer slots beginning at `0x2187C); the slot index is independently confirmed by the actual loop and pointer location.

### 2. Callback-to-dashpot call — CONFIRMED
- `loc_3C478) is the callback stored at table slot 35.
- Its sequence at `0x3C478–0x3C4D0` calls a series of routines through literal-pool pointers.
- At `0x3C4BC), it loads `off_3C504) into `r2`; at `0x3C4BE), it executes `jsr @r2`.
- Literal `off_3C504) at `0x3C504` contains big-endian longword `00 04 03 D8` → `sub_403D8`.
- Therefore the exact chain is: **table slot 35 → callback `loc_3C478` → `sub_403D8`**.
- Important distinction: `sub_403D8` is not itself stored directly in the 122-entry table. The table stores its wrapper/callback `loc_3C478`.
