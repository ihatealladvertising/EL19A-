## Objective

The EL19A calibration identity is user-verified: ROM `0x8550` is `mDSHCUT`, DSPT Prohibition Coolant Temperature, UBYTE, Celsius = raw − 50. Close the remaining target-side path around the comparison in `sub_403D8`: identify the producer/domain of query `FFFF84BA`, determine exactly what the comparison changes in `FFFF1DB4.bit5`, and trace that bit to its first meaningful consumer.

This is a bounded continuation of WO65183, not a re-identification of the calibration.

## Mandatory falsifiable hypothesis

- **Hypothesis:** `FFFF84BA` is a bounded runtime query compared against ROM threshold `0x8550`; the result controls whether `FFFF1DB4.bit5` is retained or cleared, and that bit has a traceable downstream effect on the DSPT/QDSPT control path.
- **Prediction:** existing RAM/effective-address/call indexes plus local caller context identify at least one producer of `FFFF84BA` and one consumer of `FFFF1DB4.bit5`.
- **Falsifier:** no bounded producer/consumer path is statically recoverable, or the bit is only a transient/internal state with no demonstrated control consequence.
- **Identity layer advanced:** input domain and control-effect identity.
