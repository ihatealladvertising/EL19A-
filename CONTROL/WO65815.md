## Question
Does the monotonic byte-alignment procedure used by Claude produce reliable donor-to-EL19A address correspondences in 0x8000–0x9800, and are the reported 5,644 donor-2 mappings and 842 two-donor agreements reproducible?

## Falsifiable hypothesis
The 842 reported agreements may contain useful conserved scalar correspondences, but the 45% figure may be distorted by alignment path ambiguity, traceback defects, local repeats, donor insertions/deletions or incorrect coordinate assumptions.

**Prediction:** A donor-derived scalar name is accepted only when the mapping is reproducible and the EL19A target-side reader/consumer supports the proposed identity. Donor name/address agreement alone is not functional or physical calibration proof.

## Exact scope
Reconstruct the alignment procedure from the supplied code and committed source assets. Verify its coordinate conventions, initialization, scoring, trace encoding, traceback, monotonicity, bounds and gap behavior. Recompute donor-2 alignment against EL19A using the pinned 9APCTDEM08 S19 reconstruction and compare with the reported 5,644 positions. Identify the exact donor-1 source and mapping input behind map_d1.pkl if available in repository evidence; if unavailable, state the missing artifact and do not fabricate a replacement donor-1 result. Validate known anchors including mDSHCUT donor A2L2 0x849F → EL19A 0x8550; use additional independent anchors only where prior WOs establish exact identities. Test a small set of alternative scoring/traceback settings or local-window checks to quantify mapping stability; do not optimize for a desired answer.
