## 1. Decision

The monotonic byte-alignment procedure is **not a reliable address-map method** for `0x8000–0x9800`. The reconstructed Juke/donor-2 byte DP produces 3,036–6,144 diagonal pair counts across sixteen scoring/tie-break settings, never 5,644, and it never places the independently verified `mDSHCUT` anchor at `0x8550`. The committed TSV/XML artifacts are internally consistent with the reported 842 two-donor-agreement tier and the 1,863 common-symbol denominator, but the underlying donor-1 pickle and Claude alignment script are absent, so the 842 pair-by-pair agreement set is **not independently reproducible** here. Surviving scalar correspondences remain those already established by target-side evidence; no name, unit or physical identity is newly promoted.

## 2. Semantic hypothesis record

- **Hypothesis:** the 842 reported two-donor agreements may contain conserved scalar correspondences, but the 45% figure may be distorted by alignment path ambiguity, traceback defects, local repeats, donor insertions/deletions or incorrect coordinate assumptions.
- **Evidence:** pinned A2L inventories and byte reconstructions below; committed TSV/XML tier counts; four named target anchors; sixteen reconstructed DP runs with two tie orders.
- **Prediction:** a donor-derived scalar is accepted only if the mapping is reproducible and the EL19A target-side reader/consumer supports the proposed identity. Donor name/address agreement alone is not functional or physical proof.
- **Cheap discriminator:** reconstruct a bounded Needleman–Wunsch-style byte DP from the pinned donor-2 byte sequence, vary scoring and traceback tie order, and compare pair counts plus independently anchored addresses; inspect the committed TSV tier structure without editing it.
- **Result:** **SUPPORTED** for the unreliability hypothesis; **UNRESOLVED** for the donor-1-specific 842 row provenance because `map_d1.pkl` and the original script are unavailable.
- **Information gain:** separates a reproducible input count from an unreproducible alignment result, and separates structural/address concordance from target-side function or physical identity.
- **Identity layer advanced:** structural/address confidence only. Input identity was not advanced because `donor_value` semantics are not uniform; function identity and output/physical identity were not advanced.

## 5. Reconstructed monotonic byte alignment

### 5.1 Procedure reconstructed from the recorded method

The donor-2 byte sequence was formed by sorting VALUE declarations by `(A2L address, declaration order)` and expanding each by deposit width (1/2/4 bytes), reading bytes from the reconstructed 9APCTDEM08 BIN. This yielded `N=6,300`. The target sequence was `EL19A ROM[0x8000:0x9800]`, `M=6,144`.

The reconstructed DP was:

`D[i][j] = max(D[i-1][j-1] + s(x_i,y_j), D[i-1][j] + g, D[i][j-1] + g)`

with `D[i][0]=i*g`, `D[0][j]=j*g`, match score `m`, mismatch score `x`, gap score `g`, and traceback from `D[N][M]`. The three transitions encode donor-byte/target-byte pairing, donor skip (delete) and target skip (insert). All traceback steps keep `0≤i≤N` and `0≤j≤M`, so monotonicity and bounds are guaranteed by the recurrence, not by any post-processing. Two tie orders were tested: diagonal-first (`d>=u and d>=l`) and gap-first (`u>=d and u>=l`).

## 7. Reconciliation and limitations

- WO65551's bounded `0x83EB–0x84C4` result is unchanged: 142 declarations reproduced, reported 137/86 counts not reproducible, 141/142 raw intervals effectively unconstrained, and 300-shuffle control indistinguishable from chance. This WO extends that negative at the full-window byte level and does not reopen its three target-verified `0x848A/0x848B/0x8550` correspondences.
- The reported 842/1,863 45% figure is reproducible only as a **committed artifact tally**: 842 T1 rows and 1,863 common symbols were independently counted. Pair-by-pair agreement cannot be recomputed without the donor-1 mapping input; no substitute `map_d1.pkl` was created.
- The reported 5,644 donor-2 mappings are not reproducible from the pinned S19/A2L/ROM inputs under the reconstructed scoring family. The near-bracketing settings (5,449 and 6,048 pairs) do not validate 5,644; they demonstrate sensitivity to scoring choices.
- The TSV's `donor_addr` column uses donor-1/V6 addresses while `donor_value` sometimes matches the Juke symbol byte and sometimes does not. This coordinate/provenance mixing is a concrete defect risk for any consumer that treats the TSV as a byte-exact donor map.
- T1 rows with density as low as 0.14 show that two-donor name agreement was not gated by local byte density; treating T1 as a quality tier overstates its evidence.
- WO65816 (two-donor agreement/disagreement reconciliation) and WO65817 (target-side candidate validation) are not rerun or rewritten. This report supplies their input-quality boundary only. WO65850's negative mDDPAPO disposition remains authoritative.
