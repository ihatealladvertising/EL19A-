## Decision question

Which currently unnamed or weakly named EL19A RAM symbols offer the highest semantic payoff for bounded reverse-engineering, when ranked by verified reader count, independent anchors and traceability rather than raw frequency alone?

## Required reuse

- WO65118 RAM byte/bit cross-reference and WO65121 independent audit.
- WO65116 function/GBR map, WO65117 effective-address dictionary, WO65120 callgraph/scheduler index and WO65122 independent reproduction.
- Existing Mode 01 PID map/extractor, DTC/Consult indexes and WO65173/WO65174 results when available.
- Existing candidate reports and false-alias register; do not regenerate the RAM census.

## Ranking method

1. Rank only RAM candidates supported by existing index rows. Separate direct reads from bit/byte/word/long accesses, distinct reader functions from repeated references in one function, and direct from unresolved indirect references.
2. Add independent-anchor flags: Mode 01 PID, DTC/monitor, ADC acquisition, hardware output, known calibration consumer, or donor-alignment candidate. These flags are navigation aids, not semantic proof.
3. Penalize candidates with ambiguous aliases, broad shared status bytes, initialization-only access, unresolved pointer bases or weakly supported counts.
4. Produce a top-30 shortlist with score components visible; include a small negative/control sample to show why high raw counts can mislead.
5. For each top candidate, point to the exact existing index/report evidence needed to begin a trace. Do not infer physical identity from reader count.

## Required deliverables

- Ranked top-30 RAM shortlist with address, access widths, unique reader functions, writer count, indirect-access caveat, independent anchors and score components.
- Scoring formula and explicit tie-break rules.
- Top-10 dossier pointers and one recommended next trace per candidate.
- Negative/control examples and limitations of the existing indexes.

## Stop conditions / budget

- Reuse existing indexes only; no broad LST scan or new census.
- Maximum 30 ranked candidates and 5 negative/control examples.
- If the indexes cannot reliably distinguish unique readers from repeated references, report that limitation and stop rather than inventing a score.

## Acceptance criteria

The ranking must be reproducible from existing indexed evidence and must not equate reader count with semantic importance. No RAM symbol is renamed or physically identified by this task.
