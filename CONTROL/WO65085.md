## Findings

### 1. Gate path in `sub_6F69E`

- `0x6F6B4` loads ROM pointer `0x8405`; `0x6F6B6` reads a byte; `0x6F6B8` tests mask `0x80`; `bt 0x6F6BA` selects fallback when bit 7 is clear.
- **Bit 7 set:** `0x6F6BC` loads pointer `0x965C`; `0x6F6C0` is the `bra` delay slot and executes `mov.w @r6,r2`. ROM bytes `0x965C–0x965D = 80 00`; the read is UWORD `0x8000`.
- **Bit 7 clear:** `0x6F6C2–0x6F6C8` establishes base `R11=FFFF2150`, offset `0x312`, then reads UBYTE at effective address **`FFFF2150+0x312 = FFFF2462`**. `0x6F6CC` zero-extends it and `0x6F6D0` shifts it left 8; the value is passed on the stack to `sub_22CF8` at `0x6F6DC`. The helper parameters include ROM pointers `0xFE10` / `0xC3D6` and UWORD query `[FFFF84D4+0x16] = [FFFF84EA]`. After return, R0 is copied to R2.
- Both branches merge at `0x6F6E2`; `0x6F6E8` stores the selected/computed UWORD to **`FFFF2150+0x796 = FFFF28A6`**. `0x6F79C` reads that field back; the value then participates in a multiply/add chain with a total right shift of 15 bits before further processing.
- Final UWORD result is stored at **`FFFF1000`** by `0x6F884`.
- Caller path: `loc_6ECF0` calls `sub_6F89A` at `0x6ECF6`; after return it jumps to `loc_66DF6`. That routine reads UWORD `[FFFF1000]` at `0x66E16`, zero-extends it into R7 at `0x66E1C`, and passes it to **`sub_22D30`** at `0x66E26`. This is the first bounded direct downstream consumer.

### 2. Gate path in `sub_6F8EC`

- `0x6F93A–0x6F940` loads and tests `0x8405.bit7`; `bt 0x6F942` selects fallback when bit 7 is clear.
- **Bit 7 set:** `0x6F944` loads pointer `0x1171E`; `0x6F94A` is the `bra` delay slot and executes `mov.w @r6,r5`. ROM bytes `0x1171E–0x1171F = 80 00`; UWORD `0x8000`.
- **Bit 7 clear:** `0x6F94C–0x6F952` reads UBYTE from the same effective address **`FFFF2150+0x312 = FFFF2462`**. `0x6F956` zero-extends and `0x6F95A` shifts it left 8; the local value is passed to `sub_23078` at `0x6F966`. Parameters include ROM pointers `0x129CE+0x10 = 0x129DE` and `0x11944`, plus the same UWORD query `[FFFF84D4+0x16] = [FFFF84EA]`. After return, R0 is copied to R5.
- Both branches merge at `0x6F96C`; `0x6F970` stores UWORD R5 to **`FFFF2150+0xB3A = FFFF2C8A`**.
- The subsequent calculation explicitly forms `0x8000-R5` at `0x6F974`, multiplies the complementary and selected weights by R13/R14 at `0x6F976–0x6F97E`, combines them, shifts right 15 (`0x6F992–0x6F99A)), and clamps to UWORD range. Thus R5 acts as a Q15 blend weight. With bit 7 set, R5=`0x8000`, the complementary term is zero and the R14 term is selected. This is a structural arithmetic role only; physical calibration semantics remain unknown.
- Routine also stages UWORDs at `FFFF2150+0xB26 = FFFF2C76` (`0x6F9AC`) and `FFFF2150+0xB3E = FFFF2C8E` (`0x6F9BA`). A later `sub_21BE4` result is stored at **`FFFF1000+0x26 = FFFF1026`** by `0x6FA6C`.
- Caller path: `loc_6ED74` calls `sub_6F8EC` at `0x6ED7A`; after return it jumps to `loc_67D94`. That routine reads UWORD `[FFFF1000+0x26]` at `0x67DBC`, zero-extends it to R7 at `0x67DC2`, and passes it to **`sub_230B0`** at `0x67DCA`. This is the first bounded direct downstream consumer.

### 3. ROM object / geometry at `0x1171E`

- The literal at `0x6FAB0` is pointer `0x0001171E = dword_1171C+2`; the direct reader is the UWORD load at `0x6F94A`.
- `0x1171E` is the low halfword of the big-endian 32-bit value at `0x1171C`: bytes `00 00 80 00`, U32 `0x00008000`; code reads the UWORD at +2, not a U32.
- The labelled data record `dword_1171C` spans `0x1171C–0x11743` (0x28 bytes) before `dword_11744). It contains mixed independent fields rather than a demonstrated flat table: e.g. `0x11722` is UWORD `0x2D42`, read via pointers in routines at `0x6A0F4–0x6A100` and `0x6A2BC–0x6A2CC`; `0x11738` is UWORD `0x0215`, read at `0x67E2E` after pointer setup at `0x67E28`. Their distinct uses reinforce record/constant-block treatment, not table/axis geometry.
- Extent/meaning of the full record fields is not semantically resolved. The supported statement is a single UWORD field at `0x1171E`, equal to `0x8000`, within a mixed constant-data block.

### 4. Cross-path comparison and consumer result

- Both routines test the same ROM option byte `0x8405.bit7`.
- Both bit-clear fallback branches read the **same UBYTE** `FFFF2462` and the **same UWORD query** `FFFF84EA`, shift the byte-derived value left 8, but use different helper/table parameter sets: `sub_22CF8` with `0xFE10/0xC3D6` versus `sub_23078` with `0x129DE/0x11944`.
- Both bit-set branches read UWORD `0x8000`: `0x965C` and `0x1171E`.
- They do **not** converge on one shared direct downstream consumer within the traced paths. `sub_6F69E` stages `FFFF1000`, then caller path `loc_66DF6` passes it to `sub_22D30`; `sub_6F8EC` stages `FFFF1026`, then caller path `loc_67D94` passes it to `sub_230B0`. Their intermediate `FFFF28A6` and `FFFF2C8A` cells are distinct.
- The two alternatives **within each routine** do converge on that routine's local calculation/store. A narrow fixed-offset literal census found `0x796` written/read within `sub_6F69E` and separately written at `0x29C4C`; `0xB3A` is written at `0x6F970` and separately at `0x29C82`. No additional direct read of `FFFF2150+0xB3A` was identified by this exact-literal search. This is not an exhaustive scan of indirect/indexed readers or block copies.
