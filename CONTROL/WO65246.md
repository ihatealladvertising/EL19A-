## Objective

Resolve the untraced whole-byte write candidate at PC `0x63CEE` in `sub_5FA70` against the proven hysteresis update of `FFFF1D81.bit5` in `sub_4E24C`. This byte controls a meaningful downstream query choice: when bit5 is set, the `0xAF72/0xADF2` path can force its second map query to `0xFF), selecting the last column.

The question is whether the whole-byte writer preserves, initializes, replaces or can clear the hysteresis bit, and under what path conditions.

## Mandatory falsifiable hypothesis record

- **Identity layer advanced:** state/output identity and branch-control provenance.
- **Hypothesis:** the whole-byte store at `0x63CEE` may write the same exact byte `FFFF1D81` and overwrite the independently maintained bit5 latch; the store's source and path determine whether the later `0xAF72/0xADF2` query selector is hysteresis-controlled or externally refreshed.
- **Evidence:** WO65189 proves `sub_4E24C` sets/clears `FFFF1D81.bit5` from the `E810(FFFF85A9)` result with an +8 hold band; WO65189 also identifies a whole-byte store in `sub_5FA70` at `0x63CEE` whose effective address/source was not traced. The bit5 consumer at `0xAE8E0` forces query2 to `0xFF` when ROM `0x86CC.bit0` is set.
- **Prediction:** instruction-level EA/base verification will either confirm the whole-byte store targets `FFFF1D81` and reveal its source/guard, or disprove the candidate as a same-offset/different-base access.
- **Cheap discriminator:** verify the single store PC `0x63CEE` against its base register/GBR and trace only the reaching value and immediate caller/guard.
- **Falsifier:** the store does not address `FFFF1D81`, or its proven value is a bit-preserving reconstruction that cannot alter bit5.
- **Information gain:** determine whether the established hysteresis state can be overwritten, when its branch effect is active, and whether the map's forced-last-column condition is stable or refreshed by a separate path.
- **Result:** supported, weakened, rejected or unresolved; no physical map meaning is inferred.
