## Coordinator erratum — FFFF2BB8 producer and FFFF8A54 width (2026-11-22)

The original §1 conclusion “no direct store to FFFF2150+0xA68 found” is incorrect. It missed three direct stores in the same sub_55F66 routine that later reads the threshold at 0x56086. This producer evidence was already present in the WO65130 listing trace and must be reused.

### Direct writer path in sub_55F66

GBR in sub_55F66 is FFFF8A40, established at 0x55F7A/0x55F7C. The effective address for each store is base FFFF2150 plus word offset 0x0A68, yielding FFFF2BB8.

| PC | Instruction / preceding setup | Effective write | Condition / value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0x55FCE (delay slot of bra 0x55FE2) | r2=0xFFFFFFFF; r0=0x0A68; r13=FFFF2150 | UWORD[FFFF2BB8]=0xFFFF | FFFF8A46 != 0 and UWORD[FFFF8A78] == 0xFFFF |
| 0x55FD8 (delay slot of bra 0x55FE2) | r6=UWORD[FFFF8A78]+1; r0=0x0A68; r13=FFFF2150 | UWORD[FFFF2BB8]=r6 | FFFF8A46 != 0 and UWORD[FFFF8A78] <= 0xFFFE |
| 0x55FE0 | r2=0; r0=0x0A68; r3=FFFF2150 | UWORD[FFFF2BB8]=0 | FFFF8A46 == 0 |

The path is:
- 0x55FB2 loads byte[FFFF8A46]; 0x55FB4 sets compare value 1; 0x55FB6 cmp/hs and 0x55FB8 bf loc_55FDA route a zero byte to the explicit zero store at 0x55FE0.
- For nonzero FFFF8A46, 0x55FBA loads UWORD[FFFF8A78], 0x55FC0 zero-extends it, and 0x55FC2 cmp/gt compares it with 0xFFFE. If greater than 0xFFFE (the UWORD case is 0xFFFF), the delay-slot store at 0x55FCE writes 0xFFFF; otherwise 0x55FD2 increments the value and the delay-slot store at 0x55FD8 writes the incremented word.
- sub_55F66 then reaches the lookup at 0x56006; after calculating 100 x UBYTE(result), it reads the just-updated UWORD[FFFF2BB8] at 0x56086 and compares at 0x5608A.

Corrected interpretation: FFFF2BB8 has an established in-function producer. It is maintained as a zero-or-saturating-UWORD value driven by the FFFF8A46 gate and the FFFF8A78 state word, then used as the threshold operand in Caller 1's curve-result comparison. Its physical meaning and units remain unknown. Do not describe its producer as unresolved or infer that it is written only by computed pointers/block copies. Other possible writers outside this bounded path are not assessed.

### FFFF8A54 width correction

The flag result at 0x5608E is written with mov.l r0,@(0x14,gbr) while GBR=FFFF8A40; it is a 32-bit boolean store to FFFF8A54, not an 8-bit store. The separate setup store at 0x56B12 is also mov.l to the same effective address with r0=0 (sub_56AAA sets r1=0 at 0x56AB4, and mov r1,r0 at 0x56B10). The flag's later downstream reader remains unresolved within this WO's bounded search.

### Corrected cross-path disposition

- FFFF2BB8: writer and reader are both in Caller 1's sub_55F66; value construction is established as zero / increment-with-saturation / 0xFFFF according to FFFF8A46 and FFFF8A78. Units and role beyond its use as the threshold operand are unresolved.
- FFFF8A54: 32-bit boolean output of Caller 1 plus 32-bit zero initialization; downstream reader remains unresolved.
- FFFF1E8A: existing read-modify-write/self-gate findings remain unchanged. It is internal to Caller 2 and does not cross-feed Caller 1.
- The claim that FFFF2BB8 has no writer is withdrawn. The earlier unresolved-producer scorecard and limit item are superseded by this erratum.
