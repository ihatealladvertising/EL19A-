### Direct consumer 1 — PC 0xB21D6 (sub_B203E)
- `sub_B203E` establishes GBR=`FFFF2F00`.
- PC `0xB21D6` loads UBYTE `FFFF2150+0xAA6 = FFFF2BF6`.
- That byte is stored at GBR+`0x17F+6 = FFFF3085` (PC `0xB21D8–0xB21DC`), forming part of an 8-byte status/record structure based at `FFFF307F).
- The routine also derives three signed, clamped values through `sub_21AC0` from UWORD inputs at `FFFF1D12+0x60`, `+0x5FC`, and `+0x5E2`; those values populate adjacent record bytes and contribute to later flag qualification.
- Bytes/bits in record `FFFF3086` and related structures are set/cleared using flags from `FFFF3616`, `FFFF34C1`, `FFFF1E63`, `FFFF8CCC) and `FFFF35CB`. The routine sets `FFFF35CB+0x12.bit1` before return.
- This is an internal record/status processing endpoint. No fuel-cut command, injector/ignition control, physical sensor output or peripheral register write is shown in this bounded path.

### Direct consumer 2 — PC 0xB32AA (sub_B3258)
- The higher-level dispatcher at `0xB0730` selects `sub_B3258` when its dispatch word equals `0x3040` (branch at `0xB0734–0xB075A`).
- `sub_B3258` sets GBR=`FFFF2F00`; PC `0xB32AA` reads `FFFF2BF6` and stores it to GBR+`0x12F+2 = FFFF3031` (PC `0xB32AC–0xB32AE`).
- It then uses UWORD `FFFF1CA8+0x1E` and ROM UWORD thresholds `0x93CE` / `0x93CC` to qualify state bits in the associated `FFFF35CB) / `FFFF1E44` record context. Further gates inspect `0x8B52`, `0x8CC5`, `FFFF8602`, and `FFFF1E63`.
- Its later calculation clamps a UWORD derived from `FFFF33E4+8` against `FFFF33E4+0x36`, normalizes it against `dword_91B4` when nonzero (through `sub_21AC0`), and updates structured control/status fields based at `FFFF2F00+0x17F). It returns after setting `FFFF35CB+0x13.bit1`.
- This is again software event/qualification and internal state. No direct physical engine-speed/fuel-cut output is established. The dispatcher value `0x3040` is a control/event selector in this code; it is not proof of engine RPM or a calibration mode name.
