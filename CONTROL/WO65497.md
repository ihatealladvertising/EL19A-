## Objective

Resolve the genuine 0-row low-byte sibling exposed by WO65488's mirrored-routine check. `FFFF2150+0x608=FFFF2B58` is the partner of `FFFF2B59` in a second `0x962xx`/`0x965xx` multiply/clamp routine:

- `0x96264` loads `r0=h'608` under `r6=FFFF2150` (from `0x9625A`) and reads `U8[FFFF2B58]` at `0x96268` as a multiply input with `U8[FFFF2A70]`.
- `0x9654A` loads `r0=h'608` under `r5=FFFF2150` (from `0x96546`) and writes `#0x80` at `0x9654E` in a `bra` delay slot.
- `0x96578` reloads `r5=FFFF2150` at `0x96576` and writes `extu.b(sub_22DF8 result)` at `0x9657A`.

Test whether the endpoint reaches a consequential compare, saturating write, helper argument, previously dispositioned state cell or one of the six discriminator cells.
