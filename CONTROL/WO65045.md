## Initial fragment validation — 2026-11-17

**Reproduced against the Library LST** `EL19A_Complete_Disassembly_ANNOTATED_V3_NEXT6_16_ALL_PENDING_ANNOTATIONS.lst`:

- `0x40422` loads base `FFFF84B4`; `0x40426` reads displacement +6, so the effective candidate is exactly `FFFF84BA`.
- `0x4042C` reads `FFFF1DB4`; `0x40430` tests bit `0x20`.
- **Correction to the earlier conversational interpretation:** `bt loc_40436` at `0x40432) skips the `add #3,r5` when bit 5 is SET. The +3 adjustment occurs when bit 5 is CLEAR.
- `0x4043A` loads ROM byte `0x8550`; the supplied ROM dump reports `0x0A`, and the LST confirms zero-extension at `0x4043C`.
- `cmp/hs r2,r5` at `0x4043E` means unsigned `R5 >= R2`. When true, delayed branch `bt/s loc_40446` executes `or #0x20,r0` in its delay slot and skips the clear; when false, `and #0xDF,r0` executes. The updated byte is stored to `FFFF1DB4` at `0x4044A`.
- Therefore the observed set condition is `(old bit5 SET ? raw FFFF84BA : raw FFFF84BA + 3) >= 10`; false clears bit 5. The adjustment polarity must be preserved in all subsequent analysis. Do not infer the physical meaning of this state bit.
- The visible initialization prefix at `sub_77608` sets GBR to `FFFF34C1` and accesses other RAM/ROM objects; the supplied excerpt does not show a reference to `FFFF84BA` or establish a coolant sensor block. Treat it as an unverified lead, not coolant initialization evidence.
- The annotated LST labels `FFFF84BA` as an undefined RAM byte; this is address-map metadata, not semantic identification. The reported `ea4.py` pass returned no direct W/RMW writer rows; the 120/126 reader totals vary between runs and are not authoritative.
- The LST search confirms many references to `FFFF860B`, but those are not all bit-3 writes. The specific known `sub_403D8` operation is a **test** of bit 3, not evidence of its producer.
- The existing operational register already contains WO65045 as ACTIVE. No duplicate WO was created.

**Current evidence status:** comparison behavior is high-confidence; coolant identity, direct producer of `FFFF84BA`, behavioral meaning of `FFFF1DB4.bit5`, producer of `FFFF860B.bit3`, and any dependency between the two flags remain unresolved.
