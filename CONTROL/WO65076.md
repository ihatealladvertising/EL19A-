- The function loads selector UWORD `FFFF1D12+0x52 = FFFF1D64` at `0x6A988`, zero-extends it and tests it at `0x6A990`.
- Selector zero takes the direct `FFFF1054+0x518 = FFFF156C` path at `0x6A99A`; it does not read `FFFF156A`.
- Nonzero selector is compared with `0xFFFF` at `0x6A9AE`. If equal, `0x6A9B8` reads UWORD `FFFF1054+0x516 = FFFF156A` and branches to the common output. If interior, `0x6A9C4` reads `FFFF156C`, then `0x6A9CA` reads `FFFF156A`.
- Interior arithmetic uses `65536-selector` times the `FFFF156C` endpoint plus `selector` times the `FFFF156A` endpoint; MACL products are added, high word selected and sign-extended at `0x6A9D2–0x6A9D8`. The resulting UWORD is stored at `FFFF1054+0x524 = FFFF1578` by `0x6A9E2`.
- `sub_6A96C` is called by `sub_6A9E8` at `0x6AA6A`. This proves `FFFF156A` is the upper-selector endpoint of a second, independent 16-bit blend with `FFFF156C`; selector `FFFF1D64` chooses/interpolates between them. No physical meaning or units follow.
