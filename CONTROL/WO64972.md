## Representative target reader

### Helper contract
- `sub_22D68` searches 16 points on each axis and interpolates a 16×16 UWORD surface; data row stride is 16 UWORD values.
- R5 is passed to `sub_23494`: UBYTE axis, each breakpoint multiplied by 16 for comparison. Axis 1 values at E2C9 are not multiplied in storage.
- R6 is passed to `sub_2340C`: UWORD BE axis, raw comparison.
- Keep the axes as **R5/axis 1** and **R6/axis 2**; do not assign X/Y orientation from register order alone.
