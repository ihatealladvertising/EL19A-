### A. Strong scalar/limiter cluster: ROM `0x829A–0x829F`

Raw bytes:
- `0x829A=0x7F` (127)
- `0x829B=0x0A` (10)
- `0x829C=0xFB` (signed −5)
- `0x829D=0x1F` (signed +31)
- `0x829E=0xFB` (signed −5)
- `0x829F=0x1C` (signed +28)

These are separate one-byte operands within the mixed DATA block beginning at `0x8298`, not a UWORD array.

**Software-role evidence**

- The orchestrator at `0x4C734` executes a sequential call chain: `sub_4C752` (`0x4C736`), `sub_4CAB2` (`0x4C73A`), `sub_4CB18` (`0x4C73E`), `sub_4CD7E` (`0x4C742`), `sub_4CEB8` (`0x4C746`), then `sub_4CF88` (`0x4C74A`). JSR/BSR delay slots are NOPs in this chain.
- `sub_4CB18` reads `0x829A` through pointer `0x829A` at `0x4CCCE`, `0x4CCFA`, and `0x4CD36) context. It compares the signed-byte value 127 against UWORD state `FFFF2150+0xA86`; `0x829B=10` is sign-extended at `0x4CD36–0x4CD40` and used as another threshold on that same state. The code conditionally adjusts/stores `FFFF2150+0xA86`.
- The same routine reads `0x829C` at `0x4CC14`, sign-extends it, and shifts left four bits; this yields −80. It reads `0x829D` at `0x4CC2A`, sign-extends and shifts left four bits; this yields +496. Signed comparisons at `0x4CC20` and `0x4CC30` bound the computed value before storing UWORD `FFFF2150+0xA86` at `0x4CC3A`. Thus `0x829C/0x829D` are directly evidenced as a signed scaled clamp pair `[−80,+496]` for this state update.
- `sub_4CD7E` reads `0x829E` at `0x4CDE4` and `0x829F` at `0x4CDF2`. Both are sign-extended byte loads then shifted left four bits, giving −80 and +448. Signed lower/upper selection at `0x4CDEA–0x4CDFA` clamps the intermediate before writing UWORD `FFFFA378+0xEDE` at `0x4CE06`; its bitwise complement is stored at `FFFFA378+0x3CC` in the `0x4CE0C` delay slot. If the preceding guards fail, the routine writes zero to `+0xEDE` and `0xFFFF` to `+0x3CC`.
- `sub_4CEB8` reuses the same `0x829F` positive limit (`0x4CEEA–0x4CEF6`) and `0x829E` negative limit (`0x4CF0E–0x4CF20`) when conditionally updating `FFFFA378+0xEDE`. The update path also reads `FFFF8B72`, `FFFF858D`, and `FFFF2150+0xA86`; it is gated by `FFFF99F0.bit7` and `FFFF34C1+0x63.bit4`.
- First direct consumer in the same orchestrated sequence: `sub_4CF88` reads `FFFF2150+0xA86` and `FFFFA378+0xEDE` at `0x4CF98–0x4CF9E`, adds them, clamps to signed 16-bit range (upper `0x7FFF`, lower `0x8000)), and stores the UWORD result to `FFFF1054+0x620` in the RTS delay slot at `0x4CFBA`. The following `sub_4CFBC` compares that value with ROM UWORD `0x90E4` and writes the greater value to `FFFFB5C0` (`GBR=FFFFB4A8`, `0x4CFF6`).
- **Classification:** high confidence for a cluster of byte-valued thresholds and signed scaled clamp operands; high confidence for the stated software dataflow. Physical domain, unit, and Nissan semantic name remain unknown. Do not name this as spark, torque, fuel, idle, knock, or any other physical control without further proof.
