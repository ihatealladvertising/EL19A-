## Objective and result

Verify, at instruction level only, whether the two `bit_cleared=h'80` notes attached to reconciled `U16[FFFF8230]` word rows describe the net effect of the local instruction sequences before their stores. Do not assign physical, DTC/event or calibration meaning, and do not edit the index.

**Result: HYPOTHESIS SUPPORTED — BOTH NOTES ARE NET MISLABELS.** At both sites `AND #h'7F,r0` temporarily clears bit 7, but the immediately following `OR #h'80,r0` unconditionally sets bit 7 before the word store. No intervening instruction writes `r0`; the intervening instructions write `r5` at `0x2906` and memory via `r6` at `0x3624`. The net stored bit `0x80` is therefore **SET**, not CLEAR. The correction is additive; the WO65118 rows remain unedited.
