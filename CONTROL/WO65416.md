- **Alignment correction:** `FFFF1054+0x71C=FFFF1770`. `0x1770 mod 4 = 0`, so `FFFF1770` is 4-byte aligned; the WO65416 objective's "both cells are 2 mod 4" is wrong. - **Gate correction:** the `U8[FFFF99F4+3]=U8[FFFF99F7]` gate at `0x6B432`–`0x6B436` uses `tst #4,r0`, i.e. mask `0x04`/bit2, not bit4.

- Register-offset arithmetic: `h'71C` and `h'51A` are loaded as ROM word literals into registers and added once (`mov.w @(r0,Rn)` / `mov.w Rm,@(r0,Rn)`). SH 6-bit immediate scaling does not apply. No double-scaling is used.
