## Objective

Test whether UBYTE `FFFF84BA` is written by wider-width stores or explicit pointer-offset formation from base `FFFF84B4`. This is a separate provenance task; do not assign coolant semantics or investigate dashpot linkage here.

## Method and bounded result

- Reused the preserved annotated LST and XREF index (read-only).
- Enumerated literal `mov.w ...,Rn ; h'FFFF84B4` base loads and inspected a bounded 30-instruction window for stores through the loaded register.
- Eleven base-load windows produced apparent write-like candidates:
  `0x3C558`, `0x3DA0C`, `0x50546`, `0x5FB7A`, `0x770A6`, `0x77140`, `0x7DE3C`, `0x7DF52`, `0x97534`, `0xA5F56`, `0xA84EE`.
- All apparent stores were false positives after register redefinition. Examples:
  - `0x3C558`: R5 initially receives FFFF84B4, but is replaced by lookup result at `0x3C560`; store at `0x3C56A` targets FFFF1054+0x33D.
  - `0x50546`: R4 initially receives FFFF84B4, but is replaced with FFFF1E7C at `0x50550`; stores at `0x50556/0x50564` target FFFF1E7C+0x1F.
  - `0x770A6` and `0x77140`: base register is replaced by FFFF1D12 before the apparent indexed stores.
  - `0x97534`: base register is replaced by FFFF2150 before the UWORD store at `0x9754C`.
- The remaining candidate windows likewise overwrite the base register before their store; no confirmed UBYTE/UWORD/LONG write to FFFF84BA or an enclosing wider store was found in this bounded pattern.
- The listing independently shows read-side use of byte +6, including `0x9538A`, `0x95DCE`, `0x9633C`, and direct base+6 reads at `0x5054A`, `0x770B8`, `0x77152`. These establish consumption, not production.
- The ROM object at `0x84B4` (`dword_84B4`) is distinct from RAM base `FFFF84B4`; do not confuse literal pointer references to ROM `0x84B4` with accesses to the RAM structure.

## Conclusion

**No producer found in this bounded wider-store/base-register pass.** Confidence is high for the false-positive classifications of the eleven inspected windows; the negative result is limited to the examined literal-base patterns and 30-instruction windows.

Not excluded: stores through aliases formed outside the window, GBR-relative aliases, indexed/dynamic destination pointers, block copies, peripheral/DMA transfers, or wider stores whose base is computed indirectly. Do not claim ROM-wide absence of writers.
