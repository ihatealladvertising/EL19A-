# REC-000011 — Correct the A52/A58 initializer values and signed-minimum publisher in the FFFF2150 band

**Status:** CURRENT  
**Affected addresses/objects:** `unk_FFFF2150` initializer fields `A52` (`FFFF2BA2`) and `A58` (`FFFF2BA8`); publisher `sub_6D274` at `0x6D274–0x6D4BA`; clamp fields `A1C/A1E/A20/A22` (`FFFF2B6C`–`FFFF2B72`).

## Old interpretation

FND-000010 and REC-000010 carried forward WO65733's summary that all sixteen fields initialized by `sub_67D0C` receive `0x7FFF`, including A52, and described the publisher at `0x6D496–0x6D49E` as `r4 := max(r4, U16[FFFF2BA2])`. The earlier account left the publisher's register sources unresolved and stated that the writer status of A52 was unestablished.

## Corrected/current interpretation

WO65808 establishes the following:

- `sub_67D0C` initializes fourteen of the sixteen listed fields from ROM word `U16[0x90CC] = 0x7FFF`.
- A52 (`FFFF2BA2`) and A58 (`FFFF2BA8`) are exceptions. At `0x67D54–0x67D5A`, the routine loads immediate `0x80`, shifts it left by eight, and stores `0x8000` to A52; the RTS delay slot stores the same value to A58 at `0x67D60`.
- Therefore A52 is written by `sub_67D0C`; it is not merely read-only or uninitialized. A58 also receives `0x8000`, although it lies beyond the A1C–A54 band that is the primary subject of FND-000010.
- The publisher is `sub_6D274` (`0x6D274–0x6D4BA`). Its `0x6D496–0x6D49E` sequence implements a signed minimum selection, not a maximum. The publisher commits a branch-selected set of existing firmware-state values; it does not simply forward four named physical measurements.
- For the active `f501.bin` path, ROM bytes `U8[0x8405] = 0x0C` and `U8[0x8B0E] = 0x02` select the A2E state-selection path. The remaining selector is `U8[FFFF3628+0x0C].bit6`; both outcomes remain relevant:
  - `r13 → A1E`: `U16[FFFF2B7E]` (A2E), selected at `0x6D444` or `0x6D490`.
  - `r4 → A22`: signed minimum. With selector bit 6 set, `min(U16[FFFF2BA2], U16[FFFF2BB2])`; with bit 6 clear, the earlier r4 value is min-selected against `U16[FFFF2BA2]`.
  - `r14 → A1C`: with bit 6 clear, `U16[FFFF990E]` or zero according to the 0x50 status mask; with bit 6 set, `U16[FFFF2BB2]`.
  - `r7 → A20`: with bit 6 clear, constant `0x8000`; with bit 6 set, `U16[FFFF2BB2]`.
- `FFFF990E`, `FFFF9910`, `FFFF2B7E`, `FFFF2BA2`, and `FFFF2BB2` are not established here as physical measurements. Their further producers and meanings were outside WO65808's one-level boundary.

The earlier initialization conclusion remains intact: this band is firmware-initialized RAM, not calibration-mirrored data. The corrected details do not justify a name, unit, scaling, supply address, or RomRaider object.
