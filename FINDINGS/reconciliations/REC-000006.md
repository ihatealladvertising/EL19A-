# REC-000006 — 0x0F406 exact nominal-5-V scale claim replaced by conditional AVref compatibility

**Status:** CURRENT  
**Affected addresses/objects:** `0x0F406`, V6 donor `mTTWN` `0xDC7A`, inferred `20 mV/count` and `160 mV/point` comparison.

## Old interpretation

`CONTROL/WO65134.md` §3 and `CONTROL/WO65141.md` §1/§4 described the EL19A 8-count grid as an exact `160 mV` step under a nominal 5 V / 10-bit / `>>2` assumption (`8×20 mV=160 mV`; `31×160=4960 mV`). This was used to strengthen the `mTTWN` donor candidate. The arithmetic was meaningful but the nominal-5-V equivalence was not independently proven.

## Corrected/current interpretation

`FND-000006` retains `mTTWN` as a strong structural/function-family donor candidate but relabels the grid relation **conditional numeric compatibility**. SH7058 Rev.3 §17.6 specifies external `AVref=4.5 V..AVcc` with `AVcc=5.0 V±0.5 V`; the firmware channel setup does not select or reveal the board reference. For ideal 10-bit conversion plus `>>2`, one staged count is approximately `AVref/256` and one 8-count interval `AVref/32`. At 5.0 V the interval is `156.25 mV`, not `160 mV`; exactly `160 mV` requires `AVref=5.12 V`, which is allowed by the device range but unproven on this ECU. Physical identity remains MED-LOW and coolant-versus-IAT assignment remains unresolved.

## Reason and discriminating evidence

- `CONTROL/WO65182.md` lines 106–134 establishes external AVref, the device range, the conversion arithmetic and the lack of board-level evidence.
- `CONTROL/WO65134.md` coordinator erratum lines 125–140 and `CONTROL/WO65141.md` erratum lines 143–149 explicitly correct the earlier exact wording.
- No donor ROM payload bytes are available, so bytewise curve equality is not proven.

## Confidence

Target structure HIGH; conditional arithmetic relation MED-HIGH; exact physical mV scale LOW; donor physical identity MED-LOW and not promoted.

## Affected inventory/XML references

- `CALIBRATIONS/discovered_tables_catalog.csv` row `0x0F406`.
- No XML object is edited. A future build must not encode donor `mV`, `°C`, `mTTWN` or coolant/IAT identity as target-proven.
- The original exact-scale wording is retained as history; this reconciliation does not erase it.
