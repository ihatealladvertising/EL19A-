# REC-000017 — `FFFF A074` entry count is 198 (not 229) and the `FFFF34F5.bit5` gate polarity is inverted

**Status:** CURRENT
**Affected objects:** `FFFF A074` 3-byte status table (`FFFFA074`-`FFFFA323`); status cell `FFFF34F5` bit5; saturating counter `U16[FFFFB4A8+0x116]`
**Related finding:** `FND-000015`
**Source WO:** WO65916
**Reconciled source claims:** WO65877 ("all 198 entries … have bit3 set"); WO65904 ("687 bytes = 229 three-byte entries"; "no such populate path was located"; "bit3" attributed to byte1)

## Previous interpretation

1. **WO65877:** `FFFF34F5.bit5` is *set* at `0x08B36C`-`0x08B36E` when **all 198 entries** of the 3-byte table at `FFFF A074` have their bit3 **set** — read as an "all tests complete" style conjunction.
2. **WO65904:** the descriptor at `0x13B10` bounds the buffer as `FFFFA074`-`FFFFA323`, i.e. 687 bytes = **229** three-byte entries, which did not reconcile with the 198-entry loop; it further recorded that after `sub_24166` zero-fills the buffer every entry byte1 is `0x00`, so "bit3/bit7 are CLEAR throughout until a populate path writes entries, **and no such path was located**", and it attributed the loop's bit3 test to **byte1**.

## Reason for change

- The loop's `tst #8`/`bt` pair was read instruction by instruction rather than from the recorded prose summary; on SH-2 `tst` sets T when the AND result is zero, so `bt` guards the "clear" case and the conjunction requires clear bits.
- The 229 figure was derived from descriptor geometry (687 = 229 × 3) and never from a loop counter; the counter is a literal immediate, so it cannot be a descriptor field and cannot agree with a capacity computed from extents.
- WO65904's populate-negative was scoped to fixed-displacement and ROM-copy paths. A forward window over the 93 `mov.w word_…,rN ; h'FFFFA074` base loads exposes three index-addressed setters that never appear as a fixed displacement because the address is `base + 3*index`.

## Affected calibration interpretation

No calibration object's identity, address, storage, geometry, axes, scaling, units, confidence, evidence status or XML disposition changes. `FFFF A074` is firmware RAM with no ROM image and no catalogue entry; it remains excluded from any calibration definition. What changes is the reading of one diagnostic status latch (`FFFF34F5.bit5`) and the entry count used by anything that reasons about that table.

WO65877 and WO65904 are **not edited**; their statements remain as history and this record is the additive correction.

No catalogue, CSV, XML, workbook, A2L, ROM/LST, script, tool or protected evidence is changed.

## Confidence and stop boundary

HIGH for the count, stride, polarity, field position and the three setter routines. MEDIUM for the completeness of the populate search (indirect/descriptor-routed writers not excluded beyond the three known `0x13B10` users). UNRESOLVED: the 31-entry tail's role, byte0 semantics, and the physical meaning of each flag bit. Stop boundary: the table's shape and its flag writers; the latch's downstream readers and byte0 consumers are not traced here.
