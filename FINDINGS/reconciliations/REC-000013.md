# REC-000013 — Correct four GBR-relative address attributions and one consumer set

**Status:** CURRENT
**Affected addresses/objects:** `FFFF34E4`/`FFFF34E7`, `FFFF109E`/`FFFF8E9E`, the `FFFF355D` consumer set, and the `FFFF10FE`/`FFFF36BE`/`FFFFB552` cells. No calibration object, geometry, axis, scaling, unit or XML disposition changes.

## Old interpretations and their sources

1. **WO65865 §"GBR base resolved per site":** `0x0A19B8` reads `FFFF34E4` under GBR `FFFF34B0`, taken from `0x0A10A2`. `FFFF34E4` was then treated as a live cell with one reader.
2. **WO65838, WO65859:** the `0x0A8A2` LPF staging byte is `FFFF109E`, read at `0x063C10`/`0x06431C` and written at `0x064320`.
3. **WO65886 §Consumers:** `FFFF355D` "also appears as plain `@(h'AA,gbr)`" at `0x027274`, `0x0275F8`, `0x02761C`, `0x02769A`, `0x0276A4`, `0x0278F4`, `0x0296BA` and the `mov.w` at `0x032E8E`, i.e. up to eight further readers.
4. **WO65886 §Answer:** the `FFFF34E7` bit0 flag is "cleared at `0x0A19E8`-`0x0A19EA`", and only the `sub_A14F0` block touches the byte.

## Corrected/current interpretations

1. `0x0A19B8` is inside `sub_A14F0` (`0x0A14F0`), whose base is `FFFF34B3` (`off_A1744`), so it reads **`FFFF34B3 + 0x34 = FFFF34E7`**. `FFFF34E4` is referenced only from the `FFFF34C1`-based routine at `0x07DC8A`/`0x07DCE2`/`0x07DD02`.
2. There is no cell `FFFF109E`. The LPF function `0x05FA86` (base `FFFF8E54`) reads `ULONG[FFFF9140]` at `0x063C10`/`0x06431C` and writes **`UBYTE[FFFF8E9E]`** at `0x064320`. `FFFF8E9E` has exactly two readers, `0x062DB2` and `0x062E02`, both zero/non-zero counter gates on `FFFF8F5A`/`FFFF8F5C`. All ten WO65859 sites resolve by their own base to `FFFFB4F2`, `FFFF1DE2`, `FFFF8E9E`, `FFFF350B`, `FFFF349E` and `FFFF3672`.
3. **None** of the eight `@(h'AA,gbr)` sites is `FFFF355D`. They resolve to `FFFF10FE` (`0x027274`, `0x0275F8`, `0x02761C`, `0x02769A`, `0x0276A4`, `0x0278F4` — base `FFFF1054`), `FFFF36BE` (`0x0296BA` — base `FFFF3614`) and `FFFFB552` (`0x032E8E` — base `FFFFB4A8`). `FFFF355D` is reached only from base `FFFF34B3`, so its consumer set remains exactly the two ladders at `0x07EB14` and `0x0935C2`.
4. The `FFFF34E7` bit0 clear is the **dynamic-index** store at `0x0A19EA` (`mov #h'34,r0` at `0x0A19E8`, then `and.b #h'FE,@(r0,gbr)`); `0x0A19E8` is the `mov`, not a store. The byte has 18 access sites in total — `FFFF34C1+0x26` and `FFFF34B3+0x34` are the **same cell**, with bits 0, 3, 4, 5 and 6 used — so it is not exclusively `sub_A14F0` state.

## Reason and discriminating evidence

- The discriminating evidence is the pool literal at each routine's own `ldc`: `off_A1744` = `FF FF 34 B3`, `off_25F00`/`off_271B0`/`off_2795C` = `FF FF 10 54`, `off_29818` = `byte_FFFF3614` (`FF FF 36 14`), `word_328F0` = `h'B4A8` (sign-extended to `0xFFFFB4A8`), `off_8B014` for the `FFFF34C1` family.
- The self-consistency check: `FFFF34C1 + 0x26 = FFFF34B3 + 0x34 = FFFF34E7`, and the two displacements differ by exactly the `0x0E` difference between the bases. An unrelated second cell would not have that exact offset relationship, nor would the four `FFFF34C1`-based access sites test the same bits that the `FFFF34B3` sites set and clear.
- Cause of the original errors: a fixed line window that crossed an `rts` (instances 1, 2), and matching a raw displacement across routines without resolving each routine's base (instances 3, 4).

## Confidence

**HIGH** for every corrected address (each is read from its pool literal at the cited PC). **HIGH** for the `FFFF355D` negative over the eight named sites. **MEDIUM** for the completeness of the `FFFF34E7` site list: 695 dynamic-index sites and 13 bases in that scan could not be resolved, so the 18 sites are a proven set, not a proven-exhaustive one.

## Affected inventory/XML references

None. No calibration object, axis, scaling, unit, confidence tier or XML inclusion/exclusion changes. These are address-attribution corrections on RAM cells and on one consumer set; no affected cell carries a calibration definition or a proposed name.
