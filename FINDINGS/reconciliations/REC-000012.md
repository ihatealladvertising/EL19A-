# REC-000012 — Correct the copy-helper extent cited in WO65733

**Status:** CURRENT  
**Affected object:** `copy_longwords_by_size` at `0x21F3C`; its sole call site at `0x20EF0`, with destination base `FFFF84D4`, source base `FFFF8534`, and length `0x60`. This is an evidence/mechanics correction only; it does not change the `FFFF2150` calibration disposition.

## Old interpretation

WO65733 §2 and the corresponding wording carried into REC-000010 described the helper body as a Duff's-device copier whose stores reach at most offset `+0x3C` from the caller's destination base.

## Corrected/current interpretation

WO65739 decodes the actual call at `0x20EF0`: `r1 = FFFF84D4`, `r2 = FFFF8534`, and `r0 = 0x60`. Because the requested length is at least `0x40`, the helper takes its generic path at `0x21FDC`, copying the full `0x60)-byte span. The destination range is `FFFF84D4–FFFF8533`, so the maximum destination offset is `+0x5C`, not `+0x3C`.

This corrects the helper-extent description only. The relevant exclusion for the `unk_FFFF2150` band is unchanged: the single call site targets `FFFF84D4`, not `FFFF2150`; WO65733's independent negative finding of no post-increment stores also remains as reported. The copier-extent correction supplies no evidence of a write to `FFFF2B6C–FFFF2BA4`.

## Reason and discriminating evidence

- `CONTROL/WO65739.md` — section “FFFF84EA — UWORD structure member at FFFF84D4+0x16” gives call arguments, generic-helper branch, copy span, and source/destination mapping.
- `CONTROL/WO65739.md` “Cross-work-order reconciliation alert” explicitly identifies the discrepancy with WO65733.
- `CONTROL/WO65733.md` §2 records the prior `+0x3C` statement and the single call-site destination.

## Confidence

**HIGH** for the length-selected generic helper path and the `0x60)-byte destination span described in WO65739. The corrected extent does not affect the target-band writer conclusion.
