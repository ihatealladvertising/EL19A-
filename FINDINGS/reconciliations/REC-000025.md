# REC-000025 — Reciprocal `0x0DE6E` / `0x0E2ED` curves must be treated as an edit-coupled pair

**Status:** CURRENT (additive relationship record; existing WO64938 retained unedited)  
**Affected objects:** `0x0DE6E–0x0DE7D` and `0x0E2ED–0x0E2FC`, EL19A ROM  
**Supporting finding:** `FND-000022`  
**Source report:** WO64938 (target-side reciprocal callers and helper mechanics)  
**Confidence:** HIGH for the recorded reciprocal roles; unresolved for the scanner's alternate PC `0x05AB5A`

## Prior presentation gap

WO64938 had already identified the two 16-point UBYTE vectors and their reciprocal R4/R5 use, but the relationship was not represented in the authoritative Findings address lookup. A later outside scanner summary repeated the reciprocal claim but listed `0x05AB5A` where WO64938 lists `0x05B23A`.

## Current interpretation

The two vectors are distinct ROM objects. At caller `0x05B23A`, R4 points to E2ED data and R5 to DE6E breakpoints; at caller `0x05B26A`, R4 points to DE6E data and R5 to E2ED breakpoints. Both calls use `sub_22F28` with R6 as a caller-prepared UBYTE query. Each vector therefore supplies the other lookup's axis.

This is mutual edit-coupling: changing DE6E alters the data of one lookup and the axis of the reciprocal lookup; changing E2ED has the corresponding opposite effects. It is not a single shared-storage object, and must not be conflated with REC-000021's map/curve overlap mechanism.

## Discrepancy disposition

WO64938 is the only repository artifact available here with explicit per-call R4/R5/R6 roles and caller PCs. Its `0x05B23A` / `0x05B26A` pair remains the current recorded evidence. The outside claim `0x05AB5A` is not independently supported by the committed scanner documentation, which has no per-call records, and is left UNVERIFIED. This record does not assert that no additional caller exists.

## Future-release guidance

Any later inclusion should expose both objects as separate 16-point raw UBYTE curves and cross-reference the other vector as the respective axis, with a visible linked/edit-coupled warning. No XML, calibration catalogue, scope matrix or workbook was changed here.
