# REC-000016 — Split the forward-query operand writer disposition for FFFF1064 / FFFF1066 / FFFF10A0

**Status:** CURRENT  
**Affected objects:** UWORD RAM operands \`FFFF1064\`, \`FFFF1066\`, \`FFFF10A0\`; related pair \`0x0F712/0x10F8A\`  
**Related finding:** FND-000014  
**Source WO:** WO66047  
**Reconciled source claim:** WO65098, “Query inputs — FFFF1064 / FFFF1066 / FFFF10A0” (bounded producer result)

## Previous interpretation

WO65098 grouped all three cells together and stated that no direct code writer to \`FFFF1064\`, \`FFFF1066\` or \`FFFF10A0\` was identified, leaving all three with unresolved upstream runtime/alias/hardware provenance.

That grouped negative is too broad and must not be carried forward as a common classification.

## Corrected evidence and current disposition

| Cell | Corrected disposition | Evidence / confidence |
|---|---|---|
| \`FFFF1066\` | **COMPUTED INTERNAL UWORD RESULT** | WO65089 traces a UWORD write at \`0x68F5E\` under GBR=\`FFFF1054\`, effective address \`FFFF1066\`. The value follows a two-piece lookup / scaling path. HIGH confidence for the software producer; physical input identity remains unknown. |
| \`FFFF1064\` | **PRIOR LOOKUP RESULT — PARTIAL** | \`INDEX/catalog_179_consumer_summary.tsv\` attributes a \`0x0F712\` \`sub_22FAC\` reader at \`0x69622\` with a UWORD taint/store at \`FFFF1064\` / \`0x69630\`; the same crosswalk records later consumption by \`sub_648A6\`. The upstream query into \`0x69622\` is not fully reconstructed in the cited WO closeouts. MEDIUM / PARTIAL confidence; do not claim a physical input. |
| \`FFFF10A0\` | **ADDITIVE OPERAND — SOURCE UNRESOLVED** | WO65089 confirms its UWORD addition before the forward \`sub_22FAC\` call. WO65098's bounded producer search found no direct writer. This remains a bounded negative only; no unit or physical identity is established. |

The forward reader at \`0x648D8\` is therefore consuming two previously computed/lookup-state operands, selected by ROM \`0x87C8.bit7\`, plus a separate UWORD additive operand whose producer remains unresolved. This does not establish a raw MAF, RPM, air-mass or load input.

## Reason for change

- The \`FFFF1066\` writer is already explicitly recorded in WO65089's \`sub_68D94\` caller trace; it is not an unwritten raw input.
- The committed consumer crosswalk records a prior \`0x0F712\` call and result path to \`FFFF1064\`, contradicting an unqualified no-writer statement for that cell. Because the full pre-call producer is not reconstructed in the referenced closeouts, its confidence is deliberately lower than the \`FFFF1066\` path.
- \`FFFF10A0\` remains separate: no matching writer path was established in the bounded search.

## Affected calibration interpretation

This reconciliation supports the qualified generic transfer-pair interpretation in FND-000014. It withdraws only WO65098's blanket grouping of the three cells as equally writer-unresolved. It does not rename the table as MAF/Flow and does not establish sensor identity, RPM, air mass, engine load, physical units or an actuator endpoint.

No prior WO report is edited. No catalogue, CSV, XML, workbook, A2L, ROM/LST, script, tool or protected evidence is changed.
