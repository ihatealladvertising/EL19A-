# REC-000027 — `0x77616` is a prologue offset in a sixteen-lookup routine, and its six calls address six objects, not five

**Status:** CURRENT
**Affected objects:** the reported "six-call V6 structural match" anchored at EL19A `0x77616`; the reported target set `0x0B012`, `0x0B112`, `0x0B212`, `0x1037C`, `0x0E64C`; the donor symbols `mTMSATH`, `mTMSATA`, `mTMSATB`, `mTMFPC1`, `mTCREM`, `mTDLMFD`.
**Source evidence:** WO70011; FND-000025; WO70006 (lead); WO65319 (endpoint boundary, upheld); WO65212 (V6 A2L↔HEX caveat).

## Prior presentation / risk

Three statements in the recorded lead would misdirect a release decision:

1. **`0x77616` was presented as a "caller PC or function entry" region** to be reconstructed (`WO70011.md` §1: "Determine whether `0x77616` is a caller PC or function entry"). It is neither: it is the eighth instruction of `sub_77608`'s register-save prologue.
2. **"Six calls to five unique addresses"** was presented as the central discriminator, inviting the reading that one address serves two donor symbols (`WO70011.md` §Question). That reading is void: there are six distinct objects.
3. **A one-to-one V6 function match at `0x8D4DA` with 18 shared scalar constants** was carried as a live corroboration of a six-symbol set. The target function makes twenty ROM-byte references: sixteen indexed lookups and four scalar threshold bytes.

Because the third point would have supported attaching six donor names to EL19A objects, the risk of leaving it unrecorded is a false attribution chain, not merely a bookkeeping error.

## Corrected interpretation

1. **`0x77616` is inside `sub_77608`.** Entry `0x77608`; register saves `0x77608`–`0x77614` (`r8`–`r14`); `0x77616` is `sts.l pr,@-r15`. `gbr` is installed from `off_77894` = `0xFFFF34C1` at `0x7761C`–`0x7761E`; the frame is `-0x50`; `r8` is restored at `0x79312`. `0x77616` is therefore **not** a function boundary and **not** a call PC.

2. **The reported cluster is six calls to six distinct objects.** `0x0B212` (`0x77CAA`, `sub_22DA8`), `0x0B012` (`0x77CC8`), `0x0B112` (`0x77CE2`), `0x1037C` (`0x7804C`, `sub_22E44`), **`0x103A4`** (`0x78058`, `sub_22E44`) and `0x0E64C` (`0x78238`, `sub_22DF8`). The reported five-address list omitted `0x103A4`, which is `dword_102E4+0xC0` (`off_78278`), a separate 32-byte 16-point UWORD curve 40 bytes after `0x1037C` (`dword_102E4+0x98`). The two do not share content; there is no address reuse to reconcile.

3. **Verified geometry.** `0x0B012`/`0x0B112`/`0x0B212` are three contiguous 256-byte 16×16 UBYTE maps (27/69/47 unique values), read with rows ← `0x0E0BE` (16-entry UBYTE, 8…120) and columns ← `0x0E0E2` (16-entry UBYTE, 16…120), queries `UBYTE[FFFF85A4]` and `UWORD[FFFF8494]>>8`. `0x0B212` writes `UBYTE[FFFF2870]` unconditionally; `0x0B012` and `0x0B112` are selected by `UBYTE[FFFF35BD].bit1` and both write `UBYTE[FFFF2871]`. `0x1037C` and `0x103A4` are 16-point UWORD BE curves sharing one `extu.w` query derived from `UBYTE[FFFF84B8]`, feeding `FFFF2150+0x244` and `+0x246`. `0x0E64C` is a 16-point UBYTE curve that is **all `0x00`** on this ROM, i.e. constant-neutral.

4. **The function performs exactly sixteen lookups.** It makes 20 distinct ROM-byte references: **16 indexed lookups** (each through one of the four readers) and **4 scalar threshold bytes** (`UBYTE[0x08ED8]`=`0x00`, `UBYTE[0x08CE2]`=`0x00`, `UBYTE[0x08E37]`=`0x00`, `UBYTE[0x08F9E]`=`0xFF`) dereferenced at element `[0]` only and compared. No ROM-byte reference in the function is left unresolved.

5. **Donor pairing is falsified in part on target-side geometry.** `mTMSATH`/`mTMSATA`/`mTMSATB` are 16×16 `REC_UBYTE_C` maps and `mTMFPC1` is a 16-point `REC_UBYTE_C` curve — compatible classes. But `mTCREM` is a **4-point `REC_UWORD_C`** curve and `mTDLMFD` is an **8-point `REC_UBYTE_C`** curve, while every object in this function is 16-entry or 16×16. No 4-entry and no 8-entry table exists in the constellation, so those two symbols cannot be assigned, and the 16-point UWORD curves contradict `mTCREM`'s declared point count.

## Reason and discriminating evidence

- The prologue determination is direct: the listing's `sub_77608:` label precedes `0x77616` by seven register-save instructions, and no `mov.l`/`bsr`/`jsr` references `0x77616` as a target. A matcher address landing in a save block is a systematic hazard, not a one-off.
- The six-versus-five resolution is decided by the pool words, not by inference: `off_78270` = `00 01 03 7C` and `off_78278` = `00 01 03 A4` are both present and both loaded into `r4` immediately before separate `jsr @r13` (`sub_22E44`) calls at `0x7804C` and `0x78058` with different destinations (`0x244` and `0x246`). Two calls, two addresses, two destinations.
- The lookup/threshold split is target-side and mechanical: the 16 lookups each compute an index and call `sub_22DA8`/`sub_22DF8`/`sub_22E10`/`sub_22E44`, while the 4 thresholds use a bare `mov.b @r4,r2` (or `@r6,r2`) with no index arithmetic and no `jsr` — a shape difference the listing makes unambiguous.
- The donor falsification is target-side: the reader contracts fix the set of legal point counts (`sub_22DA8` 16×16, `sub_22DF8`/`sub_22E10` 16-entry UBYTE, `sub_22E44` 16-entry UWORD), and the ROM extents confirm every object sits in that set. A 4-point or 8-point donor curve has no place to land.

## Disposition

- `WO70006.md`'s lead table and its "highest-payoff batch" note are **not edited** (another lane's closed record); the correction is additive here and the reservation it carried — "revisit the `0x0B012` family using the reported `0x77616` ↔ `0x8D4DA` function match, without overturning WO65319's target-side endpoint boundary" — is now discharged at the structural level.
- `WO70011.md`'s reported "five unique addresses" and its "six calls/symbols and five unique target addresses is the central discriminator" framing are superseded: the discriminator is not a shared address but the two donor point counts that have no target object.
- **WO65319 is upheld and unedited:** its endpoint boundary (no degree-decode, retard or knock-window endpoint demonstrated) is not contradicted; nothing here reached a retard accumulator or a named actuator.
- No object is renamed and no donor name, unit or conversion is attached to any EL19A address.
- No catalogue, XML, scope-matrix, workbook, A2L, ROM/LST or script edit (WO70011 authorizes none, and none of the addresses appears in the catalogue, XML or scope matrix).

## Confidence

**High:** the prologue determination; the six callsites with all registers; the six objects with extents, storage and geometry; the axis roles; the four reader contracts; the `0x103A4` omission; the absence of any 4- or 8-entry object in the function; WO65319's boundary being unaffected.
**Medium:** the function tail past `0x7931E` was not traced, and the four threshold gates are characterised structurally only (which comparison each guards, not what it means). The lookup count itself is HIGH: exactly 16, because the four remaining ROM-byte loads were traced to single-byte `cmp` operands rather than indexed tables.
**Unresolved:** physical identity and units of every object; the purpose of the `UBYTE[FFFF35BD].bit1` selector; the `0x1037C`/`0x103A4` pair's meaning and the 8 unclaimed bytes between them; whether any donor symbol corresponds to any target address.
