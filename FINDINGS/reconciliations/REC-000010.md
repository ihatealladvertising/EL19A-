# REC-000010 — WO65499's fixed-displacement-only “zero writers” replaced by three indexed-store initializer clusters

**Status:** CURRENT — the core indexed-store correction remains valid; see additive corrections REC-000011 and REC-000012 for the later initializer/publisher and copy-helper details.
**Affected addresses/objects:** `FFFF2150` limit band `A1C`–`A54` (`FFFF2B6C`–`FFFF2BA4`), including the `FFFF1640` clamp edges `FFFF2B6E`/`FFFF2B72`/`FFFF2B7C`.

## Old interpretation

`CONTROL/WO65499.md` §4 established that *no fixed-displacement store to any field of `A1C`–`A54` exists anywhere in the ROM* and read that absence as the reason the band must be **writable RAM state with no ROM initializer** — the bounds were treated as “adapted at runtime, never seeded by code”. `CONTROL/WO65733.md` was opened on that reading to test whether a startup ROM→RAM copy seeds the band. Downstream summaries inherited the stronger phrasing “zero field-level writers … unknown initializer”.

## Corrected/current interpretation

The absence was an artefact of the search form. The band **is written**, by three initializer clusters, all using indexed stores whose offset is a 16-bit literal-pool word — a form invisible to `@(h'XXX,rM)` displacement censuses:

- `0x29C10`–`0x29CBC`, base `r1 = FFFF2150` (`off_29DEC`): `A20`,`A22`←`r13`, `A1C`,`A1E`←`r14`, plus `A44`,`A46`,`A54` and fifteen wider fields.
- `0x6D4A0`–`0x6D4B0`, base `r6 = FFFF2150` (`off_6D6BC`, ROM bytes `FF FF 21 50`): the bound publisher — raises `r4` to at least `U16[FFFF2BA2]` and commits `A1E`,`A22`,`A1C`,`A20`.
- `sub_67D0C`, base `r4 = FFFF2150` (`off_67D88`): fills sixteen fields `A2E`–`A5E` with `U16[0x90CC]`, which is `0x7FFF` in the ROM — a saturation/no-limit reset default.

A ROM→RAM block copy is independently excluded: the ROM has **no post-increment store of any width**, and the only block copier (`copy_longwords_by_size`, `0x21F3C`) is called once, at `0x20EF0` with destination `FFFF84D4`, reaching at most `+0x3C` from its base.

Net effect: the band is firmware-initialised RAM with a `0x7FFF` default overridden by computed bounds (`FND-000010`). WO65499's *conclusion* — writable RAM state, not calibration — is unchanged and is now **positively proven** rather than inferred from absence; its *evidence* is superseded.

## Reason and discriminating evidence

- `CONTROL/WO65733.md` §2: zero post-increment stores; single `copy_longwords_by_size` call site and its Duff's-device reach (`0x21F44`–`0x21F94`); no `FFFF2Bxx` literal in the ROM.
- `CONTROL/WO65733.md` §3: the three clusters with exact PCs and the pool-word bases, plus the verified ROM bytes at `0x6D6BC` (`FF FF 21 50`) and `0x90CC` (`7F FF`).
- `CONTROL/WO65499.md` §3–§4: the band addresses, the clamp edges and the superseded census form.

## Confidence

Writer existence, base ownership and the `0x7FFF` default HIGH; writer-set completeness MEDIUM (a writer inheriting `FFFF2150` without a pool load would be missed); reachability of clusters 1–2 from reset OPEN. Physical meaning not promoted.

## Affected inventory/XML references

- None. No calibration-inventory row, catalogue row, provisional XML object or workbook cell covers this band, and this reconciliation creates none. No CSV, XML, workbook, ROM, LST, A2L or script edit was made.

## Standing method rule carried forward

Any “no writers” claim in this project must screen the indexed form `mov.* rN,@(r0,rM)` with 8/16-bit literal-pool offsets alongside fixed-displacement stores. A displacement-only census under-reports writers throughout this ROM and must not be used on its own to declare a RAM cell unwritten. The old WO65499 reading is retained here as history and is not to be re-quoted as current.


## Additive corrections recorded 2026-11-24

The following details in the original corrected/current account above are superseded, without changing this reconciliation's central conclusion that indexed stores initialize the `FFFF2150` band and that it is firmware state rather than calibration:

- **Initializer values and publisher operation — REC-000011 / WO65808:** `sub_67D0C` writes `0x7FFF` to fourteen of its sixteen listed fields; A52 (`FFFF2BA2`) and A58 (`FFFF2BA8`) receive `0x8000`. The publisher's `0x6D496–0x6D49E` operation is a signed minimum, not a maximum. A52 is written by `sub_67D0C`,  not merely read. The bounded source trace is preserved in WO65808 and REC-000011.
- **Copy-helper extent — REC-000012 / WO65739:** the `0x20EF0` call uses the generic helper path for length `0x60`, reaching destination offset `+0x5C`,  not `+0x3C`. Its destination remains `FFFF84D4`, so it still does not write the `FFFF2150` band. The no-post-increment-store observation and the no-copy conclusion for this band are unchanged.

Read REC-000011 and REC-000012 for the current statements; do not reuse the superseded details in the historical body above.
