# FND-000022 — `0x0DE6E` and `0x0E2ED` are reciprocal data/axis vectors

**Status:** CURRENT  
**Semantic layer:** structural relationship (reader argument roles and edit coupling); physical function unresolved  
**Evidence grade:** reader/helper and two caller-site roles recorded in target-side WO64938; no new scanner rerun  
**Confidence:** HIGH for the reciprocal relationship as documented by WO64938; UNRESOLVED for the scanner-reported alternative caller address `0x05AB5A`

## Objects and payloads

| Object | Extent | Type / geometry | Raw payload |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0x0DE6E` | `0x0DE6E–0x0DE7D` | UBYTE, 16×1 | `00 03 06 09 0C 0F 12 15 18 1B 1E 21 24 27 2A 2D` |
| `0x0E2ED` | `0x0E2ED–0x0E2FC` | UBYTE, 16×1 | `00 01 05 0C 15 21 2F 40 53 6A 82 9E BC DC FF FF` |

Both vectors are nondecreasing. The final two `0xFF` values in E2ED are retained payload values; they are not evidence of a shorter table.

## Reader mechanics and reciprocal roles

WO64938 records the shared helper contract for `sub_22F28`:

- R4 = data pointer;
- R5 = UBYTE axis/breakpoint pointer;
- R6 = caller-prepared UBYTE query;
- the helper searches the 16-point axis using `sub_233CC`, reads adjacent UBYTE data cells and interpolates through `sub_231C8`.

The two recorded callers reverse the vectors' roles:

| Caller PC | R4 / data | R5 / axis | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0x05B23A` | `0x0E2ED` | `0x0DE6E` | E2ED data interpolated on DE6E breakpoints |
| `0x05B26A` | `0x0DE6E` | `0x0E2ED` | DE6E data interpolated on E2ED breakpoints |

Neither vector is a permanent ROM-level “data” or “axis” object. Each participates in both roles. Editing either one changes the breakpoint grid used by the other lookup, in addition to changing its own data payload.

This is an edit-coupled reciprocal relationship, but it is not the same storage arrangement as `0x0CD56/0x0FEC0` in REC-000021: DE6E and E2ED are two distinct full vectors, each alternately serving as the other's axis/data, rather than one map's vector also being a standalone curve view.

## Call-site discrepancy and limits

The outside scanner summary supplied to WO70006 names `0x05B26A` and `0x05AB5A`. Existing target-side WO64938 instead documents `0x05B23A` and `0x05B26A`. The committed scanner documentation confirms the reciprocal pairing at a summary level but does not print call PCs or per-call R4/R5/R6 provenance. No raw machine-readable scanner output or pinned target LST was available in this work to establish whether `0x05AB5A` is another call, a transcription error, or a misidentified PC.

Accordingly, the reciprocal pairing and the two caller roles recorded by WO64938 remain the current structural evidence; `0x05AB5A` is explicitly UNVERIFIED and must not replace `0x05B23A` in a future definition without an instruction-level check.

## Release handling recommendation

Do not give either vector a permanent data/axis designation in the ROM catalogue. A future Release 2 raw/provisional definition may include both as separate 16-point UBYTE curves, with each object's displayed X-axis referencing the other object's 16-point payload. Mark the two definitions as mutually linked/edit-coupled, keep raw UBYTE values, and do not assign physical units or names. Neither object was added to Release 1 under this finding.
