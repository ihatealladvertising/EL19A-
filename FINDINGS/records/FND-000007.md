# FND-000007 — 0x0B756/0x0B7D6: paired 8×8 UWORD correction surfaces with unresolved physical query domains

**Status:** CURRENT  
**Semantic layer:** function identity at the generic correction-surface level; physical domain unresolved.

## Addresses and geometry

- ROM `0x0B756`: 8×8 UWORD BE map. Payload varies around `0x8000` (`32776..32868` in WO65137's decoded cell list), i.e. small centred offsets under donor-style `raw−32768` encoding.
- ROM `0x0B7D6`: 8×8 UWORD BE map. All 64 cells are `32768` (`0x8000`), a constant/neutral surface in this ROM.
- Shared X axis `0x0FCFC`: 8×1 UWORD BE, raw values `96, 128, 160, 192, 224, 256, 288, 320`.
- Shared Y axis `0x0FD3C`: 8×1 UWORD BE, raw values `6144, 8192, 10240, 12288, 14336, 18432, 22528, 26624`.
- Reader `sub_22CF8`; actual helper calls `0x39FE2` for `0x0B756` and `0x39FF4` for `0x0B7D6`. `0x39FD2` loads the first map base and `0x39FEE` loads the second; neither is a helper call.

## Observation

`loc_39FB8` establishes `GBR=FFFF86D4` at `0x39FC6/0x39FC8`. Both calls use the same two raw UWORD queries, `UWORD[FFFF85B2]` and `UWORD[FFFF8494]`, with axis pointers `0x0FCFC` and `0x0FD3C`. No caller-side scaling or unit conversion is applied; `sub_22CF8` zero-extends the queries and performs 8×8 UWORD interpolation. Results are staged to `UWORD[FFFF86E2]` at `0x39FE6` and `UWORD[FFFF86E0]` at `0x39FFA`.

WO65180 resolves the first meaningful consumers. The `0x0B756` result is read at `0x3A0E0` and added into an accumulator whose bounded UWORD result is stored to `FFFF3456` at `0x3A128`. The `0x0B7D6` result is read at `0x3A134`, added into a parallel accumulator, and stored to `FFFF3458` at `0x3A164`. The calls execute sequentially with the same queries and axes; they are not mutually exclusive selector alternatives. Because all `0x0B7D6` cells are `0x8000`, its returned value is a constant neutral-biased term in the observed arithmetic; it is read and used, not proven disabled or a trustful/secondary bank.

Donor A2L declarations describe `mMABFSFT` at V6 `0xB172` and `mMABFSFT2` at `0xB1F2`, both 8×8 UWORD with `COMP_0483_S = (raw−32768)×0.002`, axes `mMXABFSFT` (`12.5 rpm/count`) and `mMYABFSFT` (`1/2048 ms/count`), function `KF7-313C.1[36]` “Interface”. Applying those donor conversions to the target axis counts gives coherent rpm-like and ms-like ranges, but donor payload/axis bytes are unavailable and the target queries are not proven to be engine speed or time/pulse width.

## Conclusion

Retain `0x0B756/0x0B7D6` as two distinct, concurrently evaluated 8×8 UWORD correction surfaces sharing exact geometry, axes and raw query words. Their software-role query/first-consumer mechanics are resolved as parallel accumulator paths; their **physical query semantics and output units remain unresolved**. Donor `mMABFSFT/mMABFSFT2` names, N×TP meaning, rpm/ms units and bank-pair interpretation remain comparison-only hypotheses. The constant-neutral `0x0B7D6` payload is a central caveat against assuming duplicated donor bank semantics.
