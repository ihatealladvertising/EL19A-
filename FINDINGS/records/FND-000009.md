## Observation

WO64913 reconciled the listing's reader/Y/X argument convention against XML's conventional X/Y order. For A272, the caller supplies `R5=E006` and `R6=E002`, so the Y axis is `E006` and the X axis is `E002`; this corrects the workbook 3D crosswalk entry that previously said “Not established”. WO64966 independently found one direct `sub_22BC0` caller and no additional map placement or axis address. The helper contract makes the Y axis use ×4 comparison and the X axis raw.

`A0E2` is a different 4×4 UBYTE object read by `sub_22B44` at `0x43F02` with the same `E006/E002` axis pair. Shared axes do not make the two maps duplicates or imply identical payloads. Prior bounded investigations rejected the A0E2 `0x43` cell as the screenshot's 52.34% scalar and left its semantic role unresolved; that history is not reopened here.

## Conclusion

Adopt `E006` as A272's Y axis and `E002` as its X axis, agreeing with the provisional XML nested axes. The workbook crosswalk's “Not established” entry is superseded (`REC-000009`). Keep A272 and A0E2 as separate objects/readers even though they share axis addresses. No physical accelerator-pedal, throttle-target or other semantic label is promoted from XML names or geometry.
