# FND-000025 — `sub_77608` performs sixteen indexed lookups over twenty ROM-byte references, and its reported "six-call / five-address" set is six objects with `0x103A4` omitted

**Status:** CURRENT
**Semantic layer:** function identity (structure only). No input, function or output identity is established for any object.
**Evidence grade:** Reader-Verified (complete `r4` table-pointer census of one bounded function, four helper contracts read from code, byte-exact ROM extents)
**Confidence:** HIGH for the census, extents, geometry, axes, queries and destinations; LOW for donor symbol identity; UNRESOLVED for physical meaning, units and the `0x0B012`/`0x0B112` selector's purpose

## Scope

Bounded reconstruction of the EL19A lookup constellation in `sub_77608`, the function whose prologue offset `0x77616` was reported as a "one-to-one V6 function match at `0x8D4DA`", and reconciliation of its reported six calls against the reported five unique data addresses. Reached under WO70011. Does not reopen WO65319's endpoint boundary and does not characterise any object physically.

## Observations

1. **`0x77616` is a prologue instruction, not a function entry or a call PC.** `sub_77608` begins at `0x77608` with `mov.l r8,@-r15`, followed by six more register saves at `0x7760A`–`0x77614`; `0x77616` is the `sts.l pr,@-r15` that follows them. `gbr` is installed from `off_77894` = `0xFFFF34C1` at `0x7761C`–`0x7761E` before `add #h'FFFFFFB0,r15` takes the `-0x50` frame, so every `@(h'NN,gbr)` in the body resolves against `0xFFFF34C1`.

   **Reusable rule:** a matcher-supplied "target function/caller address" must be re-checked for a `sub_`/`loc_` label in the listing before it is treated as a function boundary or a call PC; register-save prologues produce splittings that land inside a function.

2. **The reported six-call cluster, resolved.** Three 16×16 UBYTE maps plus two 16×1 UWORD curves plus one 16×1 UBYTE curve:

   | # | PC | Helper | `r4` data | Query | Destination |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | `0x77CAA` | `sub_22DA8` | `0x0B212` | rows `UBYTE[FFFF85A4]`, cols `UWORD[FFFF8494]>>8` | `UBYTE[FFFF2870]` (`FFFF2150+0x720`) |
   | 2 | `0x77CC8` | `sub_22DA8` | `0x0B012` | same | `UBYTE[FFFF2871]` (`FFFF2150+0x721`) |
   | 3 | `0x77CE2` | `sub_22DA8` | `0x0B112` | same | `UBYTE[FFFF2871]` |
   | 4 | `0x7804C` | `sub_22E44` | `0x1037C` | `extu.w` of `((UBYTE[FFFF84B8]-0x20)<<9)` saturated `0xFFFF` | `FFFF2150+0x244` |
   | 5 | `0x78058` | `sub_22E44` | `0x103A4` | same value as #4 | `FFFF2150+0x246` |
   | 6 | `0x78238` | `sub_22DF8` | `0x0E64C` | `UBYTE[FFFF84B9]` | `r13` (`0x7823C`) |

3. **`0x0B012` and `0x0B112` are mutually exclusive and share a destination.** `0x77CB4` `mov.b @(h'FC,gbr),r0` reads `UBYTE[FFFF35BD]`; `0x77CB6` `tst #2,r0`; `0x77CB8` `bf loc_77CD4`. Bit 1 set runs `0x0B012` (`0x77CC0`), clear runs `0x0B112` (`0x77CDA`). Both write `FFFF2871`, while `0x0B212` unconditionally writes `FFFF2870`.

4. **The reported five-address set is one short; the sixth call is a distinct object.** Pool words `off_78270` = `dword_102E4+0x98` = `0x1037C` and `off_78278` = `dword_102E4+0xC0` = `0x103A4` (ROM bytes `00 01 03 7C` / `00 01 03 A4` verified). The two 32-byte curves are `0x1037C`–`0x1039B` = `0014×4, FB06 F522 EB6B DE07 CD2C B91E A22C 88B2 6D16 3138 11E5 11E5` and `0x103A4`–`0x103C3` = `003C×4, 003A×8, 0164×3, 011A`. They are 40 bytes apart; the 8 bytes `0x1039C`–`0x103A3` (`11E5 11E5 11E5 0000`) are claimed by neither read. There is no duplicate-address reuse: **six calls, six objects.**

5. **Verified extents and geometry.**

   | Object | Extent | Storage | Dims | Unique values |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | `0x0B212` | `0x0B212`–`0x0B311` (256 B) | UBYTE | 16×16 | 27 |
   | `0x0B012` | `0x0B012`–`0x0B111` (256 B) | UBYTE | 16×16 | 69 |
   | `0x0B112` | `0x0B112`–`0x0B211` (256 B) | UBYTE | 16×16 | 47 |
   | `0x1037C` | 32 B | UWORD BE | 16×1 | 10 |
   | `0x103A4` | 32 B | UWORD BE | 16×1 | 3 |
   | `0x0E64C` | 16 B | UBYTE | 16×1 | 1 (all `0x00`) |

   The three maps are contiguous (`0x0B012`+`0x100` = `0x0B112`, +`0x100` = `0x0B212`, +`0x100` = `0x0B312` which begins 16 zero bytes). They share their first three rows and diverge from row 3; `0x0B012` alone carries a saturating `0x80` ramp in its upper-left corner.

6. **Axes and their roles.** `0x0E0BE` (`08 10 14 18 20 28 30 38 40 48 50 58 60 68 70 78`, 8…120) is the **rows** axis (`r5`); `0x0E0E2` (`10 14 18 1C 20 28 30 38 40 48 50 58 60 68 70 78`, 16…120) is the **columns** axis (`r6`). Both are 16-entry UBYTE, strictly ascending. Role assignment follows `sub_22DA8`'s first-search/`r5` → row convention and its column count `0x10` at `0x22DD6` with row stride `0x10` at `0x23318`. The two axes are not adjacent (`0x0E0BE` ends at `0x0E0CD`; `0x0E0E2` starts 0x12 bytes later).

7. **The function performs sixteen lookups, not six.** Twenty ROM-byte references resolve to 20 distinct addresses; 16 are indexed lookups through an identified reader: `0x0B212`, `0x0B012`, `0x0B112` (`sub_22DA8`); `0x0ECEE`, `0x0ECFE`, `0x0ED0E`, `0x0ED1E`, `0x0E598`, `0x0E5A8`, `0x0ECCE`, `0x0ECDE`, `0x0F2F2` (`sub_22E10`); `0x0F3FE`, `0x0E64C` (`sub_22DF8`); `0x1037C`, `0x103A4` (`sub_22E44`). The four remaining `r4`/`r6` ROM-byte loads are **not lookups**: each reads only element `[0]` (`mov.b @r4,r2`) and feeds a comparison, with no index arithmetic and no reader call — `UBYTE[0x08ED8]` = `0x00` at `0x78330` (`cmp/hs` on `UBYTE[FFFF84B7]`, always true), `UBYTE[0x08CE2]` = `0x00` at `0x7833E` (`cmp/hs` on `UBYTE[FFFF9860+0x70]`, always true), `UBYTE[0x08E37]` = `0x00` at `0x784A6` via `r6` (`cmp/hi`), and `UBYTE[0x08F9E]` = `0xFF` at `0x7849C`/`0x784B4` (`cmp/hs` on `UBYTE[FFFF84D4+0x5D]`, never true — gate closed). The lookup census is therefore **exactly 16 with no unresolved member**, and the function's ROM-byte references total 20: 16 indexed lookups plus 4 scalar threshold bytes in the `0x08xxx` calibration region.

8. **Reader contracts (all verified from code).**
   - `sub_22DA8`: 16×16 UBYTE, row stride `0x10`, axes via `sub_233CC` (16-entry UBYTE).
   - `sub_22DF8`: `word_22E0A` = `0xE0`; `q >= 0xE0` returns `UBYTE[table+7]` (`locret_22E06` delay slot `mov.b @(7,r4),r0`); else `loc_23508` UBYTE interpolation.
   - `sub_22E10`: `word_22E22` = `0xF0`; `q >= 0xF0` returns `UBYTE[table+15]`; else `loc_2350C`.
   - `sub_22E44`: `extu.w` query; `off_22E5C` = `0xC19C`; `q >= 0xC19C` returns `UWORD[table+3]` (`mov.w @(6,r4),r0`); else `loc_234E4` UWORD interpolation (`loc_2351C` `swap.w r3,r3`, then the `mov.w @r4` / `@(2,r4)` branch).
   - Consequence: **every object in this function is 16-entry or 16×16.** No 4-entry and no 8-entry table exists in the constellation.

9. **Donor point counts falsify the six-for-six pairing.** From `EVIDENCE/side project/64811000p2_m.a2l` (all six under `/begin FUNCTION_LIST KF7-500.26[17]`): `mTMSATH`/`mTMSATA`/`mTMSATB` are 16×16 `REC_UBYTE_C` maps with two 16-point axes; `mTMFPC1` is a 16-point `REC_UBYTE_C` curve; **`mTCREM` is a 4-point `REC_UWORD_C` curve** (`FIX_AXIS 4, −20…40`) and **`mTDLMFD` is an 8-point `REC_UBYTE_C` curve** (`FIX_AXIS 8, −40…100`). There is no 4-entry and no 8-entry object anywhere in the target constellation, so those two symbols cannot be assigned to any object here. The only target UWORD curves (`0x1037C`, `0x103A4`) are 16-point, contradicting `mTCREM`'s declared 4 points.

## Post-publication refinement (same session)

The three loads first recorded as "not resolved to a consuming reader" were traced immediately after the initial publication (commit `cf4aecc`). They are scalar threshold bytes, not tables, and the count is exact rather than a lower bound. The refinement strengthens observation 7 and removes its only caveat; no other claim in this record changes.

## Conclusion

`sub_77608` is a large multi-lookup routine that reads **sixteen identified ROM tables**, of which the reported "six-call" cluster is a small sub-set: three contiguous 16×16 UBYTE maps (`0x0B012`/`0x0B112`/`0x0B212`, 256 B each, sharing row/column axes `0x0E0BE`/`0x0E0E2`), two 16-point UWORD curves (`0x1037C`, `0x103A4`), and one constant-neutral 16-point UBYTE curve (`0x0E64C`).

The reported "six calls to five unique addresses" mismatch is a **reporting omission, not address reuse**: the sixth object is `0x103A4`, a distinct 32-byte curve at `dword_102E4+0xC0`, and its omission is what made `0x1037C` appear to serve two donor symbols.

The reported donor pairing is **structurally weakened on target-side evidence**: three of six symbols match the map class exactly and one matches the curve class, but `mTCREM` (4 points, UWORD) and `mTDLMFD` (8 points, UBYTE) have no geometrically compatible object in this function. No symbol is therefore assigned, and no donor name, unit or conversion is promoted.

**Identity layer advanced:** function identity, structurally only — the routine's table-read inventory and per-call argument contracts. Input identity, output identity and physical meaning remain unresolved.
