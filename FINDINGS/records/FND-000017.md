## Limits and no-repeat boundary

These findings establish representation-critical geometry and helper/axis relationships only. They do not establish physical units or real-world query domains. In Release 1, use raw values and generic labels, and label fixed/index axes as raw query grids or sample indices. Do not restore RPM, percent, temperature, airflow or other physical labels from donor similarity alone.

Do not repeat the listed caller/helper traces without contradictory evidence. The successor XML implements these structural corrections while preserving the selected baseline file unchanged.
