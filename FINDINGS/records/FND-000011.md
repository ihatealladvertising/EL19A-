## Recorded observations

1. The forum describes ignition trimming values as adjustment/delta quantities rather than absolute final ignition timing or burn-time values. Its narrative places trimming/compensation within a wider calculation involving MBT, temperature, catalyst/jar, VTC/cam timing and subsequent knock/torque/acceleration corrections.
2. The user-identified raw-view screenshots show ignition/detonation table cells in byte-like ranges, including values above 128. The user-identified degree-view screenshots show tables labelled as timing adjustments in degrees alongside separate Knock Window and Knock Slice objects. The screenshots therefore illustrate different definition/presentation layers and related control objects; they do not, by themselves, prove one universal conversion formula or prove that every high-valued cell is a flag.
3. The CF48D screenshot description identifies a degree-scaled High Octane Ignition Timing Map alongside a separate 0/1 Knock Window map. The CD700/ZVNew screenshot descriptions identify raw and degree-oriented views and related cold/high-detonation/knock-control objects.
4. The 1CF43D screenshot description places raw detonation maps alongside intake/exhaust cam timing and other fuel/load tables. This is consistent with a broader compensation environment but does not prove the target's VTC compensation arithmetic or timing order.
5. The remaining contextual screenshots described by the user concern fuel compensation, cam timing/VANOS and fuel cut/restore, not direct ignition-map encoding.

## Donor-side resolution already established

WO65730 is the controlling bounded A2L cross-reference:

- 17/19 forum symbols were found in the Juke MR16DDT A2L and 18/19 in `64811000p2`; matched symbols are RAM MEASUREMENTs, not calibration table addresses.
- `COMP_0245` is the donor `X-64deg` display conversion for UBYTE trimming-map measurements `vLADVTRM`, `vADVTRMAP`, and `vADVTRM`.
- Donor `vTADVMnm` uses SBYTE `COMP_0018` (`1deg`), not the -64 conversion. Thus the forum's claim that -64 is finally applied to produce `vTADVMnm` is contradicted by the examined donor A2L.
- The donor's UBYTE trimming-map representation is consistent with a +128 storage convention described by the forum, but the donor firmware operation that removes/interprets that flag was not traced in WO65730.
- Donor `vVTCADV` exists as a RAM measurement in both A2Ls, but this does not establish an equivalent EL19A compensation path.
