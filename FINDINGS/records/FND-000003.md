# FND-000003 — 9B2E/9C2E/9DAE: three UBYTE placements, fixed axes and runtime alias disposition

**Status:** CURRENT  
**Semantic layer:** input lookup/output staging; physical identity unresolved.

## Addresses and geometry

- `0x09B2E`, `0x09C2E`, `0x09DAE`: three distinct 16×16 UBYTE ROM placements, each 256 bytes.
- Fixed axes: X `0x0E4AC` (16 UBYTE, raw comparison); Y `0x0E4BC` (16 UBYTE, comparison ×4).
- Y axis bytes: `08 0C 10 18 20 28 34 38 40 48 50 60 68 70 78 80`.
- X axis bytes: `08 10 18 20 28 30 38 40 48 50 58 60 68 70 78 80`.
- Reader: `sub_22CB8`.

## Observation

`loc_472DC` sets `GBR=0xE4AC` at `0x472EE–0x472F0` and restores the saved GBR at `0x4762E`, with no intervening GBR write. At the `0x9B2E` call `0x473C0`, `R5=GBR+0x10=0xE4BC` and `R6=GBR=0xE4AC`; the axes are fixed ROM objects, not runtime-generated values.

`0x9C2E` and `0x9DAE` have byte-identical payloads (reported SHA-256 prefix `f3fe03f0…`). In the paired fixed-axis path, `0x42BB2` reads `0x9C2E` and `0x42BC8` reads `0x9DAE`, both with the same axes, UBYTE query `FFFF849A` and UWORD query `FFFF85B2`; inputs are reloaded for the second call. Each result is masked with `0x7F`, so the signed difference is zero under the identical-payload condition. The resulting `FFFF1C79` byte is the signed high-byte conversion of base word `FFFF10F8`, not a differential correction. `FFFF1D7F.bit7` gates entry to this path; `FFFF1D7D.bit7` selects `0x9DAE` versus `0x9C2E` in the dynamic selector path.

The XML fragment overlays each placement with `Ign_Degrees` and `Knock_Window_Flag` views of the same payload. Bit 7 is saved by some `0x9B2E` callers but explicitly masked off at the `0x9C2E/0x9DAE` paired callsite, so the two views are not code-equivalent. No target evidence establishes ignition timing, knock-window or physical mode identity.

## Conclusion

Preserve three distinct ROM placements and one fixed axis pair. Treat `0x9C2E` and `0x9DAE` as payload aliases whose observed paired differential cancels; keep `0x9B2E` separate. Keep ignition/knock labels, high-octane/high-detonation labels and knock-window overlays as XML display hypotheses only, not promoted target semantics. Do not transfer A2L donor names, units or conversions.
