## Observation (reproduced from pinned inputs)

1. **Target census.** The workbench reproduces **183 non-trivial caller-backed tables** (291 table objects total, 377 calls, 34 helpers) in `VERSA f501.bin`; this matches the reported 183 exactly.
2. **Conservation.** 175/183 (95.6%) have an exact payload match in at least one of the 24 scanned donor inputs. Excluding the two C11 donors EL13A and 8W83B, **146/183 (79.8%)** are conserved in at least one non-C11 donor — exactly the reported 146/183 (80%).
3. **Missing-XML coverage.** Exactly **15** non-trivial caller-backed tables are absent from `CALIBRATIONS/EL19A_release1_conservative.xml`: `0x0DAB6`, `0x0DE6E`, `0x0E2ED`, `0x0E434`, `0x0E590`, `0x0E8B0`, `0x0EB78`, `0x0F536`, `0x10266`, `0x102FC`, `0x1064C`, `0x10CC2`, `0x12A4E`, `0x12D18`, `0x10FFA`. **15/15 have an exact-payload donor match**, and **12/15** have a match outside EL13A/8W83B. The three whose matches are C11-only are not excluded from conservation, only from the "outside C11" count.
4. **Reciprocal pair.** `0x0DE6E` and `0x0E2ED` (each 16×1 UBYTE, 16 B) are payload-conserved across all seven named families: EL13A, 8W83B, EE310, EL58B/8W94B/8W95B/EL59B, E1BR20E/E1BR50E/E1BT40A, Qashqai 2 MiB, Juke06/Juke08/Tiida. 5. **Variant-sensitive trio.** `0x10CC2`, `0x12A4E`, `0x12D18` all carry the **same** 32-byte payload, SHA-256 `98ee09bf98ce887b3639ae1a308baa28bcf5548cb73357408bfd5d537cab66db` (`00 00 00 2A 00 5A 00 94 00 D4 01 18 01 50 01 88 01 C0 01 F8 02 30 02 68 02 A0 03 48 03 48 03 48`). The reported donor coordinates `0x0CE98` / `0x16454` are therefore **raw payload offsets**, while the scanner-resolved table starts are `0x0BE6A` and `0x13DDA`. The 8W94B-vs-EL58B difference at `0x0CE98` confirms the reported 25-table revision separation at this address.

## Conclusion

EL19A's caller-backed table payloads are broadly conserved across generations and processors, and every non-trivial table omitted from Release 1 XML has an exact donor payload counterpart. This is structural evidence that the omitted objects are real, well-formed table payloads rather than scan artifacts or invalid geometry, and it supports their treatment as raw/provisional inclusion candidates with verified geometry and extent. It does **not** establish donor names, units, conversions, axis meaning, function or physical identity on EL19A.
